## Findings

### Minor

**m1. A held delayed bark is re-checked only for its condition, so it can show after its NPC is hidden or knocked out.** npc-manager.js:1229-1236 and bark-release-policy.js:27-32, 69. The knocked-out, room and hidden checks run once, when the timer fires. If a chat or minigame is open then, the bark is held. On release only `stillValid` (the mapping's `condition`) is checked again. Live, game 2618: Helen's `ncsc_notified` bark came due at 502 ms inside her own chat and was held. The probe then hid Helen with `npcBehaviorManager.setNPCVisible('helen_carver', false)`, and when the chat closed the bark rendered at 4947 ms with `isVisible === false`. The same applies to a knock-out or a room change while a bark is held, and to any bark held behind another NPC's chat or a minigame. A bark with no delay already behaves this way, but the README `barkDelay` entry (README_scenario_design.md:1616) promises the drop. No sis01, sis02 or lab ink hides or moves an NPC at the end of its chat, so no deployed line is affected today. Fix: in `_delayBark`, fold the three checks into the `stillValid` handed to `showBark`. For example, build `alive = () => !isNPCKO(npcId) && where().room === before.room && !(where().hidden && !before.hidden)` and pass `() => (stillValid ? stillValid() : true) && alive()` through `show`, so `fireBark` and the knot path put it on the payload. Add a test that hides the NPC while the bark is held and asserts `payload.stillValid()` is false. Or, if that is not wanted, add one sentence to the README saying the checks run when the delay ends, not when a held bark is released.

**m2. sis01 Ms Okafor's death: David's line still runs into Helen's.** scenario.json.erb:2232 (David, 4100) and 2315 (Helen, 8800). At David's measured 0.487 s a word, his 12-word line takes about 5.8 s and ends near 9.9 s, so Helen starts about 1.1 s before he finishes. Sarah (1500, about 2.3 s, ends near 3.8 s) is clear of David. The bed 4 death is clear: Amy's cached line is 1.61 s (800 to 2408), Sarah starts at 2500, her cached line is 3.41 s (ends 5908), and Helen starts at 6500. Fix, if the order stays: Helen 10200, Mrs Kowalski 14600 (a 300 ms gap after each measured end). m3 proposes a different fix.

**m3. Mrs Kowalski at 13.5 s reads as a late afterthought, and the authors' order cannot be kept without pushing her later.** scenario.json.erb:1322. She is the witness in the next bed. Her line, "She's gone. She stopped breathing.", reports the moment of death, but it comes 12 s after Sarah's crash call and after two people elsewhere have already reacted to the death ("Ms Okafor's dead?" at 4.1 s). In the old chorus her place in the order did not show. Now that the lines are spaced, it does. Kept in order, the three lines before hers take about 11 s of speech, so without overlap she cannot start before about 14.6 s (m2). Proposal (barkDelay numbers only, but it changes the order, so it needs the ruling revisited): put her second, straight after the crash call. Sarah 1500 (ends about 3.8 s), Mrs Kowalski 4100 (ends about 6.4 s), David 6800 (ends about 12.6 s), Helen 12900. The off-ward reactions then follow the bedside ones. An alternative with her first: Mrs Kowalski 1000, Sarah 3600, David 6200, Helen 12300. If the order ruling stands, use the m2 numbers and accept her at 14.6 s.

### Nits

**n1. The ERB comment by Mrs Kowalski's mapping is stale.** scenario.json.erb:1323 still says "Witness bark delayed 3 s to stagger after the critical bark". The delay is 13500 ms, and it now staggers her after the other death reactions, not after the critical bark (that is a different event 90 s earlier). Comments must match the code. Fix: "Witness bark: last of the four reactions to Ms Okafor's death (Sarah 1.5 s, David 4.1 s, Helen 8.8 s), spaced so the voices do not overlap." Change it to match whatever m2 or m3 settles. An ERB comment is stripped at render, so editing it does not change the deployed scenario.

**n2. The test bridge's key-press `choose` reports success when the key never reached the chat. In this setup that is expected game behaviour, and the bridge is what misleads.** Reproduced in games 2621 and 2627. In the forced debrief the first `choose(1)` ("I'm ready.") worked by key. It completes `attend_debrief`, the last task of the `missionConclusion` aim, and the ending warning opens over the chat (`ending-check-overlay`, focus on "Keep going"). Its capture-phase `keydown` on window (ending-check.js:373-382) stops every key, so each later `choose(n)` returned `{ ok: true, key: "1" }`, the console never logged "Number key", and the choices stayed put. A probe listener on document never saw the key either. Clicking the choice button did move the dialogue, because `element.click()` reaches a button under the overlay, which a player could not do. The warning appears only because `swap-scenario-game.rb` runs sis01 inside a `test_seating` game, and `test_seating/mission.json` leaves `endingCheck` on (mission.rb:130-132, show.html.erb:246). sis01's own mission.json:5 sets `"endingCheck": false`, so deployed sis01 never shows it. The bridge gaps: `pressKey` (test-bridge/minigames.js:295-303) and `_pick` (625-627) return ok without checking that anything changed, and `getState` hides `blockingUi` while a minigame is open (test-bridge/state.js:767), so a driver cannot see the overlay. For the backlog: have `choose` check that the choice list or line changed and fail with `blocking-ui` when an overlay that captures keys is up. Report body-level overlays in `blockingUi` even during a minigame. Have `swap-scenario-game.rb` carry the swapped scenario's mission.json flags (`endingCheck`, `timed`), or say in its header that it does not. Not fixed here. R1's ICO proof (game 2611) clicked choice buttons under this overlay, so it drove the debrief in a way a player could not. Its m2 result still stands, because the drop depends only on `debrief_complete` and the chat closing.

**n3. sis01's four bed 2 death barks have no cached audio.** `tts_cache/sis01_healthcare` has no entry for "Crash call, Bed 2! Amy, now!", "Crash call, Bed 2! I'm going!", "Ms Okafor's dead? That's exactly what the library was meant to stop.", "Ms Okafor? Through the pump? God. I'll have to tell the Board." or "She's gone. She stopped breathing.". The three bed 4 death lines are cached. On a server with a key they are generated on first play, which adds a variable lag before each voice and can bring back overlaps the spacing removed. Fix (tts-audio, for the mission owner): top up sis01, then re-time m2 or m3 against the real durations.

**n4. m02's staggered reactions still overlap, though less than before.** m02 scenario.json.erb:1646, 1713, 1753, 1841: on `patient_bed4_state` 'distressed', Mr Pryce (0 ms, 3 words), Raval (200, 6), Doyle (500, 10) and Ms Chen (800, 13) all start within 0.8 s. On `ward_recovering` (1653, 1804, 1862), Ms Chen (2000), Mrs Hargreaves (2500) and Doyle (3200) overlap if no minigame is open; when one is, the hold releases them one at a time. On main all of these spoke at 0 ms, so this is the authored behaviour, now honoured, and not a regression. Fix (optional, m02 owner): space them by measured line length as in sis01.
