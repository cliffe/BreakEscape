# Seat box plan: adversarial review, round 1

Reviewed: `docs/achitechture/SEAT_BOX_PLAN.md` at 319ef3fc5, against `SEAT_BOX_EXPERIMENTS.md`
(9df5647b9), the scratch outputs in the session's `scratchpad/seat-box/` (`sweep.json`,
`verify.json`, `exp.js`, `swap_game.rb`, `part2_lab_desks.patch`) and the code at HEAD. Read-only
review: no code, plan or DB change. Fresh reviewer, 2026-11-06.

The direction holds up: desks with a footprint box, desks kept as table groups, and a landing
rule that keeps the player off and away from the far side of a desk. The problems are in the
evidence for the landing rule, a process rule, the size of the block for the office chairs,
and where the opt-in lives.

## Answers to the brief's questions

1. **Process rule:** B2. Use the game-row swap (no new Mission row), or a single hand-registered row with the user's agreement, as for missions 80–82 and 234.
2. **Blast radius:** a room-level opt-in is the right kind of gate, but it should sit on the map, not the scenario (M3). Footprint boxes should stay opt-in for good, set by generators for new rooms and turned on per map when a mission is redressed. The global flip costs a regression campaign over shipped missions for little visible gain. The global part should be the landing rule (after B1 and M5). Under M1, the office chairs' seated body doesn't change at all.
3. **Landing rule:** it fails today from behind `desk1` in the chosen config (B1), and the fallback order is unsafe or contradictory (M5). Cramped cases (a chair rolled into a gap or corner, or a seat between two desks) need the flood-fill step in M5. Static seats are unaffected (`landingSpot` returns `approach`). NPC stand-up shares `standSpot` (M6).
4. **Q2:** M4. Moot under M1. Otherwise default it to "no".
5. **NPC half:** the fetch path is missing (M6). Slice 1 changes NPC stand-up through `standSpot` (M6). Q2 would change NPC-used chairs before slice 6 tests them (M4). Slices 2–4 don't depend on slice 6; slices 7 and 8 do, as the plan says.
6. **`foot`:** sound as a separate field. Use 27 for `smalldesk1` (m1).
7. **Slice 7:** rebuilding with the fixed tool is right. The plan's claim that the patch applies is already wrong, the step 9 desk items need a drawing decision, and the precondition should be step 9's logged proof, not just its commit (M7).
8. **Simpler:** M1. For the office chairs, G comes down to "footprint desk boxes plus the landing rule". That drops `keep`, Q2, the sunk-chair problem, the gap squeeze and slice 6 item 1, and may settle Q7.

## Verdict

**Revise.** The design is sound and most of the plan can stay. Before building, fix the two blockers (re-prove the landing rule from behind, no seeding), put the M1 choice to the user, and move the opt-in to the map.
