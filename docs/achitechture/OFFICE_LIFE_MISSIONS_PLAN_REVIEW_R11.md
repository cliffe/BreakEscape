# Office life missions plan: review round 11

Reviewer: fresh read-only reviewer, round 11. Plan reviewed: `docs/achitechture/OFFICE_LIFE_MISSIONS_PLAN.md` (draft 11, commit 2e0bca43f). Claims were checked against the engine (`npc-needs.js`, `npc-manager.js`, `seating.js`, `office-presets.js`, `office-presets.json`, `room-tones.json`, `lighting.js`, `core/rooms.js`, `consumables.js`, `npc-pathfinding.js`), m02's rendered scenario, its ink, the Tiled maps `room_hospital_staff.json` and `room_hospital_office_it.json`, `TESTING_WALKTHROUGH.md`, the schema, and the showcase build plan on `build/ol-sims`.

## Verdict

Not ready for stage 4. Nothing in the pilot breaks canon, a lock, a key or a story global, and the needs numbers, coupling mappings, exchange gates, print command and sync sums mostly behave as the engine does. Two wiring and design defects remain in section 6. The break on entry starts about four seconds after the player walks in, so a player crossing the hub on the walkthrough route hears nothing. The night domestic's chair is not a seat the engine knows, so she never sits. These two are the pilot's main show, so they matter for whether it convinces anyone. The rest are smaller accuracy and consistency points.

## Blockers

None.

## Majors

**M1. The entry break lands after a crossing player has left, and often while both speakers are on chatter cooldown (6.0 table, 6.4 "A break when the player walks in", 6.4 exit test).**

Evidence:
- The porter's job starts on the next needs tick (`NEEDS_TICK_MS = 250`, `npc-needs.js:22`), then a 700 ms wind-up before leaving a post (`npc-needs.js:2890`, `windupMs` default 700 at `:4780`), then a walk at 80 px/s by default (`speedFor`, `npc-needs.js:5082-5087`) from the east table's north chair (6.72, 5.78) to the boiler's cell in front of the sink counter (about 4.75, 3.9): about 90 px, 1.1 s. `npc_left_post` only then wakes the domestic (`_leavePost`, `:2961-2971`), who repeats the tick, wind-up and a walk of about 4 tiles (1.6 s). `_maybePair` needs both in `using` (`:3863-3880`). The first line is therefore about 4 s after entry.
- The plan's own figure: a crossing is "about two seconds of walking at 150 px/s" (6.4). The mapping does not change that. A player going from the corridor door to the CTO door, or from the IT door back to the corridor, is gone before the pair reach the boiler.
- An exchange starts only with the player in earshot, 160 px, same room, lit and in clear line (`_audience`, `:2705`; `OVERHEAR_RADIUS = 160`, `:47`). The south door is about 6 tiles (190 px) from the boiler cell, so a player who stops just inside the door is out of earshot as well.
- The mapping's cooldown runs on wall-clock `Date.now()` (`npc-manager.js:1207-1223`). `chatterCooldownMin` runs on the needs clock and starts when each speaker last spoke (`_pickBark`, `npc-needs.js:2641-2647`; `_cooldownMs`, `:2627`). The needs clock also stops while the VM is active (`update`, `:612`). Natural shared breaks come about every four minutes. When the player walks in within two minutes of one, the entry mapping still fires (its cooldown counts from the last entry, not the last break), but both speakers are still on cooldown and show picture chat. The exit test ("more than two minutes after the last shared break") picks out the one case that works and does not measure this.
- On the walkthrough, the IT door is opened with Bernie's key, which is instant (`TESTING_WALKTHROUGH.md` step 12; step 5 gives the key). The only stops in the staff room are optional: the board, the laptop and the lanyard (step 10).

What goes wrong: most visits by a walkthrough player see two NPCs stand up after they have left, or hear nothing. The pilot's voiced talk, its main proof, is heard only by a player who stops in the room for 5 s or more within 160 px of the boiler.

Fix: start the break one room earlier, so that it is running when the player crosses. Put the `needs: { set: { caffeine: 20 } }` mapping on the events that come just before a crossing: `room_entered:office_corridor`, `room_entered:dr_kim_office` and `room_entered:it_department`, each with the 120 s cooldown. The 30 s boiler use then covers a player who comes through within about 25 s. The exchange can still start mid-break, because `_tickChats` checks for an exchange again on every turn until one plays (`:3899-3903`). Keep the `room_entered:staff_room` mapping as a fallback. Rewrite the exit test for a moving player: from a standing start in the corridor, walk the corridor-to-CTO route at normal speed, and require at least one overheard pool or exchange line on three of five crossings, measured over a full needs cycle, whatever the time since the last natural break. Then choose `chatterCooldownMin` and the natural break spacing so that an entry break is rarely silent. For example, space the natural breaks out to about eight minutes and keep the cooldown at 2, or drop the cooldown to 1 and give each topic 6 lines.

**M2. The night domestic's post chair cannot be sat on, so "both sit" is false (6.0 table "Posts, leash, seating"; 6.3 people table; 6.4 "Posts"; 6.3 "Cast" seated frames).**

Evidence:
- The domestic's post is the east table's east chair, `hospital_chair1.png` at (7.78, 6.5) (`room_hospital_staff.json`, items layer). `seatSpec` returns a seat only for textures in `SWIVEL_SEATS` or `STATIC_SEATS` (`seating.js:246-257`). `STATIC_SEATS` lists `hospital_chair_south`, `hospital_chair_north` and `hospital_chair_north2`, but not `hospital_chair1` or `hospital_chair2` (`seating.js:206-229`), and `STATIC_SWIVEL_PROPS` does not list them either (`core/rooms.js:887-899`).
- At a post, the seat preference is `fetch` (`seatPreference`, `seating.js:1793-1803`). The only chair within `NEAR_CHAIR = 36` px (`:1732`) is the porter's south-facing chair, about 35 px away, and the `posts` filter keeps it for the porter (`:1984-1986`). Fetching takes swivel chairs only (`:1993-1994`), and the hospital chairs are static. The result is "work standing".

What goes wrong: the domestic stands beside a chair for the whole mission, while the porter sits. The 6.0 promise "come back and sit" holds for only one of the two, and the seated frames bought for her in stage 6 are never shown.

Fix, one of:
- (a) Post the domestic at a chair the engine can seat. The only other sittable chair in the room is the centre table's `hospital_chair_north` at (3.41, 6.25). That splits the pair across two tables, so the plan would then say why that still reads as a pair on a break.
- (b) Add `hospital_chair1` (faces west) and `hospital_chair2` (faces east) to `STATIC_SEATS`, with seat points, and check the side-facing seated frames. This is a shared engine data change, so it goes on the approval list with unit tests and live proof. Section 9's "no runtime engine change" would then need correcting.

Either way, 6.4's "seating finds each chair from the home cell, so both sit" is rewritten, and stage 4 proves both NPCs are seated through `window.__test.needs`.

## Minors

**m1. The post coordinates are Tiled anchors, and the porter's lies inside the table (6.3 people table; 6.4 "Posts").** In `room_hospital_staff.json`, (6.72, 5.78) and (7.78, 6.5) are the bottom-left anchors of the chair sprites. The east table `desk3.png` at (6.16, 6.56), 51 by 39 px, covers x 6.16 to 7.75 and y 5.34 to 6.56, so the porter's tile home is inside its footprint. A tile home goes through `tileToWorld` unchanged (`_resolveHome`, `npc-needs.js:3751-3765`). Fix: give each post as the walkable cell in front of its chair, read live through `window.__test.needs` in stage 4, and say in 6.3 that the numbers are placeholders until then.

**m2. Entry breaks always go to the boiler, which upsets the rhythm and pool arithmetic (6.4 "Aim", 6.8 "Totals").** The entry mapping only ever sets caffeine, so every player-triggered break is a boiler break with `coffee` pools. The staff room is the hub (four doors), so a player crossing it every two to three minutes hears a coffee line on most visits, and each 4-line coffee bag repeats within about 10 to 15 minutes, not the "about half an hour" in 6.8. An entry reset also moves the porter's caffeine phase. If his next tea falls while the domestic is away from her post, her thirst trip starts away from the post, `npc_left_post` does not fire (`_leavePost` requires `atPost`, `:2963`), and that break is solo and silent. Fix: alternate the entry break between the two topics (two mappings with complementary conditions on a scalar the scenario already sets, or one on the porter's caffeine and one on the domestic's thirst, each with a 240 s cooldown), and redo the bag arithmetic with player visits included.

**m3. Gary option (a): the snap arithmetic and the proof test do not match the engine (6.2 (a), first and last bullets).** The walk grid is 8 px (`PATHFINDING_STEP = 8`, `utils/constants.js:37`; `findNearestWalkableWorldCell`, `npc-pathfinding.js:625-653`), so any point is within 5.7 px of a cell centre. "About 11.5 px" is the figure for a 32 px grid. It also uses the spawn point, but `npcPos` is the body centre (`npc-needs.js:4793-4798`). Within `HOME_EPS = 12` the engine never moves him (`_goHome`, `:3777`; `_moveForPhase`, `:3036`), so he stays at his spawn, up to about 6 px from the cell centre, and the stage 4 proof "within 2 px of his snapped home cell" can fail with nothing wrong. Separately, the "mug on his desk" is about 1.5 tiles (roughly 50 px) from where he stands (desk `hospital_desk2` spans x 4.0 to 5.94 with its front edge at y 4.69; Gary is at (6.3, 5.8)), so he drinks from a mug he cannot reach. Fix: make the proof "within 2 px of his spawn body centre, and within `HOME_EPS` of the mug's approach cell". Place the mug on the desk corner nearest him and check a screenshot. State Gary's caffeine numbers (decay, urgentAt, initial), as the plan does for the pair.

**m4. Gary option (b) is missing the approach cells it needs (6.2 (b), first bullet).** The IT kettle is the `kettle1` sprite on the workbench (conditional_table_items at (7.06, 3.94); the workbench `it_workbench1` covers about x 7.0 to 8.9 and y 2.94 to 4.75). The preset `useFrom { dy: 1 }` (`office-presets.json`, kettle) lands inside the workbench, and `approachCell` searches only one 8 px cell round a `useFrom` point (`npc-needs.js:4841-4845`). The photocopier stands against the south wall at (6.31, 7.88), so the preset's `dy: 1` points behind it. The plan gives the boiler and microwave hand-written `useFrom` for this reason but gives none here. Fix: add hand-written `useFrom` for the IT kettle (in front of the workbench) and the copier (north of it, `dy: -1`) to option (b), so the user is choosing between two options that both work as written.

**m5. The cleared exchange can play without its setup (6.8 "Exchange gates", item 2).** `requireGlobal` is a strict equality check on every key (`requiredGlobalsHold`, `npc-needs.js:266-273`). Most players restore their cover quickly through Bernie or Val, so they never hear `night_watch_burned`, and then hear "word on the radio that the visitor checks out" with no earlier word that anyone doubted them. Fix: gate `night_watch_cleared` on `{ "cover_restored": true, "heard_night_watch_burned": true }`, or rewrite its first line so it stands alone.

**m6. The printout's wording and lifetime need pinning down (6.6 "The printout"; 6.9; 6.11).**
- An automatic reply to the seventh email would carry its subject line, "RE: URGENT -- ProFTPD 1.3.3c backdoor on backup server" (`scenario.json.erb:97`). That repeats a clue word for word and names the VM's exploit target on a free page, against the plan's own rule that the page repeats no existing clue. Fix: state the page's exact header, for example a subject "Your message to the Board Secretariat", and check it against `gary_warning_email` and `board_reply_email` (`:97-99`). Note that Kim's real answer came on 12 November, after the board reviewed it, so the page must not suggest the board never saw it.
- A reload files every revealed slot (schema `outputOf`: "a reload files every revealed slot"), and the print mapping is `onceOnly` and saved (`npc-manager.js:1418-1424`). A player who reloads before taking the page loses it for good. Fix: list this in 6.11's reload checks and accept it in writing, since the page is optional flavour.

**m7. The warm mug sits in the porter's place (6.3 "The free items in the room").** The table item `office-misc-cup.png` at (6.56, 5.79) is at the porter's post (6.72, 5.78), and its engine observation invites the player to take it ("Mug of coffee, still warm. Take it with you", `core/rooms.js:2895-2903`). The player has to lean over a seated man to take his coffee, and then he gets up to make tea. This works against a pilot meant to make staff feel alive. Fix: post the porter at the other end of the table, or accept the mug as his and say in his `observations` that it has gone cold. No existing object changes.

**m8. E1's slice name, scenario and stakes are inconsistent (section 9 E1 row; section 9 "Alignment" first bullet).** The E1 row says "slice SIMS-2 (`test_needs_sims`, with the hostile-from-start case in `test_npc_needs`)". The alignment bullets, section 13 step 3 and the showcase plan (`OFFICE_LIFE_SIMS_BUILD_PLAN.md` on `build/ol-sims`, line 107 and "SIMS-1: E1 ... in `test_needs_attack`") all say SIMS-1 in `test_needs_attack`. The alignment bullet also says E1's result "decides whether the staff-room pair keep their walks". The pair never leave the staff room, and E1's fallback is "needs inside their own room only", which is what they already have, so E1 does not gate the pilot's design. Fix: correct the E1 row to SIMS-1 and `test_needs_attack`, and say that E1 gates later missions' cross-room walkers, not the m02 pair.

**m9. The `grumble` rule in section 2, and V10 after it, are too narrow (section 2 "Talk needs a pair"; section 4 V10).** `grumble` is also spoken on `off` and `gone` turn-backs (a `grumble_<reason>` pool, else `grumble`, `npc-needs.js:2086-2094`; the failure grumble at `:1545`), and printers use `grumble_printer_off` (`:3571`). V10's "`grumble` needs an avoid or a queue the NPC can meet" would then warn on a valid pool reached through an `offWhen` outage, and miss `grumble_<reason>` pools. Fix: list the turn-back reasons that speak a grumble (avoid, queue, off, gone, printer off) in section 2 and in V10's rule.
