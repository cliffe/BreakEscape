## 2. Majors

### W1. `ringBlocked()` and `openBlocked()` read a stale `_scriptedCall`, so the first ring after any scripted scene never comes (major)

2.2 has both checks block while "`npcManager._scriptedCall` is set", and 2.9 says the ringer "must read `_scriptedCall` and `_scriptedCallQueue` directly, never `_scriptedCallBusy()`". But `_scriptedCall` is cleared lazily. `_openScriptedConversation` sets it to `{ npcId, minigame }` (`npc-manager.js:762`), and the only places that clear it are `_scriptedCallBusy()` itself, when it finds the minigame no longer open (`:746`), the start-delay guard (`:703`), a refused `beforeOpen` (`:753`) and `destroy` (`:2657`). When a scripted scene closes, nothing calls `_scriptedCallBusy()` unless another scripted conversation arrives or the pump is running (`:683`, `:776`). So after the first scripted scene of a mission, `_scriptedCall` stays set, pointing at an ended minigame.

m01 shows the effect. It opens with a `timedConversation` person-chat briefing (`scenarios/m01_first_contact/scenario.json.erb:563-569`), which leaves `_scriptedCall` set. The new `first_ring` on entering `main_office_area` (7.2) then sees `ringBlocked()` true and stays `queued` until some later scripted beat happens to clear it. A held call in m04, m05 or m09 would wait the same way, after the briefing. The unit tests won't catch it with a fake npcManager whose `_scriptedCall` is null.

Fix: in `npc-manager.js`, split today's body of `_scriptedCallBusy()` into `scriptedConversationActive()`, keeping its stale-clearing. Then `_scriptedCallBusy()` is `window.phoneRinger?.callActive?.() || this.scriptedConversationActive()`. In 2.2 and 2.9, `ringBlocked()` and `openBlocked()` call `scriptedConversationActive() || _scriptedCallQueue.length > 0`, never `_scriptedCall` directly. The two still don't call each other. Add a named test to `phone-ringer.test.mjs` and `phone-call-busy.test.mjs`: a scripted person-chat opens and closes, then a ring offer rings at once. Add a `test_phone_calls` route (A2b) that plays an opening `timedConversation` before `caller_basic`.

### W2. The post-call story reset is placed where no story is loaded, and it races the phone's reopen (major)

2.5 "Story position after a call" and A2b put the reset "in the ringer's row 25 handler, not in any shared close path": "if the saved story is inside a call knot (`this.conversation.callKnots`) or has ended, jump it to the home knot with `goToKnot(homeKnot)` and save it without running it". By the time the adapter's end hook runs (`minigame-manager.js:300`, after `cleanup`), `closeConversation()` has set `this.conversation = null` (`phone-chat-minigame.js:1449-1450`), and the minigame is gone. The adapter has no loaded story, no `callKnots` set, and no ink engine. To follow the text it would have to fetch and load the story JSON again and restore `npc.storyState` into it, as `previewReopen` does with a spare engine (`phone-chat-conversation.js:752-760`). That is asynchronous. In the same handler, row 25 reopens the player's phone (row 10), which reads `npc.storyState` straight away. If that happens before the reset is saved, the reopened thread shows the call's last choices as silent texts. That is the exact case the reset exists to prevent, and A3's `caller_thread` proof is where it would show.

Fix: do the reset inside the phone-chat minigame, synchronously, while the story is still loaded. It stays gated on `this.params.ringerCall`, so `callLaunch` and the legacy voiced calls are still untouched. One helper, `_resetAfterRingerCall()`, is called just before `complete(true)` in the three ringer end paths of 2.5 "Ending", and in `cleanup` for a non-forced ringer call that ends without `_endedByCall` (row 32). If the story has ended, or `this.conversation.callKnots` holds its current knot, the helper calls `this.conversation.goToKnot(homeKnot)` without continuing, then `_saveStoryStateFor(npcId, this.conversation)` (`:1234`). A forced call skips it (row 26). Then the adapter's row 25 and row 32 handlers do only the bookkeeping, the rows and the guarded reopen. Update 2.5, rows 25 and 32, A2b's bullet "The post-call story reset … in the adapter's row 25 handler", and the `phone-outgoing-call.test.mjs` line ("after End mid-choice the saved story is on the home knot"), adding the A3 reopen case.

### W3. Five bring-in calls would play the same clips, against the plan's own rule 3.6 (major, user requirement on variety)

3.6 says "Never the same sentence twice across missions", and 7.5 says each bring-in will "sound different". But the 7.2 rows for the m03, m04, m05 and m06 bring-ins mark the ink "light" and change only the diverts. These are the lines those knots would voice today:

The m01 rewrite in 7.2 adds a fifth ("There's a car at the front. See you at HQ."). The HaX phone records in m03, m04, m05 and m06 have the same voice block, name, style and language (checked: the normalised blocks hash the same), and the key is text plus voice and style (`tts_service.rb:171`). So four of these would not only read the same, they would be the same audio file.
- "Once you're out, you don't go back in. Whatever you haven't read stays for forensics." is word for word the same in m04 (`:440`) and m06 (`:597`). m03 (`:597`) differs by four words.
- "Car's at … Two minutes." appears in m03 `:605`, m04 `:451`, m05 `:971` and m06 `:605`.

Section 8 step 2 asks the writer to compare openings across missions. But it compares only "the opening line of every call", so these closing lines slip through, and the rows' line counts (~6 to ~12) leave no room to rewrite them.

Fix:
1. In each 7.2 bring-in row (m03, m04, m05, m06, and m01's rewrite), name the opening line, the "car" line and the sign-off as lines to rewrite, mark the ink "rewrite" in place of "light", and give each mission its own sign-off tied to its story. For example: m03 after the turn, "Leave her to us. Side door."; m04 "Albion's grid is someone else's now. Loading dock."; m05 "He'll keep till Thursday. Front entrance."; m06 a night pick-up; m01 drops "See you at HQ." for something only m01 can say.
2. Extend section 8 step 2 and 3.6 from "the opening line of every call" to "the opening line and the last NPC line of every call". Add a check to the M slices' step 3: the key diff shows no new key that is the same as a key from another mission's call knot.
3. Raise the 7.4 bring-in line count to match.

## 3. Minors

- **w4. A text toast clicked during a ringer call hangs up.** During a phone-chat, other contacts' barks stay on top (`MinigameFramework.holdsBarks` is false for `phone-chat`, `js/minigames/framework/minigame-manager.js:195-199`). A click opens that contact's thread through `startMinigame` (`js/systems/npc-barks.js:449-455, 668`), which ends the call. Only a forced call is guarded (`inventoryBlockedByForcedMinigame`). Under row 32 that is a miss with a callback, so HaX's bring-in can be cut off by one stray click, and new texts arrive often in these beats (m01's "Texting from here, then."). Fix: in A2b, `_shouldDefer` (`npc-barks.js:175-186`) also holds barks while `window.phoneRinger?.callActive?.()`, and `endMinigame` already drains them once nothing is open (`minigame-manager.js:300-303`). Add a case to `phone-ring-layer.test.mjs`.
- **w5. Auto-challenges are dropped during a ringer call, and the plan doesn't say so.** In the busy branch a `dropIfBusy` conversation (any `*_in_view` event, `npc-manager.js:1391`) is dropped, not queued (`:719-723`). With the busy rule, a guard who sees the player during a call drops the challenge, and it fires again on the next sighting. Today the challenge would close the phone. That's a reasonable outcome, but it changes a mechanic m03, m10 and `test_gate_tailgate` use (`grep _in_view`). Fix: state it in 2.9 and the README busy-rule paragraph (A7), and add a case to `phone-call-busy.test.mjs`.
- **w6. m04's bring-in can say "See you at HQ." after the player chose "Keep going".** m04's debrief mapping uses the mapping-level `endingCheck: true` (`scenarios/m04_critical_failure/scenario.json.erb:1556-1564`). The question is asked when `mission_complete` is set (`npc-manager.js:1021-1037`), which happens as the tags of the line "See you at HQ." are applied (`m04_phone_agent0x99.ink:462-466`). The call types and voices that line under the overlay, whatever the answer. A text did the same, but a voiced hand-off makes it jarring. Fix: in M4, put `#ending_check` inside the `[On my way.]` choice's brackets (asked before the choice is taken, `phone-chat-minigame.js:1007-1022`), or test both answers in the M4 proof, as M8 does for m01.
- **w7. 3.6 and the m04 row disagree on the answer-delay line.** 3.6 gives m04 a clipped `call_answer_seconds > 10` line ("Twenty seconds is a long time on a turbine"), and says every call where a wait means something gets one. The 7.2 m04 dial-ring row gives only a callback line, and the 2.11 example knot (also `on_anomaly_confirmed`) uses different wording again. Fix: pick one and write it into the 7.2 row. "Twenty seconds" can't happen on a default ring, which rings out at 20 s.
- **w8. Two A2b acceptance items need later slices.** `caller_patient`'s "declined at 30 s leaves a callback" needs A6. `caller_basic`'s "after 12 s the second time" needs a second offer, but the contact has one `onceOnly` `room_entered` mapping. Fix: mark the callback half "A6", as `caller_decline` is marked. Give `caller_basic` a lever-set global (or a second room) for the slow-answer run.
- **w9. The engine VAR writer ignores the channel.** 2.11 says "every other channel … gets the defaults", but the sketched writer `({ npcId }) => callVariablesFor(npcId)` returns the live values for any channel, including the phone preload (`phone-chat-conversation.js:865`, channel `phone-preload`). Fix: the writer returns the live values only when `channel === 'phone-chat'` and the story is the call's own, else the defaults. Add the case to `phone-call-vars.test.mjs`.
