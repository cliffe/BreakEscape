# Lock types, the keyring and the second step: do they co-exist?

Review for the author's question on `docs/achitechture/OPS_LOCK_SECOND_STEP_PLAN.md` (the "lock
plan", A25): "Have fingerprint biometrics, bluetooth proximity lock, rfid fob all been considered
in terms of how they combine with the keyring?" Read-only audit, 2026-11-19, worktree `ops-plan`.
Paths: `game.rb` is `app/models/break_escape/game.rb`, `gc.rb` is
`app/controllers/break_escape/games_controller.rb`, `us.js` is
`public/break_escape/js/systems/unlock-system.js`. Plan pointers are `plan:line`; the log plan
is `log:line` (`docs/achitechture/OPS_LOG_ROWS_PLAN.md`), the MFA plan `mfa:line`.

## 0. The short answer (for the decisions page)

The keyring (A15) and the physical locks never touch. The keyring holds sign-in credentials
only and is read only by `POST /login` at a sign-in PC; no lock type in `validate_unlock`
reads it, and a lock never accepts a keyring entry. Cards and fobs are inventory keycards plus
server-recorded clones, fingerprints are a separate list of lifted prints, and Bluetooth/BLE
are a scan made in the browser. So there is no double-counting and no way for one to stand in
for another. The second step sits on top of each lock the same way, after its own check. The
real issues are smaller and are on the browser side: three places that show "Door opened" or
play the release sound before the server has answered (they would lie while a step is
pending), two places where the approval pane would open over a screen that is already up (the
card copier and an NPC conversation), and one log rule that would record a badge swipe when an
NPC opens a card door. None needs the model changed; one (the pane over a conversation) needs
a decision. Fingerprint and Bluetooth/BLE locks are trusted from the browser today, so for
them the second step is the only part the server enforces.

## 2. Interactions and conflicts

Grades: **fine**, **needs a sentence** (in a plan, or a small listed fix), **needs a design
change** (a decision the plan does not yet make).

| # | Interaction | Evidence | Grade |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Keyring vs every lock.** No lock reads A15 and no keyring entry opens a lock. The keyring is read only by `GET /keyring` and `attempt_credential_login!`; `validate_unlock` has no keyring branch. A lock step therefore cannot be skipped through the keyring, and the MFA plan already stops a saved connection skipping the sign-in step. | `game.rb:4597-4782`; ACCESS:635-637; mfa:364-373 | fine |
| 2 | **A fob or card held as an item vs a keyring credential.** A keycard is an inventory item checked by `held_keycard_ref_ids` (`game.rb:4040-4051`); a keyring `held` proof is a separate inventory test on an item's `credential` field (`credential_keyring.rb:42-64`). They can only meet if an author puts `credential` on a keycard (a "smart card" that both opens doors and signs in). Schema allows any tray type (`scenario-schema.json:5158`). Then: a clone opens the door but never proves the keyring entry (identity-strict, rejects forged items), which is right; a **revoked** card still proves the keyring entry, because the keyring test never reads `revoked_card_ids`. No scenario does this today. | `game.rb:4019-4021` vs `credential_keyring.rb:42-64` (no revoked check) | needs a sentence (ACCESS A15 or the validator: warn on `credential` on a `keycard`, or make revocation apply) |
| 3 | **Cloned card whose holder is not the step's owner.** Allowed and coherent: the step owner is authored (`secondStep.owner`, required on a room, plan:175) and is independent of whose card opened the reader. The log will show `mfa_challenge`/`mfa_prompt_*` as `{ npc: owner }` and then `badge_swipe ok` as the cloned card's holder: two different people in adjacent rows. That is a fair teaching outcome (the badge says Maya, the approval came from Moira's phone), but log readers and debrief text should expect it. | plan:672-675, :690-692; log:1161 | needs a sentence (log plan 13.8 or lock plan 13: "the two rows may name different people") |
| 4 | **Proximity locks have no attempt moment?** They do. No code opens a Bluetooth or BLE lock by proximity alone: the scanners only list devices (`bluetooth-scanner-minigame.js:238-260`, `ble-scanner-minigame.js:228-260`), and every unlock goes through `handleUnlock` on interaction, which posts `/unlock` (`us.js:1305-1385`); a grep finds no other `notifyServerUnlock` caller for these methods. BLE posts after its scanner closes (`ble-scanner-minigame.js:612`, `us.js:1374-1378`). The pane opens on that post. | `us.js:1305-1385` | fine |
| 5 | **Success shown before the server answers (would lie under a pending step).** Three callers: the Bluetooth branch shows "Successfully connected ... and unlocked" synchronously, before the post resolves (`us.js:1339-1343`); the RFID direct-card branch plays `door_release` and "Door opened with X" in `.then()` without testing `success` (`us.js:1526-1529`); the RFID clone branch does the same after the copier (`us.js:1478-1482`). Key and lockpick success toasts come from `minigame-starters.js` at minigame completion (comment at `us.js:1160-1161`). The plan guards `unlockTarget` (plan:367-370), so the lock stays shut, but the sound and toast still fire on a `secondStepCancelled` answer, and the Bluetooth toast fires before the pane even opens. | `us.js:1339-1343`, `:1478-1482`, `:1526-1529`, `:1160-1161` | needs a sentence (5.4: name these four callers; move the toast/sound after a `success` test; a client test per caller) |
| 6 | **Pane opening over a screen already up: the card copier.** At an access-list rfid lock the copier is the current minigame when `requestRfidAccessUnlock` calls `ApiClient.unlock` (`us.js:1489`, `:177`), so the step pane opens while the copier is open. The plan says that with "a card" the pane "opens alone" (plan:379-380), which is true only for the direct-card branch. The copier also has a late-yes path that closes it (`us.js:1439-1444`, `:1492`). On a cancel, `requestRfidAccessUnlock` reports `denied` and the copier emits `door_unlock_failed` with reason `wrong_card` (`us.js:1510-1514`), which misnames a right card and can trigger hint or NPC reactions mapped to that event. | `us.js:1456-1494`, `:1505-1518`; plan:371-372 | needs a sentence (copier is the pane's parent, as a pad is; map `second_step_cancelled` to its own outcome, not `denied`/`wrong_card`) |
| 7 | **Pane opening over an NPC conversation.** The ink tag `unlock_door` fires `NPCGameBridge.unlockDoor` and lets the conversation carry on (`chat-helpers.js:99-121`); `unlockDoor` awaits `apiClient.unlock('door', roomId, npcId, 'npc')` (`npc-game-bridge.js:108`). With a step, `ApiClient.unlock` opens the pane mid-conversation. The plan lists "an NPC's door" as opening alone (plan:380). Where the pane goes (over the chat as a child, or queued until the chat closes), and what the chat shows meanwhile (today a cancel shows "Failed to unlock ... Unknown error", `chat-helpers.js:111-112`), is not decided. If the NPC is also the step owner, the player is asked to wait on a push to the person they are talking to. The validator warns on that pairing (lock plan section 9 warnings, NPC `unlockable`/`unlockDoor` on a step target), but the UX is still open. | `chat-helpers.js:99-121`; `npc-game-bridge.js:78-110`; plan:285-287, :373, :380 | needs a design change (small: pick queue-until-chat-closes or child-of-chat; and say what an owner-NPC's own `unlockDoor` does under Q1 (a)) |
| 8 | **MFA sign-in step vs a lock step on the same object.** Cannot be the same object: the lock plan refuses `secondStep` on a `login` lock (plan:144-146, validator section 9). They do meet in series: a `secondStep` archive inside a sign-in PC's desktop (plan:177-179). The player then meets the sign-in step and the archive's step, both drawing on the owner's one patience count `mfaPrompts[npcId]` (plan:322-324), so pestering at sign-in wears down the archive's owner too. The plan states this. | plan:144-146, :177-179, :322-324 | fine |
| 9 | **Fingerprint locks: prints are not items and the server trusts the reader.** Lifted prints live in `biometricSamples` (not inventory, not keyring); the server merge keeps only authored `fingerprintOwner`s but takes the client's quality (`game.rb:1006-1044`), and `validate_unlock` never reads the samples (`game.rb:4652-4657`, `:4757-4760`). A crafted post opens any biometric lock today. With a step, the step is the only enforced part (the plan says so, plan:307-309). `requires` on a biometric lock is a fingerprint owner name that may not be an NPC id, so it cannot serve as the step owner; the plan already requires an explicit `secondStep.owner` on rooms and on objects without a single `owner` (plan:175). The reader closes on "Accepted" before the post (`fingerprint-reader-minigame.js:241-256`, `:289-295`), so the pane opens alone, as planned. | as cited | fine (the plan's own sentence at plan:307-309 covers it; optionally say a scenario that needs a print to be proven should not rely on the print alone) |
| 10 | **Bluetooth/BLE on carried devices.** The Bluetooth scanner lists locked devices in the inventory as well (`bluetooth-scanner-minigame.js:362-385`), so a Bluetooth lock is often a phone or tablet the player can carry. The plan refuses `secondStep` on a top-level carried item (plan:180-181, Q7 (a)), so a takeable Bluetooth device cannot take a step; a step on one inside a container is fine. | plan:180-181, :733-735 | fine (already refused by the validator; worth one line in the G2 author note that "a carried Bluetooth device takes no step") |
| 11 | **Log rows per type.** Only rfid has a lock row of its own (`badge_swipe`, log:1054, :1161-1162). Key, lockpick, PIN, password, combination, cryptex, biometric, Bluetooth and BLE write no row when ungated, and with a step write `mfa_challenge` with `method` naming the first factor (plan:677), then `mfa_*` rows. Refused fingerprint or Bluetooth attempts never reach the server (the reader and scanner refuse locally), so no failed-print row can exist. Consistent with the plans. | log:1161-1162; plan:667-692 | fine |
| 12 | **`badge_swipe ok` when an NPC opens a card door.** The log plan keys the granted row on "rfid lock in force, not open, `validate_unlock` passed ... never on the request's `method`" (log:222-229). An NPC `unlockDoor` on an rfid door (`method: "npc"`) passes `validate_unlock` (`game.rb:4623-4626`), so it would write a badge swipe, with `as` taken from whatever keycard the player holds or none. With a step, the final `npc` post would write it after the gate. That records a swipe no card made. | log:222-229, :1161; `game.rb:4623-4626` | needs a sentence (log plan 5.1: write `badge_swipe` only when the method is `rfid`; the lock plan's section 13 sentence at :690-692 then holds as written) |
| 13 | **Cancelled step and the refused badge row.** A step that is denied, expires or is closed writes the `mfa_*` row and no `badge_swipe denied` (the denied row is only on a failed first factor, log:1162). Correct: the card was right. | plan:681-692; log:1162 | fine |
| 14 | **PIN dust and lock steps share a key format.** `lockSteps["object:<id>"]` uses the key `PinDust` uses (plan:318, `pin_dust.rb:77-80`) but in its own map, so no collision. | plan:318 | fine |
| 15 | **"Fob" as an item type.** The validator counts item types `fob` and `rfid` as rfid credentials (`scripts/validate_scenario.rb:2321`), but the engine only recognises `type: "keycard"` (`game.rb:4045`; `us.js:89-90`). A `fob` item would pass the validator's reachability check and open nothing. No scenario uses either type. Pre-existing and outside the lock plan. | as cited | needs a sentence (validator, separate from A25: drop `fob`/`rfid` or make the engine accept them) |

## 3. What was not checked

- `minigame-starters.js` key/lockpick success toast lines were taken from the comment at
  `us.js:1160-1161`, not read.
- Whether `MinigameFramework` can stack a child scene over `person-chat` or the RFID copier
  (relevant to items 6 and 7) was not read.
- Bluetooth on a door: the scanner reads room objects only, so a Bluetooth door works only when
  `requires` names a device object in the room. No scenario has one; not tested.
