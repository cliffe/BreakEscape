### Addendum: item 6 follow-up, timed dialogue paused under the resume overlay

**Fixed.** The cause was the auto-advance timer, as item 6 guessed. A control run with the pause turned off lost three briefing lines in 15 s under the overlay.

- **Signal:** `show.html.erb` sets `window.breakEscapeSessionOverlayShowing` when it shows the overlay. Resume clears it and dispatches `break-escape:session-overlay-closed` on `window`. Restart and New Session reload, so they need nothing. The key guard (`session-resume-keys.js`) is unchanged. The helpers are in `utils/session-overlay-pause.js`: `isSessionOverlayShowing`, `onSessionOverlayClosed` and `whenSessionOverlayClosed`.
- **person-chat:** every timed advance goes through `scheduleDialogueAdvance`, so the hold is there. While the overlay is up, the line stays on screen and no timer runs. At Resume the line gets its full delay from the start. That is fine because the overlay only shows at page load. A timer that comes due while the overlay is up waits too. A held advance is dropped if the line moved on meanwhile (a jump, choices, a newer schedule) or the minigame was cleaned up. Click-through mode is unchanged.
- **Voice:** TTS returns the clip's length once playback starts, and the advance waits that long. So the voice would have played under the overlay and the hold would only have covered the gap after it. The line's voice now starts at Resume (`await whenSessionOverlayClosed()` before `ttsManager.play`). Nothing is voiced on :3001, so this part was tested in node only.
- **Closing line:** a `disableClose` cutscene closes itself 2 s after its last line. That 2 s now starts at Resume.
- **Phone:** texts don't auto-advance and stay in the thread, so no line can be missed. Typing still waits for Resume (`_showStoryLine`), so the "typing…" bubble doesn't run unseen. Timed phone messages and barks are untouched: they aren't dialogue advance.
- **When does a cutscene sit under the overlay at all?** Rarely. On a reload the title screen is under the overlay, and the opening briefing doesn't replay once `briefing_played` is set (m01 and m08 set it as the briefing starts). It happens when the title screen's 2-minute failsafe closes it under the overlay, or when `briefing_played` didn't reach the server (m08 game 2685, after a Restart).
