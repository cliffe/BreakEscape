## Things to look at first

- **Marks versus picture.** Dialogue marks come from real TTS playback edges and are safe to cut on (the subtitle is on screen about 0.1 to 0.2 s before line-start and stays until the next line). Minigame marks: t03 lock-open 13.76 is accurate to the picture (overworld from 13.8); t08 input-set lags the picture by about 0.3 s. Voiced lines auto-advance (about 0.9 s after a line ends), e.g. Pryce's second line at 13.93 and Reeves's wall-phone line.
- **Spoilers in the in-point windows.** None of the listed windows shows a code, PIN, login, readable post-it text beyond "one of the ones on the sticky note...", the ROT note output (crop t08), the IT cabinet contents (out before 14.5 in t03), or a toast/objective panel (frames checked at every window edge listed). The t14a recovery screen carries the finale's choice names (Ghost's keys, ENTROPY key material): editor's call. t15 narration before 3.6 names a badge id.
- **Overworld shots are small in frame.** At `--zoom-step 1` the rooms take roughly a third to half of the 1920x1080 frame in t02, t04 to t07, t09, t12, t13 (the room is a small tile map on black); plan crops. The ward and corridor shots have the camera following the player.
- **Game observations (not fixed).** (1) The recovery console panel is taller than the 1080 viewport (top cut). (2) Ghost's call on first use of the recovery console seizes the screen (by design). (3) The bridge `interact('patient_bed4')` picks the ventilator panel from the tap menu because its description contains the name. (4) Opening the CyberChef laptop leaves the page's AudioContext suspended; picking the workstation up or preloading it before recording made the capture drop about 30 % of frames.
