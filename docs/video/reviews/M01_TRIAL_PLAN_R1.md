## Verdict

Ready with fixes. The overall design (capture the real game with live audio on a virtual display, deterministic take scripts, data-driven Remotion edit, serial stages, the cost-first model split) is sound and matches the user's corrections: live capture, pixel fonts, cuts versus glitch rules, logo plus title, per-mission slideshow. The blockers are all in stage 0 and the shot list and are cheap to fix: F1 (own launcher, kiosk, viewport), F2 (Pulse and Node facts, shared `:99`/`be_rec`), F3 (the title screen has no "Break Escape" title; shot 2 must be a card and the wordmark is an open asset question). Fix those, then add the F5 to F11 corrections (voice-clip check, localStorage volumes, integer-scale framing, heavy-scene smoke test, slate after gesture, entry-state per shot) before dispatching stage 2.
