# m05 60 s trailer: picks (orchestrator draft, before takes)

Sources: `docs/video/edits/M05_TRAILER60_OUTLINE.md` (its "Decided lines (2026-11-23)" block wins), `docs/video/scout/m05_scout.md`, `docs/video/recipes/mission-trailer.md`, `docs/video/GUIDELINES.md`, and the m04 picks for form. Routes below were read in `scenarios/m05_insider_trading/ink/` and `scenario.json.erb`; none was played. Lengths are the cached clip lengths from the scout's live `/tts` run, plus the 23 Nov correction: the Recruiter's "I find people the week before they break, agent. Then I make them an offer." is cached (with its `#delivery:` note), 5.50 s. The scout's 503 on it was a test without the note. "Shame. I'd have placed you..." is not in the ink and is not used.

Spine: someone inside a Home Office contractor is selling the 999 network's keys; eight vetted people and one of them shouldn't be; you read the evidence and it points at the wrong person first; after hours the lights go out, and ENTROPY's Recruiter rings to ask your price.

**Torres stays hidden, off screen and unnamed, throughout (owner).** No face, portrait, name, office, briefcase, mug text, badge-log row, reference card or subtitle of his. He is the one NPC dropped from the crescendo, by that rule. Every Recruiter line before the price line in the call names him or calls the insider "the man": those clips stay out of the audio, and their subtitles out of the picture.

Day shots (before `torres_identified`) come first, then the night turn. Setting `torres_identified` unhides Torres in the data centre and turns Lisa's, Owen's and Patricia's barks to "David": night shots stay in empty corridors, and no bark audio from a night take reaches the mix.
