# m10 60 s trailer: picks (orchestrator draft, before takes)

Sources: `docs/video/edits/M10_TRAILER60_OUTLINE.md` (its "Decided lines (2026-11-23)" block wins), `docs/video/scout/m10_scout.md`, `docs/video/recipes/mission-trailer.md`, `docs/video/GUIDELINES.md`. Routes below were read in `scenarios/m10_full_disclosure/ink/` and `scenario.json.erb` and checked against the scout. None was played. Lengths are the cached clip lengths from the scout's live `/tts` run (the decided ending tested with its `#delivery:` note). Every cut inside a line is an estimate: check it by ear.

Spine: at six a man SAFETYNET trained will publish every flaw the service ever catalogued. The bunker's door already knows your name, and someone on your own team works for him. You go down, kill the gallery security by hand with HaX on the phone, fight past the tunnel post, and the Architect tells you why.

The whole mission is one night underground (the forward post is outdoors under lamps), so there is no day/night split. Story order is kept apart from two moves: Quintero's door line comes before the sleeper line, and the tunnel fight comes after the hacking, because the action belongs late (as the outline has it).
