Sentence cut points are the scout's `silencedetect` measurements or my word-count estimates: all are "estimate, check by ear".

Spine: SAFETYNET's own headquarters on Level Red. A holographic AI tells you the leak came from inside the building and that one of three colleagues got two agents killed. You interview the suspects, find the door audit and put it back to them, break into SAFETYNET's own code server, lose your temper with one of them, and the Director tells you the mole is within a hundred metres.

**The twist rule.** The mole's identity is the mission's end twist. Nothing names, singles out or shows him: each suspect gets exactly one voiced line, none of them is the "calm one" line, and nothing comes from `m08_nightshade_confrontation.ink`, the interrogation suite with a suspect in the chair, the USB print, the go-bag, the badge audit's text, or the Director's reading answers.

The whole mission is one night (`lighting.defaultMode: "on"`, `scenario.json.erb:601`; motion lights only in the server room, archives and suite, `:2040`, `:2133`, `:2318`). No day/night split. Keep shots from before `mole_identified` only: the music changes to the threat playlist at that flag (`:212`) and several hubs change with it.
