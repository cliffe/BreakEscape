## The ending route (filmed cleanly)

## The tracer shot (new mechanic, about 3.5 s)

What the board does (`public/break_escape/js/minigames/blockchain-explorer/mixer-match-board.js`): it is click, then click, not drag (the outline says drag). Pick a deposit on the left, the middle pane shows it and "Look for: this deposit less 1.5%, at least 6 hours later", each credit on the right shows its hold time and payout per cent, credits that cleared before the deposit are marked "before the deposit"; click the right credit and the feedback goes green ("Match. ... after the 6-hour hold."), both cards take the deposit's colour and the status bar reads "Matched 1/3".

The action: the board opens (pool-rules chips, `INTO HC-MIXER-01` with the three deposits "Ransomware proceeds", "Exploit sales", "Disinformation contracts", `OUT OF HC-MIXER-01` with the credits) 1 s; click "Ransomware proceeds", the credits show their deltas 1 s; click the matching credit, the green match and the colour link 1.5 s. The take script finds the right credit from the board's own model (`evaluateLink` in `mixer-match-model.js`); this file does not name it. Out before a second match.

Safe to show: the three deposit cards (labels, wallets, amounts, times), the rules chips, the middle pane including "Show working" and the change output, the credits' times, amounts and deltas, the green feedback text (it names amounts and hours, no slot), "Matched 1/3", the "How analysts match" tips.

**Mosaic on every frame and every aspect:** each `slot NNNN` label in the OUT column (`.bce-mx-slot`) and the "→ slot NNNN · time" link that appears on a matched deposit card (`.bce-mx-card-link`). A matched card names the fund's custody slot, which is the custody-console answer; mosaic all the slot labels so no frame pairs a match with a number.

**Never in the shot:** the all-matched middle pane ("ADDRESS REUSE" with its row of slot buttons), the "TRACED" pane with the big slot number and the completion text, the "Server files" view (the settlement log and custody index), and the custody console next to it.
