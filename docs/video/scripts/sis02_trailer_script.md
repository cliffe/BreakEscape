# sis02 trailer script (Albion Battery Hall: Code Red)

Status: script round 1 (both reviews applied: Opus sell and truth, Sonnet timing and take plan, plus the orchestrator's decisions). Not voiced, no takes yet. Scout: `docs/video/scout/sis02_scout.md` (a desk scout). Recipe: `docs/video/recipes/critical-cases-trailer.md`. Length **1802 frames at 30 fps = 60.07 s** (cap 65 s). Brand: "Incident Simulations: Critical Cases". No narrator VO (question 1). Cast lines only (Helen Marsh), all cached and measured. The game bar is cropped and the sentences used are burned as captions.

Clip times are from silencedetect (-35 dB, 0.2 s min, plus 0.06 s passes where a split mattered), measured by the Sonnet review and re-measured here for a0fe34. Each cast window is in = speech start minus 0.2 s and out = speech end plus 0.15 s (the brief's lead and tail), rounded to whole frames with the tail never under 0.13 s. Where a cast shot follows a card or the title (normal, mechanic) the lead is 0.30 s and the shot is 3 f longer, so the first word does not start under a half-faded picture. Every cut sits in a measured silence. The take marks the real audio start in game, and the edit cuts from the mark.

All durations are whole frames at 30 fps. Cards are 50 frames. The Join column says how a shot starts: "cut", "dissolve" (8 f pixel dissolve, inside the incoming shot's first 8 f, no extra length) or "glitch" (alarm to title only, 8 f, same rule).
