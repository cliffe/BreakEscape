## Major

**M1. Forced line breaks in cast-line captions are not honoured, so the hook and end captions are one line in 16:9, break the 28-character rule, and the lock-icon analysis rests on a wrong premise.**
Evidence: `CaCaption` (`CaSeats.tsx`, the cast-line caption, default skin) renders `{line.text}` in a `div` with no `white-space` setting, so a `\n` collapses to a space. Only `CaVo` (`whiteSpace: cue.caption ? 'pre-line'`) and the `pixel` skin (`CaCaptionPixel`, `pre-line`) keep breaks. The landed round 1 render shows it: `ca16_trailer_r1_16x9.mp4` at 65.2 s has `Niamh:  Could I borrow your login for an hour?` on one line (box y 864-982), although `ca16_trailer_r1.json` has `"text": "Could I borrow your login\nfor an hour?"`. Consequences for the R2 script:
1. "Burned captions (each line at most 28 characters)" and "the caption is two lines in both shots" are false in 16:9. The hook caption is 35 characters on one line, the two end captions 33 and 40 (the scripted break after 26 is not drawn). The speaker prefix "Niamh: " (the default skin adds it whenever the line has a `speaker`) adds 7 more characters.
2. 1:1 and 9:16 wrap by width, not at the script's break: 1:1 box max width 0.7 x 1080 = 756 px, 9:16 0.88 x 1080 = 950 px.
3. The hook check. One line in 16:9 is the case R2 m8 computed: box 864-982, monitor with lock icon at output y 970-1080, so the caption clips about 12 px of the icon top (acceptable). If breaks are made to work, a two-line caption is about 188 px tall (56 px x 1.25 x 2 + 2 x 20 padding + 8 border): box 864-1052, which covers the whole icon (970-1050 on the frame at 9.9 s) and the seated Niamh's lower legs at 7.0 s. The script's ordered remedy cannot then work: clearing the box needs the centre moved down 93 source px (window y 363-903), but the alert marker spans source y 352-422, so its top is 11 px outside the window in the end shot. Remedy 2 (zoom 1) is the only one that works.
Fix, either:
- A. Engine: make `CaCaption` honour breaks only when asked, so landed edits (ca16 r1, ca19 r1, ca20 r1, ca_product r2/r3 all carry `\n` in `lines[].text`) do not change. Add `whiteSpace: shot.caption_breaks ? 'pre-line' : undefined` to the text `div` in `CaCaption` and set `"caption_breaks": true` on the shot; `caCaptionBox` takes `line`, not `shot`, so pass the flag in and count `text.split('\n').length` lines there (it only moves the VO plate, and the hook and end carry no VO). Omit `speaker` on these lines (the silent viewer sees who talks, and the prefix breaks the rule). Then set both shots to zoom 1 in 16:9 and 1:1 now (monitor at source y 765-810, above the 16:9 box at 864 and the 1:1 box at about 838), and keep 9:16 at 1.125 (monitor at output y 1410-1500, box from about 1678).
- B. No engine change: `"caption_skin": "pixel"` on the hook and `niamh_end` shots. It already uses `pre-line`, sizes to 32 px for 20-character lines, and a two-line box is about 148 px (16:9 y 864-1012). The look is the dark Press Start 2P slab the product trailer uses, against the light VT323 plate of the narrator, so this is an owner call. Zoom 1 is still needed in 16:9 and 1:1.
Whichever is chosen, say it in the script's "Framing" paragraph and in "Not checked", and drop "the hook line is 35 characters and must match the spoken words, so it cannot go on one line" in favour of the actual mechanism.
