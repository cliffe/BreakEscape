Status: script round 1 (both reviews applied: Opus sell and truth, Sonnet timing). Not voiced, no takes yet. Scout: `docs/video/scout/sis01_scout.md` (a desk scout). Cast lines only, all cached and measured. The game bar is cropped and the sentences used are burned as captions.

Clip times are from silencedetect (-35 dB, 0.15 s min), re-measured this round for e1d540 and 9e67b3 and cross-checked against the Sonnet table for the rest. Each cast window is in = speech start minus 0.2 s and out = speech end plus 0.15 s (the brief's lead and tail), then rounded to whole frames with the tail never under 0.13 s. Every cut sits in a measured silence. The take marks the real audio start in game, and the edit cuts from the mark.

All durations are whole frames at 30 fps. Cards are 50 frames. The Join column says how a shot starts: "cut", "dissolve" (8 f pixel dissolve, inside the incoming shot's first 8 f, no extra length) or "glitch" (hook to title only, 8 f, same rule).
