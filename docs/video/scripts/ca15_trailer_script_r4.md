## 1. What changed from r1, and why

The r1 cut told four stories at once: a deadline "pack", a red-team kit, a tracker and session note, a six-months page, and a sort. It never said what tied them together, because the episode doesn't either (`ca15_usb_placement.md` section 1). r2 has one idea and says it in the first 20 seconds:

**Shadow IT is when staff take shortcuts round IT.** Every shot after that is an example of it, getting sharper, then the attacker's view of why a shortcut is an opening, then the learner's turn to sort them.

1. Hook: a stick on the office floor, "Whose is this?", played whole.
2. Name it: "This episode: Shadow IT." Define it: "When staff take shortcuts round IT."
3. The shortcuts, one card each, in rising order: client files to a personal inbox; a free app on a leaver's account; an untrusted device on the work network (the visitor's stick in a work laptop).
4. Sharpest last: "Even a found USB stick." The stick by the kettle, then Joanne plugs it into her work PC to find the owner. That is the shortcut: plugging in a device IT has never seen rather than handing it in.
5. The attacker's side, stated as a rewind: "You're the attacker. Your stick, your code." (the drop, under an `ATTACKER` tab and an `AUTHORISED TEST` chip). This says what plugging it in did: the stick carried the attacker's code, and it ran on her PC. Nilesh says why it works. "An attacker goes looking for shortcuts." over the six-months consequence of the first shortcut.
6. Your turn: "You sort the shortcuts", "Now you sort them.", the inbox, Fatima's found stick.
7. Close and CTA.

Cut from r1: the red-team panel (t10: "One from the red-team kit", Look up, Carry on), Nilesh's hand-over (its "Received: Red-team stick" toast), the session-note tray shot and v21 "The tracker reports home." ("tracker" needed the hand-over to make sense), v2_r3, v16 ("pack"), v17, v19, v9 (its "Found sticks go to IT, unplugged" is an advisory line). The drop now reads as part of the Shadow IT story: a device IT doesn't know about, on a work PC.

Hook fix: r1 cut the hook at 45.02 s of t11, in the pause between "Someone's dropped a memory stick." and "Whose is this?", so the second sentence never played. r2 holds the shot to the last frames of her bubble and lets the whole line finish over the open card (section 4).
