# sis03 trailer script (Meridian Cyber Insurance: Coverage Determination)

Status: script round 1 (both reviews applied: Opus sell and truth, Sonnet timing and take plan, plus the orchestrator's decisions). Not voiced, no takes yet. Scout: `docs/video/scout/sis03_scout.md` (a desk scout). Recipe: `docs/video/recipes/critical-cases-trailer.md`. Length **1800 frames at 30 fps = 60.00 s** (cap 65). Brand: "Incident Simulations: Critical Cases". No narrator VO (question 1). Eleanor Vance is the only cached voice, so every spoken line is hers; the cast does the work with a hard cut between her two sides. The game bar is cropped and the sentences used are burned as captions. No VM or console footage: the case is in-game only. No real public body (Ofgem, NCSC, NESO) and none of sis02's solution (the ESD, the SIS, the cable pull, the containment steps) is in picture or voice.

Clip times are from silencedetect (-35 dB, 0.2 s min, plus 0.06 s and -40/-45 dB passes where a split mattered), measured by the Sonnet review: d10703 S2 ends 4.745 and S4 ends 12.506 (at -45 dB 12.64, so 12.506 is the real end of "damage"). Each cast window is in = speech start minus 0.2 s and out = speech end plus 0.15 s (the brief's lead and tail), rounded to whole frames with the tail never under 0.13 s. Every cut sits in a measured silence. The take marks the real audio start in game, and the edit cuts from the mark.

All durations are whole frames at 30 fps. Cards are 50 frames. The Join column says how a shot starts: "cut", "dissolve" (8 f pixel dissolve, inside the incoming shot's first 8 f, no extra length), "dissolve 6" (6 f, used where the 0.2 s lead cannot be widened) or "glitch" (hook to title only, 8 f, same rule).

Stakes, truthfully: a claim of £8.2 million from an operator whose site is back after six weeks offline, a full rebuild of its control systems and damaged battery cells, and a coverage call under market pressure. Nobody is shown hurt and the script never says so. The line "physical battery cell damage" tells a sis02 player that the hall was damaged; that is mild and says nothing about how to play it.
