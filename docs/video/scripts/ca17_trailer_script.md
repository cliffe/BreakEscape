# ca17 trailer script (Acceptable use)

Status: script round 1 (first draft plus round 1 review fixes from Opus sell and truth and Sonnet timing; see "Changes from review (round 1)"), captions only, not voiced, no takes yet (scout: `docs/video/scout/ca17_scout.md`, a desk scout). Model: ca15's order without its choice half, and ca10's hook, end and card rules. Length 71.7 s. Owner card: none yet.

Owner rules (settled on ca12, 2026-11-22, with the batch 3 review rules): the trailer stands alone as a sell for the training, narrator VO about the course throughout; open on a strong in-game line, cut to the 5.0 s cold-open limit; open card with v1; name the topic in VO over footage that shows it; chapter cards are title only, and the blue card says what is being done, never what to check; in-game cast lines, no narrator over a character line; end on a living-office moment, never host advice; ca-close with the tagline and the Hacktivity.co.uk call (the card text and the spoken close use the same words). Spoilers fine except answers, tells, advisory lines and tallies, which stay hidden. No narration over verdict buttons. The narration never says "kiosk". PeaceFolk bed (D331, the scenario plays it); music is left to the edit. VO budget 2.3 words/s plus 0.3 s per internal stop, 0.25 s between cues, 0.2 s clear of cast lines, 0.2 s lead and 0.15 s tail per shot (Sulafat). Close 5.5 s.

Shape: ca17 is defend only (no attacker, no flip, no step-away), so there is no red chapter card and no flip. The left seat reads YOUR CALLS if the edit needs one. The sell is the "can I?" sort, a short inbox, and a depot that carries on. Nilesh opens (his own name, then his point), the format line comes from the same take after the blue card, the cards run as a montage, and the end is a depot moment.

## Notes for the takes and edit
