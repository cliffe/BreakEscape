What I'd flag

Points to judge

Music by ear

Steel Shadows in E Minor was picked from the beat grid, not by listening. It is a Noir track m02 already plays, but m10 also uses it in game. Say if it is wrong; there are two analysed fallbacks.

The antagonist

Graham Reeves is shown as the mystery's answer (45.4 to 52 s). Your call whether to reveal him. Fallback: a non-face beat, or a different line.

The ransom screen

The reception ransom note (13.5 s) shows a fictional wallet and support URL and the £150,000 demand. It is the mission's premise and a player sees it in the first minute, so it is not an answer. Say if you want it cropped tighter.

Kept off screen

CyberChef shows the encoded input only (the decoded pane is cropped off). The keypad digits are decoys, not the code. Gary's login post-it is cropped out of the password cut. Reeves's wide starts after his badge number clears.

RFID left out

m02 never hands the player the cloner, so there is no RFID tap screen to film. The crescendo runs ten cuts without it.

Real Hacking

A titled placeholder until in-game VM capture exists. m02's real tools are anonymous FTP, the ProFTPD backdoor, SSH as Gary and CyberChef (no Hydra: that is m01).
