## The human cost the scenario gives

Truthful reading, from the notification note and Eleanor's briefing: the attack switched off the safety trips at a battery site, racks overheated (up to about 58 C) and a hardwired emergency shutdown stopped it before thermal runaway. **Nobody is shown hurt anywhere in the game.** The cost is: six weeks offline for the whole 100 MW site, a replaced hall of batteries, a neighbouring pumping station (Trent Water) that lost time cleaning a workstation, and an operator that did the right thing on the night and now waits on a decision. The pressure on the player is the other side of it: pay everything and warranties mean nothing, refuse and the next operator learns insurance will not stand behind them. So the trailer's stakes are a near miss that was stopped and a company and its neighbours waiting on a verdict. No casualties, no invented danger now, no real insurer branding. Nothing in the clip says anyone was injured.
