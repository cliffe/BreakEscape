## Things to look at first

- Marks versus picture: dialogue marks are made from real TTS playback edges, and the game subtitle is on screen about 0.1 to 0.2 s before each line-start mark and stays until the next line starts. So the marks are safe to cut on. The t14 CyberChef marks lag by 0.5 s (see the table). The t15 keypad marks are accurate.
- All voiced lines were confirmed playing (a `line-start`/`line-end` pair from the TTS manager exists for each target, and the lengths match the scout's ffprobe values: 3.83, 1.95, 3.51, 2.82, 3.52, 4.23 s).
- Spoiler check: t12's pre-target section contains Maya's "kill people / Operation Shatter" lines and Derek's name (the lines at 14 to 17 s and 33.6 s are skipped mid-line, so they are partly audible). Cut to the marks only. t13 also includes "Derek's projects" at 16.81 to 17.27, just before the target.
- Walking shots are short: Sarah, Kevin and Maya have about 1.3 to 3 s of actual motion each, the rest is a still hold; the rooms are too small for a longer crossing, and the editor may prefer a 2 to 3 s excerpt.
- Environment at the end: Postgres 16 and the keyless Rails server (port 3001) are left running. Display 97 and its PulseAudio sink are left up (`VIDEO_STATE_DIR=/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-BreakEscape/4ad0f0fc-9167-59c4-a9ed-f5bc845c2a2d/scratchpad/video-takes2`).
