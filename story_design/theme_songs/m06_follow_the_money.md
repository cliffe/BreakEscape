# Follow the Money

## Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
He took a legend's name and called himself the Second,
He keeps a ghost for signing when the debts are reckoned.
Hospital ransoms pooling like rain on marble floors,
Washed white and paid out quietly through other doors.

[Pre-chorus]
You walk in with a lanyard and a regulator's smile,
The paper's good, the paper's gold, the paper lasts a while,
But one call to the FCA and all of it is gone,
Keep them talking, darling, keep your story on.

[Chorus]
Follow the money,
Down where the clean coins drown,
Through the mixer, through the dark, through every wallet in town.
Follow the money,
Every hop a hand that lied,
Follow the money, honey, and find who's on the other side.

[Verse 2]
She built a lock of lattices to keep the people free,
Now blood runs through the ledgers of her perfect privacy.
Her badge hangs at her collar, close enough to steal,
Lay the costed dead before her, and see if she will deal.
Below, a server's breathing in a room of humming light,
And every flag you carry home's a debt brought into sight.

[Pre-chorus]
Twelve point eight million, signed and set to go,
The wallets have gone quiet, and the quiet's how you know.
Three days on the clock and a smile across the desk,
He'll talk of something golden while you read the rest.

[Chorus]
Follow the money,
Down where the clean coins drown,
Through the mixer, through the dark, through every wallet in town.
Follow the money,
Every hop a hand that lied,
Follow the money, honey, and find who's on the other side.

[Bridge]
Freeze it, he's a martyr on every screen,
Watch it, and it carries on, the cleanest dirt you've seen.
He'll tell you either road you take, the turning was his own,
But a fortune leaves a footprint, and the footprints lead back home.
For somewhere past the final hop a doctor keeps the books,
He calls the dead his lessons, and his hand is in the way you look.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Follow the money,
Down to the cold where the keys lie still,
Every coin remembers, every ghost has left a will.
Follow the money,
Every vault a heart that's bought,
Follow the money, honey, all the way to the one who taught.

[Outro]
Money talks.
Money leaks.
Follow the money...

### Notes
- Verse 1 is Satoshi Nakamoto II, "the Second" of a borrowed name, who signs The Architect's Fund as "Satoshi's Ghost". The pooled hospital ransoms are Ransomware Incorporated's $2.4 million, and "other doors" is the payout to the Insider Threat Initiative through TalentStack.
- Pre-chorus 1 is the FCA supervisory-visit cover from HaX's briefing: the paperwork gets you past reception but won't survive a phone call to the FCA. "Clean coins drown" and "through the mixer" in the chorus stand for the Bitcoin-to-Monero mixer and the cold wallets, kept as imagery.
- Verse 2 is Dr Irina Volkova: her lattice-based cryptography, the lanyard "within a cloner's range" for the RFID cloner, and the turn-or-detain choice, where you show her the casualty projection on the fund allocation. The server breathing in the humming room is the server-room terminal, and the flags go back to the SAFETYNET drop-site as debts brought into sight.
- Pre-chorus 2 is the $12.8 million fund, the wallets going quiet before a payout, and the three-day clock. In the bridge, "Freeze it... watch it" is the asset choice, along with Satoshi's claim that either one serves his purpose (martyr or a year of surveillance). The cold storage recovery keys turn up in the final chorus as "the cold where the keys lie still".
- Twist hint: Satoshi's private Identity File names a former SAFETYNET strategist who mentored agents as the likely Architect. "The footprints lead back home", "a doctor keeps the books / his hand is in the way you look" (Dr Tesseract, and the training he wrote) and "the one who taught" point at this without naming him. "He calls the dead his lessons" echoes Satoshi's line that The Architect calls the casualties lessons.
- The outro twists Nightshade's briefing line, "Money's just another protocol, and this one leaks".

## Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
I built a dark room for the money to hide,
it goes in burning and it comes out snow.
I signed off every flag that came inside
and never asked the names of where they'd go.
One night I wrote a line I never quote,
*what have I become?* Then pulled the lanyard round my throat.

[Pre-chorus]
You walk in on borrowed paper,
asking for machines and not the files.
I know you're no one's regulator.
Don't tell me yet. Stay a while.

[Chorus]
Follow the money, follow it down,
past every lock I swore I'd keep,
through all the hands that never made a sound
to the last cold slot where the balances sleep.
Follow the money, I won't stand in your way.
They put the dead in a cell and moved on.
Follow the money home to me,
I built the dark it's running from.

[Verse 2]
Upstairs a ghost has framed a prophet's page
and signs the fund in someone else's name.
He says the world will fall, so set the date
and choose who's standing nearest to the flame.
The hospital ransoms came in through my door.
I called it privacy. I can't say that any more.

[Pre-chorus]
The clock says seventy-two,
and you lay the page across my desk.
I could unclip this and hand it through.
Don't ask me yet. Don't ask.

[Chorus]
Follow the money, follow it down,
past every lock I swore I'd keep,
through all the hands that never made a sound
to the last cold slot where the balances sleep.
Follow the money, I won't stand in your way.
They put the dead in a cell and moved on.
Follow the money home to me,
I built the dark it's running from.

[Bridge]
I built my locks on lattices against the day
the curves give way and every wallet's read.
I never thought the thing to break would be the way
I saw what I had made and turned my head.
And the one who wrote the clock for every cell
keeps his name behind a door whose numbers I know well.

[Final chorus, up a step]
Follow the money, follow it down,
I'll open every door I swore I'd keep.
They put the dead in a cell and moved on.
Follow the money home to me,
I built the dark. I'll show you where it's gone.

[Outro]
Freeze it, or watch it run.
He smiles either way.
What have I become?

### Notes
- Narrator is Dr Irina Volkova, HashChain's CTO. "A dark room for the money... in burning, out snow" is her mixer, which washes Bitcoin through Monero into cold wallets. "Signed off every flag" is the transaction review she does personally. "What have I become?" is the last line of her research note on her workstation, and the lanyard is the office badge she has worn for four years.
- "I could unclip this and hand it through" is that badge, which she lends to the player (or the player clones it off her lanyard). "Borrowed paper", "asking for machines and not the files", "no one's regulator" and "Don't tell me yet" follow her suspicion of the fake FCA supervisor almost word for word.
- "The last cold slot where the balances sleep", "twelve million", "the clock says seventy-two" and "they put the dead in a cell" cover the Architect's Fund custody slot, the $12.8M pre-signed release, the 72-hour payout and the casualty projection. "Cell" means both the spreadsheet cell and an ENTROPY cell, and comes from her line "They put a number in a cell and moved on."
- Verse 2 opens on Satoshi Nakamoto II: the whitepaper framed behind his desk and the fund signed as Satoshi's Ghost, then his "the system's going to fall anyway; I'd rather choose who pays". "I called it privacy" is her "I told myself it was about privacy". "Hospital ransoms" is the RansomInc money from m02. The outro's "freeze it, or watch it run / he smiles either way" is the seize-or-monitor choice and his "either choice serves our purpose".
- The bridge opens with her post-quantum lattice research. Its last two lines hint at the twist: Irina knows Satoshi's safe code (2140) and that whatever he keeps on The Architect is inside; the page names Dr Adrian Tesseract as the likely Architect. The song doesn't name him.
- The final chorus turns from her guilt to her choosing to help ("I'll open every door"), which leaves room for the player to recruit her, though the song doesn't say she was turned.

## Review

- Version A: brassy, well-plotted melodrama with the best chorus hook of the pair ("Follow the money, honey") and dense, accurate mission detail; one invented beat and a bridge line that pointed at the wrong teacher. 8/10 → 8.5/10.
- Version B: an intimate, guilt-driven Irina monologue with the stronger imagery ("in burning, out snow", the dead "in a cell"); the second chorus was cut short to save words, and the bridge let Irina know something she doesn't. 8/10 → 9/10.
- Changes made:
  - A, Pre-chorus 2: "He'll pour you something golden while you read the rest" → "He'll talk of something golden while you read the rest". Satoshi never pours a drink in the ink; "golden" now reads as his gold-standard sermon.
  - A, Bridge: "and he taught you how to look" → "and he taught your teacher how to look". Canon has Tesseract teaching HaX ("I was one of his students") and writing the training HaX gave the player, so the new line is accurate. The old "taught you" pointed at whoever taught the player to follow money, and in m06 that is Nightshade's briefing ("Money's just another protocol, and this one leaks", which the outro also quotes). It risked casting m08's mole as the Architect. The line now stays inside what m06 already reveals (the 87% Tesseract page and HaX's "he wrote the training I gave you") and still keeps the m09 confirmation offstage.
  - B, second Chorus: restored to the full eight-line chorus. It had been trimmed for word count and its new line "Twelve million reasons and a sum" was weak and misquoted the $12.8M. Repeat choruses don't count towards the limit.
  - B, Bridge: "learned his patience in a house you know too well" → "keeps his name behind a door whose numbers I know well". Irina never learns of the SAFETYNET link; she knows the safe code (2140) and that Satoshi keeps his Architect notes inside. The new line keeps the foreshadowing inside her point of view.
- Canon check on the teacher hint: canon. The safe file says Tesseract "mentored several current SAFETYNET agents", and the m06 debrief has HaX as his student. The season arc makes Nightshade a peer recruited alongside the player, not a mentor, so with "your teacher" the line points at HaX's mentor and not at Nightshade. The finale reveal is that the 87% guess is true, which A only hints at ("the footprints lead back home").
- Stronger version: B, narrowly. It holds one voice all the way through, its chorus turns from guilt to help without stating the recruit outcome, and the "cell" double meaning is the best line in either song. A has the catchier hook.
- Left for the user: B's "pulled the lanyard round my throat" can be heard as a noose image. I kept it as a deliberate dark double meaning, but you may want it lighter.

### Cross-season pass

- A, Bridge: "a teacher keeps the books, / He calls the dead his lessons, and he taught your teacher how to look." → "a doctor keeps the books, / He calls the dead his lessons, and his hand is in the way you look." **Architect thread.** "Taught your teacher" was almost word for word m07 A's "who taught your teacher that art?", so m07 would only have restated m06. m06's reveal is narrower: the safe page names a doctor, and HaX says "he wrote the training I gave you". The new lines say that (a Dr behind the fund, his hand in the agent's training) and leave HaX's teacher to m07. "The one who taught" in the final chorus stays as the payoff.
- A, V2: "Below, a cursor's breathing… / And every flag you carry home's a candle in the night." → "Below, a server's breathing… / And every flag you carry home's a debt brought into sight." Cursors appear in four songs and candles in five. "A debt brought into sight" is follow-the-money.
- Notes updated to match.
