# Licence to Hack: theme songs

These are Bond-style title songs for the ten Licence to Hack missions, one per mission, written to play over an imagined opening-titles sequence. They aren't in the game. Each mission has two versions by different authors working from `BRIEF.md` and the mission's ink and scenario. Version A is classic (Bassey and *Goldfinger*: brass, melodrama, a narrator who addresses the mark). Version B is modern (*Skyfall* and *No Time to Die*: intimate, from inside one character's head). A reviewer then merged each pair into `<mission>.md`, scored both against the brief, fixed canon slips, scansion and spoilers with targeted line edits, and listed what it couldn't settle. Last came a cross-season pass that read all ten in order. It thinned out lines and images that repeated across the set ("let it hurt afterwards", "darling", clocks, candles, doors left open), kept the mole hints from giving m08 away early, made the Architect thread build from song to song, and corrected WREN's allegiance in m09. Each file's `### Cross-season pass` section lists those edits.

Version C came later: a third song per mission, sung by Agent 0x00, the player, nameless and ungendered. It follows the agent through the season, keen on day one, tested, betrayed from inside in m08, then hunting, then confessing on the way down in m10. Each C was reviewed on its own (`## Review (C)`). A second cross-season pass then read the ten C songs in order as one voice, against the A/B set. It varied images that recurred in three or more songs (strangers, signing in, "HaX in my ear", "nobody here knows", wires, keys, the deal/cuffs/door bridge list, the dig hooks and "signed" in m10). It took the last Nightshade pointers out of m04–m06 (m05 B's "always here", m06 A's "the one who taught", m06 C's "course I sat", m04 C's "friend on the wire"), and it made every C hold whatever the player chose. Each file's `### Cross-season pass (C)` section lists those edits. The first drafts are in `drafts/`.

## Decisions for the designer

**Season-wide (cross-season pass)**
- The villain sings to the agent in both versions of m01, m03, m07, m09 and m10 (and in m02 A). Re-voicing one pair would mean a new draft, not a line edit.
- "Darling" is now capped at two per song but still appears in eight missions' A versions. Keep it as the classic versions' signature, or cut it further.
- Badges and lanyards recur in most missions because cloning badges recurs in the game. They were thinned (m01 B, m04 A, m05 A), not removed.
- The Architect's "file on you" (the Reading) appears in m07 B, both m09 songs and both m10 songs. It was kept as the thread that builds, ending on m10 B's hook.
- "Let it hurt afterwards" now survives only in m07 B (the Architect's first use) and m08 B (Nightshade's parting line), plus the canon card "Afterwards, then" that ends both m10 songs. You could give m10 A a different last word.

**Version C, season-wide (cross-season pass C)**
- Nearly every C bridge sings the mission's choice, as A and B do. The pass removed the outright ending lists (m03's deal/cuffs/door, m05's cell/second life/headline) but kept m02's pay/refuse/take-it-free and m08's two keys, which sing consequences rather than name endings. Cut further if the device still feels samey across thirty songs.
- Hands appear in all ten C songs. m10's hook ("the hand is mine") depends on them, so the pass thinned only m02 and m04.
- Mole thread, read m01–m07 in order: after this pass no pre-m08 song in any version ties a teacher, a classmate, "always here", calm or a friend to SAFETYNET. The leak-at-home hints start in m07 (A's and B's unlatched windows, C's "only our side knew"), as mole_seeding_plan.md allows. The only pre-m08 "taught" left is m07 A's "taught your teacher", which points at HaX's teacher, Tesseract. Confirm you're happy for m06 A to end on "the one who's never caught", which replaces the earlier pass's "the one who taught" payoff.
- m05 B V1 is now "Nobody looks twice once a badge is cleared;" (was "Nobody checks the one who's always here;"). Confirm it sings.
- Two Patricias are canon: Wells in m01 and Morgan in m05. Both C songs name "Patricia", so a listener playing the season may think they are one person. m05 C could say "the security chief" if that matters.

**m01 First Contact**
- A quotes the chalkboard hint "think election year" word for word. It is the clue, not the answer, but you could make it vaguer.
- Neither song touches the recruit ending.
- C: "Nobody here knows my face" is the agent's side of B's "the auditor that no one saw", so the two rest on the same irony. "Notes that end in an equals sign" is close enough to the encoded notes to read as a clue. C has no drop-site terminal, flags or lizard sign-off from HaX.

**m02 Ransomed Trust**
- B's "I only switched the screen off in your hand" gives Ghost's act to the insider (kept as complicity, but it could read as Reeves overclaiming).
- B's "I knew which drawer it slept in" implies Reeves knew where the offline keys were. The ink doesn't say so, and they are in a PIN safe.
- A's "fog" is invented weather. Canon only has rain.
- A runs to 378 words with choruses counted once, right at the limit.
- A's chorus "held the door" comes back in m08 A ("He'll hold the door"), which can be heard as a callback between the two insiders.
- C: "I won't pick you" is a resolve, and many runs do pick locks. "Not one reader left" ignores Gary's working reader.

**m03 Ghost in the Machine**
- Both versions have Sterling drawing the network diagram, when in the ink it is already on the whiteboard (poetic licence).
- A's "St. Catherine's was a rehearsal" paraphrases a line that only plays on the escape ending.
- C: "I said it too quickly" assumes the player took the forty thousand, and it anticipates Sterling's "You said it very quickly". Check that "HaX ticking them off as they come and go" (pre-chorus 2) sings.

**m04 Critical Failure**
- B's backstory (warnings "stamped later") is invented. It fits "former grid engineer" but no source states it.
- Both verse 2s still name the card clone and the print lift in passing.

**m05 Insider Trading**
- B's original Halloran couplet can't be recovered from the draft. A line naming her as the wrong suspect would clear her early.
- C: the auditor cover is shared with A ("an auditor's case"). It is the mission's premise, so it stays.
- Cross-season pass: A's bridge couplet ("ask the calm ones back at home…") is now the Recruiter's bid for the agent ("you're the one without a price… I never ask twice"). This supersedes the review's fallback line. Confirm you're happy for m05 to carry no hint of a SAFETYNET leak.

**m06 Follow the Money**
- B's "pulled the lanyard round my throat" can be heard as a noose image (kept as a deliberate dark double meaning).
- Cross-season pass: A's bridge now says "a doctor keeps the books… his hand is in the way you look" in place of "taught your teacher", leaving HaX's teacher to m07. Pass C also changed A's final line to "the one who's never caught" (see season-wide).
- C: "receipt" lands on a weak beat and needs a push in the delivery.

**m07 The Architect's Gambit**
- Both choruses say outright that the agent was the target. The Architect says so himself at T-1, but you could save it for the final chorus.
- Each version is about 389 words with repeat choruses counted, under 380 without them.
- Settled by the cross-season pass: B keeps its "let it hurt" paraphrase as the season's first use.
- C: the outro "and only our side knew" is a faint leak seed. Cut it if m07 should leave the leak to A's and B's unlatched windows.

**m08 The Mole**
- A still sings "ten-fifteen", the true door-in time and a wrong answer to the audit question. Cut it if you'd rather the song quoted no evidence values.
- Both songs lean on "calm", which the game treats as a fair but misleading clue.
- C: "the wrong one hangs" is a figure of speech (SAFETYNET has no arrest powers and nobody is executed). "The wrong one falls" is the alternative. Pass C made no m08 changes.
- Cross-season pass: A's outro (the only m08 change) is now "Every reader's burning red, love, / And somebody still walked through." in place of a near-copy of m07 B's bridge. Confirm.

**m09 Digital Archaeology**
- Settled by the cross-season pass: "four hands you shook" is now a question about all four ("Are all of them true?"), and neither song claims WREN for the Architect (she is the Initiative's placement, per FINALE_ARC.md). Recheck if m09's stage design moves the Recruiter's offer or the ledger.

**m10 Full Disclosure**
- Lyrics rely on stage 1 details that may change: the 03:45 countdown step ("a quarter to four"), Asante as the margin-note corrector (A's "they put right my sums", B's "a hand that I know"), the gallery's "one walkway, one window", the kit-check laugh staying Kowalczyk's beat (behind B's "swore" fix), and the sheriff's roadblock.
- Branch-dependent hints: "sterling for sale where the shutters are drawn" (Victoria is in the gallery only on some m03 branches), and "the one on your line" being his pupil, which assumes "she learned that from you" stays canon.
- C relies on stage 1 details that may change: "two vans on the gravel" (cold open) and the photo with "Do not detain" in the security office (review W28 calls it crowded). "You sat in his classroom" assumes every player learns before m10 that he taught HaX (FINALE_ARC rows 70 and 98). Check that "Whoever among us came here for the core" doesn't narrow the WREN field. It is meant to be true of the whole team.
- A's bridge "They locked it away in a drawer" is deliberate villain spin (he never sent the notice himself). Change it to "I" only if you want the song to admit the rebuttal.
