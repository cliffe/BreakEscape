## Version A (classic)

Eight long nights you have danced my floor,

[Pre-chorus]
The ghost on the handset is selling the dawn,

[Chorus]

[Bridge]

[Final chorus, up a key]

[Outro]
Afterwards, then.

### Notes

- Narrator is Dr Adrian Tesseract at the core, singing to Agent 0x00. "I have stopped the clock, so take your time" (held back for the final chorus, since he only stops it when 0x00 walks in) and "I'd rather it was you" are his arrival line. "Lay your hand on mine", "it was you" and "I chose you by design" are the Publication's need for a SAFETYNET hand on the key, and the Reading's prediction. "Prove my reading untrue" points to "Then I was wrong about one thing."
- Setting and route: the Cold War bunker in Montana, the blast door keyed to 0x00's name, the countdown ("a quarter to four", the 03:45 step as the player leaves the server hall, fifteen minutes before the cells' head start). Ghost sells the head start on the security-office handset, Victoria Sterling waits in the gallery behind the shutter ("sterling for sale"), and the power room cuts the lights. "Show you how it works" is the first step of the Join path.
- Season glance: "eight long nights you have danced my floor", with ransom (m02), ghosts (m03) and the mole (m08) in passing, is the m07 idea that he was measuring the player all along. The bridge's warning and "Albion's morning" are the m09 Albion papers and Critical Mass's m04 attack. "They locked it away" is his own spin: the papers show he drafted the vendor notice and never sent it, which is the mission's rebuttal.
- HaX: "the one on your line" was his pupil, and tonight her line goes to static in the gallery.
- The talk-down: the signed casualty sheet, corrected in red in the margin, and "I thanked them instead" (the systems lead's "I corrected his sums. He thanked me."), the mission's own rebuttal on the first claim.
- Hinted twists: "a wren at your shoulder who's sung here before" uses only the codename every player hears at the briefing, with no pronoun, and fits any of the suspects. "Mind where my other hand is" hints at the dead-man switch under the console. "Keep it, or send it, or burn the lot" are the three database choices, and "Afterwards, then" is his card on the console in Scorched Earth.

### Notes

- Narrator is Tesseract, waiting at the core: "Take your time. I'd rather it was you" and "My file says it's you" lift his core lines ("I've paused it. I'd rather it was you", "My file says it will be you"). "I left you a door... It knows you by name" is the invitation and the blast door keyed to 0x00. "Two times on a screen" is the 04:00 head start and 06:00 fallback on the countdown, and "the road's been closed off" is the sheriff's roadblock.
- The WREN twist is hinted without a name, pronoun or personal trait: someone "at your shoulder who swore like the rest" (every suspect took the same oath; "swore" also reads as cursing) "has propped a back door", the core's service door propped at 02:10, with a hacking double meaning. Players who don't find WREN watch that door open at the core.
- Verse 2 looks back over the season: the hospital bedside (m02), the city under a clock (m07's countdown), "you pressed it, or didn't, that first day" (m01's launch choice, one of his record lines), and "pinned to my wall" for the file of choices he argues from.
- The second pre-chorus gives away the talk-down's first rebuttal as a slip in his own voice: the casualty sheet he signed knowing it was low, corrected in the margin by his systems lead. "I'd sign it tonight" and "burn in the light" are his creed: he knew the figure was low and signed anyway, and the Publication is the fire.
- The bridge covers the gallery (HaX's line jammed, one walkway, slit windows, one door), the clock he stops, the database choice (secure, disclose, wipe both: "keep it, or give it, or burn it to the ground") and the dead-man switch under the console. "Don't make me wrong. Or do" nods to the Reading and his "Then I was wrong about one thing". The spoken outro is the card he leaves on the console in Scorched Earth.
- Hacking appears only as imagery: doors, a back door, a key, a map of weaknesses, a file.

## Review

- **Version A:** a proper brassy Bassey villain's song. "Full disclosure, darling" is the best hook of the two and the title sits naturally in it, with a narrator seducing the hero towards the key. One canon slip: the margin note was someone else correcting him, not his own invitation to be corrected. Score 7 → 8.
- **Version B:** tighter, more menacing, and richer in mission detail (roadblock, two marks on the countdown, propped service door, gallery jam, the Auden "stopped all the clocks"). The verse 2 look back over the season works well, and the final-chorus turn ("Don't make me wrong. Or do.") is the best line in either song. One whodunit giveaway, fixed below. Score 8 → 9.
- **Changes made:**
  - A, verse 2: "And I've signed it in ink with a note in the red: / If my sums are mistaken, then tell me instead." → "And I signed it in ink, though the margin ran red: / They put right my sums, and I thanked them instead." Canon: the correction is in another hand (stage 1 §6.1) and he signed the low figure after it. The old line made him the one inviting correction, which undercut the rebuttal.
  - A, final chorus: "I have stopped the clock, there's no more hurry," → "I have stopped the clock, so take your time," because "hurry" rhymed with nothing and the new line echoes his canon "Take your time". Per the coordinator I checked whether this line belongs in choruses 1 and 2 as well and left it out: pre-chorus 1 has the clock running at a quarter to four, and he stops it only when 0x00 reaches the core.
  - B, pre-chorus 1: "and one at your shoulder who laughs like the rest / has propped me a door." → "and one at your shoulder who swore like the rest / has propped a back door." **Whodunit fix.** Laughing with Arakawa at the kit check is listed in stage 1 §8.1 as *Kowalczyk's* own beat, and it happens at the cold open, the same kit check where she clones Malhotra's badge. An attentive player could single her out from the title song. "Swore" fits every suspect. "Propped me a door" also implied she propped it for Tesseract, but canon has her propping it for her own exit with the stolen copy, and he refuses her ("You're mine").
  - A's "a wren at your shoulder who's sung here before" was left as it is. It uses only the codename the briefing gives every player, with no pronoun. "At your shoulder" fits Quintero (placed beside the player in each room, and the deliberate red herring) better than Kowalczyk (a room ahead), so if anything it misdirects. "Sung here before" points no further than clue 2's tunnel pair. Neither version gives the sleeper away after the B edit.
- **Stronger version:** B, for denser and more exact mission references, a darker build that suits a finale, and a final chorus that turns the Reading into the closing hook. A has the more memorable chorus and would make the better single.
- **Left for the user:**
  - Stage 1 details the lyrics rely on, which may change: A's "quarter to four" (the proposed 03:45 countdown step); the margin-note corrector (Asante in stage 1) behind A's "they put right my sums" and B's "a hand that I know"; B's "one walkway, one window" (the gallery layout); B's "laughs/swore" fix assumes the kit-check laugh stays Kowalczyk's beat; the sheriff's roadblock in B verse 1.
  - Branch-dependent references, kept as passing hints: A's "sterling for sale where the shutters are drawn" (Victoria is in the gallery only on the recruited or escaped m03 branches, and sells the raid only if trusted blind; otherwise it is Vos); "the one on your line" being his pupil assumes "she learned that from you" stays canon.
  - A's bridge "They locked it away in a drawer" is deliberate villain spin (he never sent the notice himself). Change it to "I" only if you want the song to admit the rebuttal outright.

### Cross-season pass

- A, V2: "The voice in your ear, she was mine long ago, / I taught her to hurt only after the show;" → "The one on your line, she was mine long ago, / She'll be calling your name through the static below;". HaX's line is now only in m07 B and m08 B. "She was mine long ago" keeps the teacher claim, and the new line moves it forward to the finale's gallery jam. "The voice in your ear" was in m07 A, m10 A and m10 B, and B keeps it.
- A, Pre-chorus 1: "The clock on the wall reads a quarter to four," → "The numbers burn red at a quarter to four,". "The clock on the wall" was in three songs. m10's own clock image is the one he stops, kept in the final chorus.
- A, Final chorus: "Press the key or prove my file untrue." → "Press the key or prove my reading untrue.". B's hook is "My file says it's you". A now names the Reading and reads both ways.
- B, Pre-chorus 2: "She'll tell you: let it hurt afterwards, not during. / I say let it hurt now, and watch the hoarders burning." → "I signed it regardless; I'd sign it tonight. / At six all the hoarders will burn in the light." This removes the line's last full quote after m08. It keeps his creed and the casualty sheet, and fixes the loose during/burning rhyme.
- Kept: both outros, "Afterwards, then." It is the card he leaves in Scorched Earth (canon) and now the only trace of HaX's line in the finale, so it lands as an echo for anyone who played m07 and m08.
- WREN check: A's "a wren at your shoulder" and B's "one at your shoulder who swore like the rest" make no claim on whose she is, which fits FINALE_ARC (an Initiative placement whom he refuses at the key). No change.

### Notes

- Tradition: modern (*Skyfall* / *No Time to Die*), inside the agent's head on the way down through Tomb Gamma, with a classic lift for the final chorus. A and B make the title the Publication in Tesseract's mouth. C makes it the agent's own confession, spoken before the man who kept a file on them can read it out. Tesseract reads the player's record back from his ledger at the core, so "read it back to me" is dramatic irony.
- The finale's route: the forward post (gravel turnout, two vans, the fence; the pines are added colour), the security office photo among the guards' shift notes with "Do not detain" (the guards fight the player anyway), the five full scenes as faces "where I left them", the server hall schedule in plain words (infusion pumps, water, dispatch), and HaX on the line from the forward post.
- Verse 2, one line per mission, each true whatever the player chose: the m03 interview cover with Victoria, the exploit broker (every player); Albion's battery racks at 61°C while the SCADA screens show normal (m04, every player reads the dial); the forty-seven candidates on the Recruiter's list, which every player reads in m09's ledger whether or not the m05 list was found or sold; the fund frozen or followed (m06); the two dead agents whose names hang on Task Force Null's wall (m07, the same two for every player, whichever sites were left); Nightshade taking the database out (m08, true whatever his fate).
- What the agent doesn't know yet: "keep on talking me down" comes before the gallery jam. "The hand is mine" means "my own choices" to the agent, and to anyone who has reached the core it is the SAFETYNET hand he needs on the key. "He's waiting for me" is the invitation and the photo, which every player has. "I'm afraid that you're right" points to the offer and Join without naming either.
- WREN: "one of us lies" and "whoever among us is keeping a score" are what every player learns from the m09 ledger and the briefing. No pronoun, role, kit or position, and the PIN cracker is left out.

## Review (C)

- **Version C:** a confessional third voice that suits a finale. The agent sings into the argument Tesseract is about to make, and "full disclosure, and the hand is mine" is a strong hook: the title sits naturally and the double meaning hints at the core without saying it. The draft had one canon error (a turbine hall at a battery plant), two lines that assumed one outcome, and two images borrowed from B. Score 7 → 8.
- **Changes made:**
  - V1: "\"Do not detain\", like a card at a place." → "\"Do not detain\", and the guards give chase." The place-card image repeats B's hook, "I kept a place for you". The new line is the canon irony (guards told not to detain, fighting anyway).
  - Pre-chorus 1: "one I saved, one I bargained, and one I let go," → "each one where I left them, a long time ago,". Not every player saved, bargained with or let go of anyone in particular (protected or unprotected Gary, Victoria arrested, Voltage captured). "Where I left them" fits every branch, and the five slots are filled by branch anyway.
  - V2 (m03): "I've smiled at a broker and bought what she sold," → "I've smiled at a broker and lied as I was told,". This drops a trade image close to A's "sterling for sale" and puts the m03 cover story in instead, which every player sat through.
  - V2 (m04): "I've stood in a turbine hall gone cold," → "I've stood by the racks while the screens said cold,". Albion is a battery storage plant (Battery Halls 1 and 2, rack banks A–C) with no turbines, and its halls ran hot. The fix keeps the rhyme and now carries the mission's central lie: the SCADA screens said 28°C while the racks were at 61°C.
  - V2 (m05): "I've walked forty-seven names out on my own," → "I've read forty-seven names that a headhunter owned,". SAFETYNET warns the forty-seven only on `found_pipeline_list and not recruiter_deal_accepted` (FINALE_ARC row 62). Every player reads them in m09's ledger (row 63), and "headhunter" is TalentStack without a pronoun.
  - V2 (m08): "and one of our own sold the map that we'd made." → "and one of our own walked our secrets out the gate." "The map" is B's final-chorus image ("at six they read the map"). The new line is true whatever Nightshade's fate.
  - Bridge: "He wrote that I'd press it. He's been right before." → "He's waiting for me. He's been right before." The Reading's prediction is known only on `m09_reading_finished`, so the old line gave some agents more than they know. Every player has the invitation and the photo.
- **Checked, no change:** the agent's voice in every line; no name, pronoun or body detail ("my face" fixes nothing); no banned words, "darling", clock on the wall, candles or "let it hurt afterwards". WREN lines don't narrow the field. On `m09_sleeper_named` they still read as the briefing's "there may be others". "The hand is mine" hints and doesn't spoil: the key is named only as something the agent fears, and the SAFETYNET-hand motive stays unsaid. "Signed" mirrors A's and B's casualty sheet with the agent's own signature. That is an echo, not reuse, because the image (a confession) and the signer differ, and the contrast only works if the word recurs.
- **Stronger version:** B is still the strongest of the three for mission density and its final turn. C is the best fit for the player's arc and the only one that gives the agent the last word before the core.
- **Left for the user:**
  - Lines that depend on stage 1–2 docs, which have round 1 reviews but no applied revisions: "two vans on the gravel" (the cold open, stage 1 §cold open) and the photo with "Do not detain" (stage 1 security office, which review W28 flags as crowded, so it could be trimmed). The schedule entries, HaX at the forward post and the two dead agents come from FINALE_ARC and are stable.
  - "Signed" in all three songs: keep as an echo, or change C's chorus if you want no shared word with A and B.
  - "You sat in his classroom" assumes the m09 reveal that he taught HaX reaches every player before m10, which it does in FINALE_ARC row 70/98, including the cold-start briefing.

### Cross-season pass (C)

- Chorus: "here's the whole of it, signed:" → "here's the whole of it, plain:" (both choruses), and final chorus "here's the last of it, signed:" → "…plain:". A ("I signed it in ink") and B ("I signed it regardless") both give the signature to Tesseract's casualty sheet, so all three m10 songs used it. "Plain" is plain speaking, the root of full disclosure, and it keeps the slant rhyme with "behind" and "mine". This settles the review's "signed" item.
- Bridge: "Whoever among us is keeping a score," → "Whoever among us came here for the core,". m05 C has "one is keeping score" for its insider, so the line was a direct lift from one hidden-insider song to the next. The new line is true of the whole team, and for the sleeper it means the copy she takes at the core, with no pronoun, role or kit. It also rhymes with "yours" and "before".
