# Critical Failure: Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
I used to keep this city breathing,
I knew the hum of every line.
I sent my warnings up the ladder;
they stamped them "later", every time.
So I came back in borrowed colours,
a firm that never sent a bill,
three nights alone out on the floodplain
where the racks lie warm and still.

[Pre-chorus]
The screen reads twenty-eight and smiles,
I taught it how to lie.
But the needle on the far wall's counting,
and so am I.

[Chorus]
This is a critical failure,
a slow heat under the skin,
one cell whispers to the next one
till the whole hall lets it in.
You built it on hope and gaffer tape,
I only came to show the seam.
Call it a critical failure.
Call it waking from the dream.

[Verse 2]
Now you come in on a stranger's badge,
four hours early through the frost.
You borrow a card from an old man's lanyard,
a print from a panel he touched and forgot.
Nine in hi-vis on my cameras
walking out into the cold.
How humane. So you've done your sums too.
Let's see what your sums hold.

[Pre-chorus]
The phone in your pocket says hurry,
a voice that's never been afraid.
I know the number to one decimal place,
and I came here anyway.

[Chorus]
This is a critical failure,
a slow heat under the skin,
one cell whispers to the next one
till the whole hall lets it in.
You built it on hope and gaffer tape,
I only came to show the seam.
Call it a critical failure.
Call it waking from the dream.

[Bridge]
There's a red head on the wall behind me,
wired straight, no road to it but hands,
and here's my hand above the keyboard.
You can't reach both. You understand.
So choose: the button, or me.
Save your eleven, lose the thread,
or take me in and learn the cost
of every name I've left unsaid.

[Final chorus, lift]
Maybe I'm the critical failure,
the one cell that ran too hot,
the warning nobody answered
grown into the thing it fought.
Pull me out, or pull the power,
either way the lights grow dim.
Call it a critical failure.
Call it eight o'clock again.

[Outro]
The needle's climbing.
Ja.
I know.

## Notes
- Sung from inside Voltage's head: the former grid engineer whose ignored warnings ("stamped them later") turned him, matching his ink line "We didn't make it fragile. We just stopped pretending it wasn't." "Borrowed colours, a firm that never sent a bill" is the OptiGrid cover; "three nights on the floodplain" is his "shed on a floodplain" and HaX's "three days to get ready".
- Pre-chorus 1 is the mission's core puzzle as imagery: the SCADA screen reading 28 while the analogue dial on the Rack Bank C wall reads 61. "I taught it how to lie" nods to the faked temperature sensors. Pre-chorus 2 quotes his casualty-model line ("I know it to one decimal place") and puts Agent HaX on the phone.
- Chorus: "one cell whispers to the next" is thermal runaway rack to rack, with "cell" also meaning the ENTROPY cell. "Hope and gaffer tape" is Vance's own phrase from the debrief, turned against him by the villain.
- Verse 2 runs the player's route in passing: the regulator cover arriving four hours early, the cloner on Vance's lanyard, the print lifted off the duty round panel for the HV fingerprint reader, and the optional evacuation (Voltage's "Nine people in hi-vis on my cameras... Very humane").
- The bridge foreshadows the plant-room choice without spelling it out: the hardwired red mushroom ESD versus Voltage at the trigger laptop ("The button, or me"). "Save your eleven, lose the thread" hints that pressing the button lets him walk, taking the route to The Architect with him.
- The final chorus turns the title on the narrator: the critical failure might be Voltage himself. The trailing "Ja" echoes his one verbal tic in the ink.
