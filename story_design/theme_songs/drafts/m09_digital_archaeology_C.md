# Digital Archaeology: Version C (agent)

*A blend. Modern verses, low and dry, on a slow 4/4 with brushed snare and a single guitar; the classic tradition comes in at the bridge, when the strings and brass arrive, and the final chorus goes up a key. Sung by Agent 0x00, alone in the building, with HaX in one ear.*

[Verse 1]
Three days of leave, and they let me have one;
then a sheet they took off a courier's run
said *burn it all before the Publication*,
a book nobody's read, and it isn't done.
So it's picks at a dock in the Helena dark,
past a front desk where the trolleys park;
a boy with a torch walks the fence alone.
She says he's nobody's. I let him go home.

[Pre-chorus]
"One of ours, once," is all they'll say,
no name till a court can hear;
and the last of ours we trusted with the keys
had them fifteen years.

[Chorus]
It's only digital archaeology,
the dead, the dark, and me;
I'm turning the ground of the life he left,
and he can't turn round to see.
So I keep my lamp low and I keep to his track,
and the dead don't dig back.

[Verse 2]
Paper comes down like a wedding's end
round a careful clerk with a rubber stamp;
she inks *destroyed* on each life in a box,
and I lift her thumb from it, still damp.
And back in the racks where the tapes run cold,
one machine's as warm as a hand;
of all of the things they were told to burn,
they forgot the one place I'd stand.

[Pre-chorus]
"Same classroom," she wrote. "Don't ask me whose."
So I don't. I let it lie.
If HaX had a name, she'd have handed it over;
she was never the kind to hide.

[Chorus]
It's only digital archaeology,
the dead, the dark, and me;
I'm turning the ground of the life he left,
and he can't turn round to see.
So I keep my lamp low and I keep to his track,
and the dead don't dig back.

[Bridge]
There are two cards at the end of the wall,
Fraser, Ferreira, no outcome at all;
nobody read them out loud today,
so I've said them the whole long way.
I came in keen, and I've been sold since,
and I've stopped needing a name:
whoever's behind that red-ringed "A.",
I'm digging all the same.

[Final chorus, up a key]
It's only digital archaeology,
the dead in their rows, asleep;
I'm turning the ground of the life he left,
and nobody's reading me.
I'm the one with the lamp, I'm the one on the track,
and the dead don't dig back.
No. The dead don't dig back.

[Outro]
Three days of leave. I got one.
I'll sleep when the digging's done.

## Notes

- **Tradition and voice:** a blend. The verses are inside the agent's head (modern), and the bridge and the lifted final chorus open out into the classic sweep. By m09 the agent is past being betrayed and is hunting: tired, dry, and set on the job ("I came in keen, and I've been sold since"). There's no name or pronoun for the agent and no body detail beyond a hand and a thumbprint.
- **Story references:** the leave cut from three days to one; the intercepted order ("Burn Helena before the Publication", which nobody can explain yet: "a book nobody's read, and it isn't done"); lockpicks at the loading dock; Noor's trolleys at the front desk; Old Coyote on his round ("not ours, not theirs, don't hurt him" becomes "she says he's nobody's"); the shredder's cross-cut confetti ("a wedding's end"); Sorensen stamping boxes *destroyed* and the print lifted off her stamp; the tape room's cold racks and Tomb Beta's server, warm among them; the two names on the wall's last cards, which nobody reads at the briefing; the circled "-- A." sign-off; Netherton's "a name I can say in court"; HaX's "Same classroom… don't ask me whose" text from m08; Nightshade as "the last of ours" who held the keys for fifteen years.
- **Dramatic irony (the agent sees none of it):** "he can't turn round to see", "nobody's reading me" and the hook "the dead don't dig back" are confident lines to the agent. Anyone who has played the mission knows the Architect wrote a file on 0x00 (the Reading) and that Sorensen keeps it. "They forgot the one place I'd stand" reads to the agent as a lucky oversight, and to a player as the server left off the purge on purpose for the agent to reach. "She was never the kind to hide" is the agent's trust in HaX the night she confesses she sat on the name.
- **Kept out on purpose:** WREN, the raid team and Kowalczyk's cracker are absent (the agent doesn't know there is a sleeper, and the cracker is m10's clue). No name for the Architect, and no flags, Field Guide or technical detail. The title is the first line of every chorus.
- **Avoiding A and B:** no sodium light, grain store, dust or brushing, bones, "dig me up", tape-to-snow, shelves, cage or mesh, morning hour or presses; the narrator is the hunter, not the buried man. No season repeats (no "darling", no clock, no "let it hurt afterwards", no flags as candles, no doors left open).
