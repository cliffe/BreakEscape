# First Contact: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
I signed the book in a stranger's hand
and folded my own name small.
Sarah gave me a key and a pass to wear
and pointed me down the east hall.
Kevin worries for a living, he says,
and the old locks give if you're kind,
then he gave me the kit that he keeps for the ones
who leave their own keys behind.

[Pre-chorus]
HaX on the line says, slow, slow,
write it down before you move.
So I learn the floor like a tightrope wire
in somebody else's shoes.

[Chorus]
First contact.
Nobody here knows my face.
I'm the hum in the lights, I'm the ink in the book,
I'm a guest who'll be gone without trace.
Write it down, HaX: first contact.
Day one, and I'm in.

[Verse 2]
Maya jumps every time the door goes.
She's been waiting for weeks for a knock.
Patricia's chair still faces the hall,
her questions shut up in a lock.
I read notes that end in an equals sign
like a sum someone left undone,
and a list of two million sleeping names
with a mark by the weakest ones.

[Pre-chorus]
HaX on the line says, slow, slow,
write it down before you move.
But the logs keep signing a kind man's name,
and I've only got paper for proof.

[Chorus]
First contact.
Nobody here knows my face.
I'm the hum in the lights, I'm the ink in the book,
I'm a guest who'll be gone without trace.
Write it down, HaX: first contact.
Day one, and I'm in.

[Bridge]
He said come back when I'd found something real,
and he never once lifted his head.
Now the cleaners have gone and his door's still lit
at the end of a hall gone dead.
And the last lock left in the building's a man
with his hand on what I can't see.
Whatever I choose when I open that door
is the agent I'm going to be.

[Final chorus, up a key]
First contact.
Let him look up and learn my face.
I was the hum in the lights, I was ink in the book,
now I'm the one thing he couldn't trace.
Write it down, HaX: first contact.
Day one, and I'm going in.

[Outro]
Signed in this morning.
Not signed out.

## Notes
- Tradition: a blend. The verses are modern and close-miked, a keen rookie's inner voice on the first day in the field. The chorus is built like a classic Bond hook: the title as a hit, then a short tag ("Day one, and I'm in") for a held note. The final chorus moves from hiding to stepping forward.
- Verse 1 is the arrival: the visitor sign-in log, Sarah's badge and main-office key, directions to the IT room on the east side, and Kevin the "professional worrier" with the lockpick set he bought for people who lock themselves out. "The old locks give if you're kind" is his line about the older locks. Pre-chorus 1 is HaX's briefing advice ("gather intelligence and evidence before making any moves", "document everything") and the IT-contractor cover.
- Verse 2: Maya startling at the door and "waiting for weeks"; Patricia's empty office, with her notes locked in the safe; the encoded notes (imagery of a trailing equals sign only); and the target database of two million people profiled for vulnerability.
- Dramatic irony 1: "Nobody here knows my face" and "he never once lifted his head" are what the agent believes. A player knows Derek's opening line in the confrontation ("I've been watching you... The lockpicking"). "Let him look up and learn my face" and "the one thing he couldn't trace" turn that confidence round for anyone who has played.
- Dramatic irony 2: "the logs keep signing a kind man's name, / and I've only got paper for proof" is a rookie's unease about Kevin. Players know about Derek's contingency plan, which uses fake logs and forged emails to frame him. Kevin is never named in that line, and the frame-up is never stated.
- The bridge is Derek's brush-off ("come back when you've actually found something"), his late working hours, the launch device the agent hasn't seen yet, and the open moral choice (fight, arrest, recruit, expose, accept surrender), sung as the agent finding out who they will be. That is m01's place in the season. The outro takes the sign-in book from verse 1 and adds the risk.
- Kept clear of A and B: no glass tower, mural, lanyard, pins, keypad numbers, clock, Sunday launch time, casualty figures, briefcase, chalkboard, voice in the pocket, server glow, verification creed or Architect. No darling, no flags as candles.
