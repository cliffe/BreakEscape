# Follow the Money: Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
He took a legend's name and called himself the Second,
He keeps a ghost for signing when the debts are reckoned.
Hospital ransoms pooling like rain on marble floors,
Washed white and paid out quietly through other doors.

[Pre-chorus]
You walk in with a lanyard and a regulator's smile,
The paper's good, the paper's gold, the paper lasts a while,
But one call to the FCA and all of it is gone,
Keep them talking, darling, keep your story on.

[Chorus]
Follow the money,
Down where the clean coins drown,
Through the mixer, through the dark, through every wallet in town.
Follow the money,
Every hop a hand that lied,
Follow the money, honey, and find who's on the other side.

[Verse 2]
She built a lock of lattices to keep the people free,
Now blood runs through the ledgers of her perfect privacy.
Her badge hangs at her collar, close enough to steal,
Lay the costed dead before her, and see if she will deal.
Below, a cursor's breathing in a room of humming light,
And every flag you carry home's a candle in the night.

[Pre-chorus]
Twelve point eight million, signed and set to go,
The wallets have gone quiet, and the quiet's how you know.
Three days on the clock and a smile across the desk,
He'll pour you something golden while you read the rest.

[Chorus]
Follow the money,
Down where the clean coins drown,
Through the mixer, through the dark, through every wallet in town.
Follow the money,
Every hop a hand that lied,
Follow the money, honey, and find who's on the other side.

[Bridge]
Freeze it, he's a martyr on every screen,
Watch it, and it carries on, the cleanest dirt you've seen.
He'll tell you either road you take, the turning was his own,
But a fortune leaves a footprint, and the footprints lead back home.
For somewhere past the final hop a teacher keeps the books,
He calls the dead his lessons, and he taught you how to look.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Follow the money,
Down to the cold where the keys lie still,
Every coin remembers, every ghost has left a will.
Follow the money,
Every vault a heart that's bought,
Follow the money, honey, all the way to the one who taught.

[Outro]
Money talks.
Money leaks.
Follow the money...

## Notes
- Verse 1 is Satoshi Nakamoto II, "the Second" of a borrowed name, who signs The Architect's Fund as "Satoshi's Ghost". The pooled hospital ransoms are Ransomware Incorporated's $2.4 million, and "other doors" is the payout to the Insider Threat Initiative through TalentStack.
- Pre-chorus 1 is the FCA supervisory-visit cover from HaX's briefing: the paperwork gets you past reception but won't survive a phone call to the FCA. "Clean coins drown" and "through the mixer" in the chorus stand for the Bitcoin-to-Monero mixer and the cold wallets, kept as imagery.
- Verse 2 is Dr Irina Volkova: her lattice-based cryptography, the lanyard "within a cloner's range" for the RFID cloner, and the turn-or-detain choice, where you show her the casualty projection on the fund allocation. The cursor in the humming room is the server-room terminal, and the flags go back to the SAFETYNET drop-site.
- Pre-chorus 2 is the $12.8 million fund, the wallets going quiet before a payout, and the three-day clock. In the bridge, "Freeze it... watch it" is the asset choice, along with Satoshi's claim that either one serves his purpose (martyr or a year of surveillance). The cold storage recovery keys turn up in the final chorus as "the cold where the keys lie still".
- Twist hint: Satoshi's private Identity File names a former SAFETYNET strategist who mentored agents as the likely Architect. "The footprints lead back home", "a teacher keeps the books / he taught you how to look" and "the one who taught" point at this without naming him. "He calls the dead his lessons" echoes Satoshi's line that The Architect calls the casualties lessons.
- The outro twists Nightshade's briefing line, "Money's just another protocol, and this one leaks".
