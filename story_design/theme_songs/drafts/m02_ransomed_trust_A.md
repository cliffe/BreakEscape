# Ransomed Trust: Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
Two forty-seven and the screens have all gone red,
the wards are breathing on a diesel heart instead.
You never heard me coming, darling, I was always near,
I've had my ear against your walls since the fire bell rang clear.
Seven letters on your table, seven times you looked away,
you bought yourselves a shining eye and let the old door stay.
So I've written you an invoice, signed it "yours sincerely, Ghost",
with a price for every hour, love, and the hours that cost the most.

[Pre-chorus]
Who holds the keys tonight?
Not the card upon your chest.
Every door is just a question
of whose hand you trusted best.

[Chorus]
Ransomed trust!
Seven warnings left to gather dust.
You can pay me in the dark or bleed out in the light,
but somebody held the door for me tonight.
Ransomed trust,
every lock you never turned has gone to rust.

[Verse 2]
You came in from the rain with your name upon the log
and a badge that opens nothing in the diesel and the fog.
You sweet-talked Bernadette for keys, you won a weary man his card,
then you turned my own back door on me and caught me off my guard.
Four seconds on your network and your handler didn't know,
I could stay for four more hours, there is nowhere I can't go.
I'll lay my keys before you, darling, free as summer rain,
but my keys will turn my doors, love, and I'll walk back in again.

[Pre-chorus]
Who holds the keys tonight?
Not the one who signs the cheque.
Every hand that keeps you safe, love,
wears a lanyard round its neck.

[Chorus, repeat]

[Bridge]
So drag me to the dock and I will stand and tell it all,
I showed my working, line by line, in chalk upon the wall.
And the nine who signed it off by day, with minutes and a pen,
will watch me from the gallery and never stand again.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Ransomed trust!
Seven warnings left to gather dust.
Will you pay me in the dark or burn it in the light?
There's a kind face by the telephone tonight.
Ransomed trust,
ransomed trust,
and the locks have gone to rust.

[Outro, spoken over the last chord]
Forty feet away, a machine breathes for her.
Count them, darling.

## Notes
- The opening reprises the mission's own facts: the 02:47 encryption, the 47 patients on generators, the six-week listening device planted "during a fire drill" (the "fire bell" line), Gary Whitlock's seven ignored emails, and the £3.2m scanner bought instead of the £85k fix ("a shining eye", "let the old door stay").
- "signed it 'yours sincerely, Ghost'" and "the hours that cost the most" come from Ransomware Incorporated's business-invoice ransom note and from Ghost pricing the ransom against deaths per hour.
- Verse 2 follows the player's route: the visitor log, the badge that opens nothing (the access control server is encrypted), Bernadette's override keys, Gary's isolated server-room card, turning the ProFTPD backdoor on its owner ("my own back door", kept as imagery), Ghost's four-second capability demonstration ("your handler didn't know"), and Ghost's offer of free keys with "my keys, my doors" in the small print.
- The chorus line "pay me in the dark or bleed out in the light" covers the moral choices: pay quietly, refuse and recover by hand, or expose the hospital publicly. The final chorus turns it into a question put to the listener.
- Insider twist, hinted only: "somebody held the door for me", "whose hand you trusted best", "wears a lanyard round its neck" (Reeves's hand goes to his lanyard when he's unmasked) and "a kind face by the telephone" (his comms watch and the eleven-word phone call that burns the agent's cover). Nothing names him or his job.
- The bridge is Ghost's wish for a trial: "I showed my working", the "nine people who deferred it in writing" sitting in the gallery, and the "patients are their chalkboard" line from the briefing. The outro returns to the woman on ECMO, "forty feet" from the agent (the line the player can throw at Ghost).
