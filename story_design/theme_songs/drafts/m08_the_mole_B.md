# The Mole: Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
Every badge light in the building's burning red,
and no one meets the eyes across the desk.
I keep my hands flat on the bench, my coffee warm;
a quiet man's the one they never test.
The little box that cracks a keypad sleeps beside my lamp.
I chose which doors you'd open the long way.
Forty-seven minutes with a plan nobody signed,
and a cursor that remembers where I stayed.

[Pre-chorus]
You'll come down to my lab tonight
and ask me where I've been.
I'll give you nothing but the truth
and leave out what it means.

[Chorus]
So who is the mole?
Go ask the one who taught you how to ask.
Fifteen years I kept the candle cupped
and wore my calm the way you'd wear a mask.
Who is the mole?
Every lesson that I gave you was sound.
I never said where I had learned it.
I learned it underground.

[Verse 2]
Four fires in the dark and one bucket to throw;
I did the sum of who, and didn't look away.
At the wake I passed a tissue to the one who cried the most
and let her grieve the friends I gave away.
While you chose which fire to fight, the vault walked out the door,
the one I left unlatched and didn't mention.
The storm was always going to win. I only helped the wind,
and called it honesty, and called it vision.

[Pre-chorus]
A door can tell you who came in,
not what he did inside.
I passed that little light each dawn
and never dreamed it testified.

[Chorus]
So who is the mole?
Go ask the one who taught you how to ask.
Fifteen years I kept the candle cupped
and wore my calm the way you'd wear a mask.
Who is the mole?
Every lesson that I gave you was sound.
I never said where I had learned it.
I learned it underground.

[Bridge]
I wiped the stick and left my thumb.
Fifteen years, and I forgot my thumb.
There's a passport in the lining and a flight I never took;
a man who runs confesses, so I stayed to watch you look.
Now choose: a cell, or a leash.
Keep me as your ghost inside their machine
for just as long as it suits me.
I'll give you a bunker in the snow
and the man who dug me in.

[Final chorus, lift]
So who is the mole?
You finally asked the one who taught you how.
Fifteen years I kept the candle cupped.
Look, the wind is in the room with us now.
Who is the mole?
Every lesson that I gave you was sound,
and here's the last of them for free:
some things grow underground.

[Outro]
Let it hurt afterwards,
not during.
Close the door gently.
The whole floor's listening.

## Notes
- The narrator is the mole, never named. "Go ask the one who taught you how to ask" and "Every lesson that I gave you was sound" hint that the traitor is the player's old teacher (Nightshade taught the insider-threat briefing, and Netherton told 0x00 to learn mole-catching from him) without saying who. A listener can hear the line as a dare or as a confession.
- Verse 1: ATHENA's Level Red opening (every badge reader blinking red, nobody looking at the next desk), Nightshade's calm hands flat on the table, the PIN cracker kept on his bench since St. Catherine's ("I chose what you went in without"), and the forty-seven minutes on the Portland plan from the lab terminal, sung as a cursor rather than a log.
- Candle and wind come from his confession ("Order is a candle in a hurricane... I helped the wind"). "Four fires, one bucket" and "the sum of who" are his Portland arithmetic. The tissue at the wake is HaX's line about the debrief. The vault going out an unlatched door is the threat-database theft hidden behind the four attacks, kept as imagery.
- Pre-chorus 2 is the door audit that catches him: "I walked past that reader every morning... never once thought of it as a witness", and "A door says who walked in. It doesn't say what anyone did at the bench."
- Bridge: the fingerprint on the USB stick ("forgot my thumb"), the go-bag with a passport and the Montana flight he never took, the moral choice (police cell or triple-agent leash), his "ghost inside their machine" warning, and Tomb Gamma, hinted only as a bunker in the snow along with the Architect who placed him.
- The outro takes his parting line to 0x00 and the line he gives after an accusation made too early; the second doubles as the tip-off mechanic, since the floor hears everything.
