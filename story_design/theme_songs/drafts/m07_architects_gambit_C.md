# The Architect's Gambit: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
Rain on the interstate, forty miles of it,
and the fires read out like a weather report.
Everything I carry was cut for a door:
a pin, a badge, a print on the glass.
The handbook has pages on sending the force
and none on the people you pass.

[Pre-chorus]
I've stood in a room with the power failing;
nobody asked me who goes without.
Now I say one word and an aircraft turns,
and two lights stay red when the word goes out.

[Chorus]
If this is the Architect's gambit,
I'm the piece he never played,
come in off the rain on an empty road,
not bought and not sold and not paid.
Three states on a wire through the dark of December:
I'll hold the part I can reach
and carry the rest in place of sleep.

[Verse 2]
A guard with a torch and a debt he can't give back,
a woman with the dark held in her teeth,
a man at the console so sure of his sum
he'd hand it to me like a gift, and believe.
Everyone here was sold something smaller
than the thing that they hold in their hands.
I go through their doors on a copy of a card,
and nobody knows where I stand.

[Pre-chorus]
He says he has read what they wrote of me,
the shape that the file needs me in.
He asks how I chose, and I tell him: the figures,
the best our side has ever been given.

[Chorus]
If this is the Architect's gambit,
I'm the piece he never played,
come in off the rain on an empty road,
not bought and not sold and not paid.

[Bridge]
Somewhere I'm not, there's a ward with its lists torn up,
a count that nobody will trust,
a signed little sleeper inside someone's update
waiting ninety days out in the dust.
I sent what I had where I swore it was needed.
I'd send it again. I think. I don't know.

[Final chorus, up a key]
If this is the Architect's gambit,
let me be the piece he never played,
come in off the rain on an empty road,
not bought and not sold and not paid.
I'll hold every street I can reach
and carry the rest in place of sleep.

[Outro]
Forty minutes of rain.

## Notes
- **Tradition:** modern (the *No Time to Die* / *Skyfall* line), sung low and close from the driver's seat, with a lift only in the final chorus. The second chorus is cut to its first four lines. At m07 the agent has six missions behind them ("I've stood in a room with the power failing" nods to the infrastructure jobs) and has never had to pick who goes without. The voice is tested and still trusts its own side. Nobody has betrayed the agent yet.
- **Verse 1 and pre-chorus 1** are the briefing and the delegation: the I-5 in the rain, forty minutes out, with Netherton reading the three operations "flat" (his own word in the comparison) like a weather report. The go-bag is picks, cloner and print kit, each made for a door and none of them for a choice. The handbook's eleven pages on delegation of force, with nothing on "which lot of people to leave", become the last couplet. "An aircraft turns" and "two stay red" are the commit ("Say the word and their aircraft turns", "The other two stay red"). The song never says which city the agent picks, so it fits every run.
- **Verse 2** walks the building through the agent's eyes. Hollis has his torch and a payment he "can't give back". Elena works with the flashlight in her teeth. Mercer would hand over his casualty sum and stand by it. Each of them was sold a smaller story than the one they ended up holding (a badge renewal, a six-hour outage, "a building, empty"). The cloned zone badge is "a copy of a card".
- **Dramatic irony, no twist stated.** The agent sings "the piece he never played", "not bought and not sold", "nobody knows where I stand" and believes them. A player knows the intercept named 0x00 for Portland before the deployment order: the agent was the piece he played. The figures were "the best our side has ever been given", and given is exactly the problem, because the Architect let SAFETYNET capture every number. The bridge's "waiting ninety days" is the brief's figure, which the agent takes at its word. Anyone who read the Austin row knows it was nine. Nothing points at a leak at home or at anyone inside SAFETYNET (mole_seeding_plan.md hard rule).
- **Pre-chorus 2** is the Architect's first call heard from the agent's side ("I've read your file… a particular shape"; "the shape that the file needs me in"). His handset tricks and his questions from A and B are left out. The bridge lists the three fates with no city names (Meltdown's cancelled theatre lists, Fracture's vote nobody trusts, Trojan Horse's dormant signed update), because the agent doesn't yet know which two will come true. "The part I can reach" in the chorus is Netherton's send-off ("Go and do the part you can reach"), and the outro goes back to the forty-minute drive.
- **Kept clear of A and B and the season list:** no queen, board, bait or prize; no handset lighting up without a ring, no trace or call log; no "number or stomach"; no unlatched window or taught-your-teacher; no eight million lights, no candles, no wall clock, no endearments; no "let it hurt afterwards"; no flags. "Borrowed name", "stranger", "one move" and "ledger" are avoided because earlier songs use them. There is no technical detail beyond doors, a badge and a copied card.
