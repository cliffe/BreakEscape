# Follow the Money: Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
I built a dark room for the money to hide,
it goes in burning and it comes out snow.
I signed off every flag that came inside
and never asked the names of where they'd go.
One night I wrote a line I never quote,
*what have I become?* Then pulled the lanyard round my throat.

[Pre-chorus]
You walk in on borrowed paper,
asking for machines and not the files.
I know you're no one's regulator.
Don't tell me yet. Stay a while.

[Chorus]
Follow the money, follow it down,
past every lock I swore I'd keep,
through all the hands that never made a sound
to the last cold slot where the balances sleep.
Follow the money, I won't stand in your way.
They put the dead in a cell and moved on.
Follow the money home to me,
I built the dark it's running from.

[Verse 2]
Upstairs a ghost has framed a prophet's page
and signs the fund in someone else's name.
He says the world will fall, so set the date
and choose who's standing nearest to the flame.
The hospital ransoms came in through my door.
I called it privacy. I can't say that any more.

[Pre-chorus]
The clock says seventy-two,
and you lay the page across my desk.
I could unclip this and hand it through.
Don't ask me yet. Don't ask.

[Chorus]
Follow the money, follow it down
to the last cold slot where the balances sleep.
Follow the money, I won't stand in your way.
Twelve million reasons and a sum.
Follow the money home to me,
I built the dark it's running from.

[Bridge]
I built my locks on lattices against the day
the curves give way and every wallet's read.
I never thought the thing to break would be the way
I saw what I had made and turned my head.
And the one who wrote the clock for every cell
learned his patience in a house you know too well.

[Final chorus, up a step]
Follow the money, follow it down,
I'll open every door I swore I'd keep.
They put the dead in a cell and moved on.
Follow the money home to me,
I built the dark. I'll show you where it's gone.

[Outro]
Freeze it, or watch it run.
He smiles either way.
What have I become?

## Notes
- Narrator is Dr Irina Volkova, HashChain's CTO. "A dark room for the money... in burning, out snow" is her mixer, which washes Bitcoin through Monero into cold wallets. "Signed off every flag" is the transaction review she does personally. "What have I become?" is the last line of her research note on her workstation, and the lanyard is the office badge she has worn for four years.
- "I could unclip this and hand it through" is that badge, which she lends to the player (or the player clones it off her lanyard). "Borrowed paper", "asking for machines and not the files", "no one's regulator" and "Don't tell me yet" follow her suspicion of the fake FCA supervisor almost word for word.
- "The last cold slot where the balances sleep", "twelve million", "the clock says seventy-two" and "they put the dead in a cell" cover the Architect's Fund custody slot, the $12.8M pre-signed release, the 72-hour payout and the casualty projection. "Cell" means both the spreadsheet cell and an ENTROPY cell, and comes from her line "They put a number in a cell and moved on."
- Verse 2 opens on Satoshi Nakamoto II: the whitepaper framed behind his desk and the fund signed as Satoshi's Ghost, then his "the system's going to fall anyway; I'd rather choose who pays". "I called it privacy" is her "I told myself it was about privacy". "Hospital ransoms" is the RansomInc money from m02. The outro's "freeze it, or watch it run / he smiles either way" is the seize-or-monitor choice and his "either choice serves our purpose".
- The bridge opens with her post-quantum lattice research. Its last two lines hint at the twist: Satoshi's safe names Dr Adrian Tesseract, SAFETYNET's former strategist and Agent HaX's teacher, as the likely Architect. The song doesn't name him.
- The final chorus turns from her guilt to her choosing to help ("I'll open every door"), which leaves room for the player to recruit her, though the song doesn't say she was turned.
