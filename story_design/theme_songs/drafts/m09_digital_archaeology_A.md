# Digital Archaeology: Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
Sodium light on a grain-store wall,
a shredder singing down the hall,
you came by night to read the dead
in dust and tape and ribbon red.
They filed the bodies, box by box,
they filed the hearts behind the locks,
and deep in the restricted rows
lies the one who taught the one you chose.

[Pre-chorus]
So dig, darling, dig,
before the morning eats the page;
every secret has a shelf,
and a lady minds the cage.

[Chorus]
Digital archaeology!
You brush the dust and you find me,
every word I ever signed
laid out in the dark for you.
Digital archaeology,
go deep enough and you will see
the name you never dared to say
was shining through.

[Verse 2]
A cursor pulses on a borrowed screen,
a ledger tells you where I've been:
four faces at the briefing, all so fine,
four hands you shook, and one is mine.
A velvet voice comes down the line,
she'll sell a name for one more sign,
and the kindest hand that held the door
put a key in yours. Now what's it for?

[Pre-chorus]
So dig, darling, dig,
the safe is humming out its code;
there's a folder with your name on it,
and it knows the way you'll go.

[Chorus]
Digital archaeology!
You brush the dust and you find me,
every word I ever signed
laid out in the dark for you.
Digital archaeology,
go deep enough and you will see
the name you never dared to say
was shining through.

[Bridge]
Nine long winters they kept a broken lock
and called it insurance on the clock;
I wrote the letter, never sent.
Now ask your masters what they meant.
"Let it hurt afterwards," I taught,
and she learned it better than I thought.
Burn every page, but leave one light,
one server humming through the night.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Digital archaeology!
You'll dig me up and then you'll see
the man who wrote your every move
was waiting at the end for you.
Digital archaeology,
the hand that buried it was me.
So dig, darling, dig,
I left the door wide open,
come and find me before six.

[Outro]
Paper turns to ash, love,
tape turns to snow...
I filed you years ago.

## Notes
- Setting and the purge: the sodium-lit grain warehouse, the shredder audible from the first minute, "dust and tape" (paper stacks, tape room), the cage with Sorensen inside it ("a lady minds the cage"), and "tape turns to snow" for the deck wipe.
- Narrator is the Architect speaking to Agent 0x00. "The one who taught the one you chose" and "she learned it better than I thought" point at HaX's teacher without naming Tesseract. The chorus's "name you never dared to say" is the identity reveal.
- Game elements: the drop terminal ("a cursor pulses on a borrowed screen"), the flags as shelves opened, the ledger (flag 2), the Recruiter's phone offer ("a velvet voice... she'll sell a name"), Sorensen's safe opened with the PIN cracker ("the safe is humming out its code"), and the Reading ("a folder with your name on it, and it knows the way you'll go").
- Twist hints: "four hands you shook, and one is mine" is the ledger's sleeper on the raid team, and "the kindest hand... put a key in yours" points at Kowalczyk's cracker without naming her. "Leave one light, one server humming" and "I left the door wide open" hint that the purge spared Tomb Beta's server on purpose because he wants to be found and the raid to come.
- Bridge: the Albion flaw SAFETYNET kept for nine years, his unsent vendor notice, and "Let it hurt afterwards" turned against him. "Before six" is the Publication's 06:00 UTC hour; "I filed you years ago" closes on the Reading.
