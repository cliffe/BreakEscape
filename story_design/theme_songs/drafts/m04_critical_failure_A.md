# Critical Failure: Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
You came through the gate with a contractor's grace
And an order number nobody could trace.
You were the man who kept a city alight;
Now you sit at my bench and you cost out the night.

[Pre-chorus]
The screen swears I'm cool at a calm twenty-eight,
But the old iron dial by the racks knows my fate:
Sixty-one, and climbing, love,
Sixty-one, and climbing...

[Chorus]
Critical failure,
Oh, the heat of your hand,
You've a whole sleeping city at your command.
You've counted us down to one decimal place,
Critical failure, and it's there in your face.

[Verse 2]
Now a stranger comes calling four hours too soon
With a badge for the gate and a lie for the room,
Takes a card off a lanyard across a man's desk,
Takes a print from the panel, and the dial does the rest.
The hydrogen glows amber down the long rack row,
And a voice on the line whispers, "Carefully. Go."

[Pre-chorus]
You told them eight o'clock, you were cool, you were sure,
But a klaxon is singing them out the fire door,
Nine in hi-vis, and your clock's running, love,
Nine in hi-vis, and running...

[Chorus]
Critical failure,
Oh, the heat of your hand,
One keystroke from ruin, and the ruin was planned.
You signed for the number, you know it by heart,
Critical failure, it was there from the start.

[Bridge]
There's a heart on the wall that you never could own,
Red as a warning, and wired alone.
"The button, or me. And you'd better be quick.
You don't get to have both." And the seconds still tick.
Lift the flap, or go for him, you can't have the two,
While the dock door stands open and the night's coming through.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Critical failure,
Take the heat from my hand,
Find the red in the dark where the cold racks stand.
They'll call it an accident come morning light,
But critical failure...
Not tonight.

[Outro]
Two hundred and forty thousand
Sleep on through the night,
And never know whose hand
Left on their light.

## Notes
- The narrator is the Albion Energy Storage plant, singing to Voltage as a lover he has turned on. He is the ex-grid engineer who "kept a city alight", and the title doubles as a love affair breaking down and a grid failing. "It was there from the start" echoes his line that ENTROPY "didn't make it fragile", they "just stopped pretending it wasn't".
- Verse 1 and pre-chorus 1: Critical Mass's OptiGrid cover and the order number Vance never found; the faked SCADA reading (28°C on the screen) against the mechanical dial by Rack Bank C (61°C), which is how the mission is won: trust the physical instrument over the screen.
- Chorus and verse 2: Voltage's casualty model ("I know it to one decimal place", "I countersigned"), the one-keystroke trigger, and the player's regulator cover arriving four hours early, cloning Vance's prox card across his desk, the fingerprint on the HV panel, the amber hydrogen panel in the halls, and Agent HaX on the phone ("Go carefully").
- Pre-chorus 2 hints at a consequence without spelling it out: sounding the evacuation alarm gets the nine night crew out of Hall 2 through the fire doors, and "your clock's running" suggests that it also starts Voltage's clock early.
- Bridge: the moral choice in the plant room, "The button, or me... You don't get to have both", and the hardwired red mushroom-head ESD behind its hinged flap, "wired alone" off the network. The open dock door hints that he gets away if you go for the button.
- Final chorus and outro: the debrief choice between disclosure and calling it an accident ("Nobody gets to call it an accident"), and the 240,000 people on the feed who never knew.
