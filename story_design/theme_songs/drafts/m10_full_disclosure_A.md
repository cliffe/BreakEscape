# Full Disclosure: Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
Down in Montana where the old war sleeps,
Under the snow where the concrete keeps,
The blast door rolls and it knows your name,
I carved it there when I set the game.
Eight long nights you have danced my floor,
Ransom and ghosts and a mole at the door.

[Pre-chorus]
The clock on the wall reads a quarter to four,
There's a wren at your shoulder who's sung here before,
So come down, come down, through the smoke and the steel,
I'll show you the key, and I'll show you the deal.

[Chorus]
Full disclosure!
Every crack they hid behind the wall,
Every secret free for one and all.
Lay your hand on mine and let it through,
Full disclosure, darling,
And I'd rather it was you.

[Verse 2]
The voice in your ear, she was mine long ago,
I taught her to hurt only after the show;
There's a sheet on my desk where the dead are a sum,
A price for the peace of the world that's to come,
And I've signed it in ink with a note in the red:
If my sums are mistaken, then tell me instead.

[Pre-chorus]
The ghost on the handset is selling the dawn,
There's sterling for sale where the shutters are drawn,
So come down, come down, where the lights have gone low,
I'll show you how it works, if you want to know.

[Chorus]
Full disclosure!
Every crack they hid behind the wall,
Every secret free for one and all.
Lay your hand on mine and let it through,
Full disclosure, darling,
And I'd rather it was you.

[Bridge]
Nine years gone, I wrote them a warning,
They locked it away in a drawer,
And the lights went out on Albion's morning,
So tell me who you're fighting for.
Keep it, or send it, or burn the lot,
Take me in chains, or take my hand,
And mind where my other hand is, love:
Not everything goes as planned.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Full disclosure!
Every secret, and the last one's mine:
I chose you, and I chose you by design.
I have stopped the clock, there's no more hurry,
Press the key or prove my file untrue.
Full disclosure, darling,
I'd rather it was you.

[Outro]
Afterwards, then.

## Notes

- Narrator is Dr Adrian Tesseract at the core, singing to Agent 0x00. "I have stopped the clock" (held back for the final chorus) and "I'd rather it was you" are his arrival line. "Lay your hand on mine", "it was you" and "I chose you by design" are the Publication's need for a SAFETYNET hand on the key, and the Reading's prediction. "Prove my file untrue" points to "Then I was wrong about one thing."
- Setting and route: the Cold War bunker in the Montana hills, the blast door keyed to 0x00's name, the countdown ("a quarter to four", just before the cells' 04:00 head start). Ghost sells the head start on the security-office handset, Victoria Sterling waits in the gallery behind the shutter ("sterling for sale"), and the power room cuts the lights. "Show you how it works" is the first step of the Join path.
- Season glance: "eight long nights you have danced my floor", with ransom (m02), ghosts (m03) and the mole (m08) in passing, is the m07 idea that he was measuring the player all along. The bridge's unsent warning and "Albion's morning" are the m09 Albion papers and Critical Mass's m04 attack, the partly true case he makes.
- HaX: "the voice in your ear" was his pupil. "Hurt only after the show" twists "Let it hurt afterwards, not during".
- The talk-down: the signed casualty sheet with its red margin note, and "if my sums are mistaken, then tell me instead", the mission's own rebuttal on the first claim.
- Hinted twists: "a wren at your shoulder who's sung here before" points to the sleeper inside the raid team (Kowalczyk, eleven years in) without naming her. "Mind where my other hand is" hints at the dead-man switch under the console. "Keep it, or send it, or burn the lot" are the three database choices, and "Afterwards, then" is his card on the console in Scorched Earth.
