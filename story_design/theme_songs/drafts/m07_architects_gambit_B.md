## Notes
- Sung by the Architect to Agent 0x00 from inside the handset. Verse 1 and the outro use his calls from the ink: the phone that lights without ringing, the trace that isn't there, "I've read your file… a particular shape", no call in the log, "He hangs up first. He has hung up first every time."
- The pre-chorus and bridge carry the triage choice: four operations in sixty seconds, one tactical team, two crises left unanswered. "Number or… stomach", "Let it hurt afterwards, not during", "somebody with a tablet" and "I'll have the figures by morning" come from his taunts and sign-off. The three cities are listed the way Netherton lists them.
- Verse 2 covers the people in the facility. Mercer signed the casualty projection and thinks the lesson is his. Elena was sold a six-hour, nobody-hurt outage and her copy had the hospital carve-out. Game elements appear as imagery: lockpick, RFID clone of the zone badge, the attack host, root as "the throne".
- Twists, hinted only. "Laid down to turn your eyes away" and "whose lines he read" point at Mercer and the grid being the diversion ("the grid was never the point"). "Your house has one unlocked window" and "read your orders… before the ink had time to dry" point at the SAFETYNET mole who leaked the deployment before it was made. The final chorus says outright what the night was measuring: the agent.
- "A hundred and forty-seven candles" are the substations held. "Doors are closing" echoes "Doors close. It's the one thing they reliably do."
- The title is sung as "Architect's gambit", without "The" in front, so the chorus downbeat lands on "Ar-".
