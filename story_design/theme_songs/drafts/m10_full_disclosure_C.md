# Full Disclosure: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
Two vans on the gravel, the pines and the wire,
a ridge full of concrete they poured for the fire.
We pull on our gloves and we don't meet our eyes:
we're all of us breathing, and one of us lies.
Downstairs, by the rotas, they've pinned up my face:
"Do not detain", like a card at a place.

[Pre-chorus]
And each floor going down has a face that I know,
one I saved, one I bargained, and one I let go,
and the man at the bottom has written them down,
so I'll say them myself, and I'll say them out loud.

[Chorus]
Full disclosure,
here's the whole of it, signed:
every lock that I forced, every word that I broke,
every name that I left behind.
Read it back to me line by line, I've got it by heart:
full disclosure, and the hand is mine.

[Verse 2]
I've smiled at a broker and bought what she sold,
I've stood in a turbine hall gone cold,
I've walked forty-seven names out on my own,
I've frozen a fortune, or followed it home.
Two names on a wall, for the doors I didn't take,
and one of our own sold the map that we'd made.

[Pre-chorus]
There's a schedule down here: the pumps by the beds,
the taps and the calls going quietly dead,
and he'll say that we hoarded it. Part of me knows.
So I'll lay it all down, and I'll lay it down slow.

[Chorus]
Full disclosure,
here's the whole of it, signed:
every lock that I forced, every word that I broke,
every name that I left behind.
Read it back to me line by line, I've got it by heart:
full disclosure, and the hand is mine.

[Bridge]
HaX, keep on talking me down every stair;
you sat in his classroom, and I sat in yours.
Whoever among us is keeping a score,
I'll know you by morning, or you'll know me first.
He wrote that I'd press it. He's been right before.
So hear it from me, with the whole of it bare:

[Final chorus, up a key]
Full disclosure,
here's the last of it, signed:
I'm afraid that you're right, I'm afraid of the key,
and I'm walking in with my eyes open wide.
Read it back to me line by line, I've got it by heart:
full disclosure, and the hand is mine.

[Outro]
The hand is mine.
(spoken) Going in.

## Notes

- **Tradition:** modern (the *Skyfall* / *No Time to Die* line), sung inside the agent's head on the way down through Tomb Gamma, with a classic brassy lift for the final chorus. The title is turned around. A and B make it the Publication, sung by Tesseract. Here it is the agent's own confession, made before the man who kept a file on them can read it out. "Signed" is the agent's signature on that confession. It deliberately mirrors his signed casualty sheet, which this song never shows.
- **The finale's route:** the forward post's vans and the fence (verse 1); the security office's photo pinned among the guards' rotas with "Do not detain" (a place card at his table, meaning the invitation); the five full scenes as "a face that I know" on each floor (saved: Gary; bargained: Ghost or Nightshade; let go: Voltage or Victoria, by branch); the server hall's schedule in plain words (infusion pumps, water, dispatch going dead); HaX on the line, and the quiet as the agent walks into the core.
- **The season looking back (verse 2):** Victoria the broker (m03), the Albion plant (m04), the forty-seven names (m05), the fund frozen or followed (m06, both branches in one line), the two dead agents' names on Task Force Null's wall for the sites not covered (m07), and Nightshade selling SAFETYNET's map (m08). "Same classroom" (HaX's m08 text) comes back in the bridge as "you sat in his classroom, and I sat in yours".
- **What the agent doesn't know yet:** "keep on talking me down every stair" is sung before the agent knows the jam will cut HaX off in the gallery. "The hand is mine" means "I'm nobody's" to the agent. To anyone who has played the core it is the hand he needs on the key. "He wrote that I'd press it" is the Reading from m09, which the agent has read. "I'm afraid that you're right" foreshadows the offer and Join without naming either.
- **WREN:** "one of us lies" and "whoever among us is keeping a score" come only from what every player learns from the ledger and the briefing. There is no pronoun, no role, no kit and no position ("among us" stands for the whole team, never one place beside the agent). The PIN cracker is left out on purpose because it would point to whoever handed it over. "You'll know me first" works for any of the suspects, and also for the service door at the core.
- **Avoided:** A's and B's hooks (darling, "I'd rather it was you", "my file says it's you", keep/send/burn, the keyed door, the stopped clock, the dead-man switch, the margin note, "at your shoulder", "voice in your ear"), plus the season-wide bans. Hacking appears only as locks, a key and a map.
