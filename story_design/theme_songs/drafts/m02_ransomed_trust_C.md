# Ransomed Trust: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
Rain on the car park, red behind the glass,
a generator beating out the time.
They gave me a card with my name in pen
and not one reader left to call it mine.
The building lost its memory in a minute,
every name behind the same black screen,
so I walk your halls on borrowed say-so,
smiling like a face you've seen.

[Pre-chorus]
HaX in my ear says a pick is a confession,
the people are the lock,
so I ask, and I wait, and I thank the helpful ones,
and I trust whoever answers when I knock.

[Chorus]
Ransomed trust,
the only key I'm carrying tonight:
a name in someone's book
that any passing pen could strike.
I won't pick you, I can't buy you,
all I've got's a name and half a lie.
Ransomed trust,
lend it me, and I'll give you back your eyes.

[Verse 2]
On the ward a nurse is standing in for a screen,
her biro keeps the watch the wires kept,
and a man in bed four asks if I'm the one,
and I say yes, and squeeze, and count his breath.
Then a tired man skids his one live card
across a squared-up stack of being right:
"When they blame me, you'll have known."
I fold it in my pocket with the night.

[Pre-chorus]
There's a cursor in the dark that talks like a ledger,
says it's easier to hate a thing that blinks.
HaX says keep it out of my head.
It's in. It's sums I've seen before. It asks me what I think.

[Chorus]
Ransomed trust,
the only key I'm carrying tonight:
a name in someone's book
that any passing pen could strike.
I won't pick you, I can't buy you,
all I've got's a name and half a lie.
Ransomed trust,
lend it me, and I'll give you back your eyes.

[Bridge]
Then the cursor wears a hood,
a shape against the light,
holds the keys out, free of charge,
if I'll put the board in print tonight.
Pay, and the next ward pays.
Say no, and count the hours by hand.
Take it free, and never know
whose doors I've opened, or where I stand.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Ransomed trust,
the only key I'm carrying tonight:
a name in someone's book
that any passing pen could strike.
Somebody signed their name for mine,
and I won't make that kindness lie.
Ransomed trust,
lend it me, and I'll give you back your eyes,
lend it me, and I'll give you back your eyes.

[Outro]
Rain on the car park.
The badge gone soft as tissue in my hand.
I thanked them all tonight.
I thanked them all.

## Notes
- Tradition: a blend. Verses and pre-choruses are modern and close-up (*Skyfall*, *No Time to Die*), sung inside the agent's head at a slow 6/8. The final chorus lifts a key for a classic belt. Season position: the second field operation. The agent is still keen but is tested here for the first time, with Ghost's body-count arithmetic as "sums I've seen before" (Derek's casualty projections in m01, which HaX ties to this case in the debrief).
- Angle: the title read from the agent's side. With the access control server encrypted, the only credential the agent has is other people's belief: a paper badge Dr Kim signed ("a card with my name in pen"), the visitor log ("a name in someone's book"), and a cover story that is itself "half a lie". "Give you back your eyes" is the monitoring the agent is trying to restore (Mrs Hargreaves: "can't see me screen").
- Game and story references: rain and red emergency lighting on arrival, the generator, every reader dead, "the building stopped knowing who anybody was" (Bernie), HaX's "the picks are a confession / this is a mission about people", Sister Doyle as the alarm with her biro obs, Mr Pryce in bed four and the hand-squeezed resuscitation bag, Gary skidding the one working server-room card across his printed emails ("you'll have known, and said nothing"), Ghost as the cursor that is "easier to despise", the hooded video at the recovery console, and the three-way choice (pay, refuse and restore by hand, or Ghost's free keys in exchange for publishing the board's evidence).
- Dramatic irony, with the agent not yet seeing it: "I thank the helpful ones / and I trust whoever answers" (the insider has been "helpful to this entire hospital all night"), "any passing pen could strike" (the log line struck through in a different biro before the agent knows why), "whose doors I've opened" (Ghost's "my keys, my doors", staying in the network after the sweep), and the outro "I thanked them all", which a player hears as including him. Nothing names the insider, his post, the telephone or the badge number.
- Kept clear of A and B: no trust/dust/rust rhyme, no held door, no "seven", no scanner, no "who holds the keys", no lamp, no switched-off screen, no "I'll be here" or "mind how you go", no "long night by hand". Also none of the season-wide repeats: no "darling", no clock on the wall, no flags as candles, no "let it hurt afterwards". The agent has no name, pronouns or body detail. Ghost is "it" and "the cursor", never gendered.
- Bernie vouching ("Somebody signed their name for mine") and saving Mr Pryce are optional branches. They're sung as things the agent hopes for or does, so they still fit a run where they didn't happen. Word count is about 330 with each chorus counted once.
