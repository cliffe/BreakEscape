# Insider Trading: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
Half past four on a Wednesday, all glass and clipped-back lawn,
I sign the book as an auditor, in a name I've barely worn.
Patricia hands me eight names and a corridor of doors:
seven of them are honest, and one is keeping score.
I trade a coffee for a rumour, a favour for a card,
and I smile the way they smile here, and it isn't very hard.

[Pre-chorus]
Hunt the pattern, I was taught, and never mind the face,
the door that opens late at night, the money out of place.

[Chorus]
Insider trading,
I came to catch a thief and learnt the trade:
a smile for a door, a lie for a name, and every lie gets paid.
Insider trading,
I'm the stranger with the key,
and every call they leave to ring
is ringing back to me.

[Verse 2]
By seven the lab wing empties and the strip lights hum alone;
I read the desks like diaries with a pick I call my own.
Their server keeps a side door that was left upon the latch,
and flag by flag I'm learning what it is I'm here to catch.
The powder I brought back from Albion lifts a thumb but not a why,
and each time I name the wrong one, I buy the real one time.

[Pre-chorus]
HaX is in my ear: keep the hunch and proof apart,
one pile for what I know, and one pile for my heart.

[Chorus]
Insider trading,
I came to catch a thief and learnt the trade:
a smile for a door, a lie for a name, and every lie gets paid.
Insider trading,
I'm the stranger with the key,
and every call they leave to ring
is ringing back to me.

[Bridge]
Four jobs in, I thought I'd learnt the look of someone bad;
I've learnt instead the look of someone spending all they had.
Three doors out of the data centre, and not one of them is clean:
a cell, a second life, or a headline on a screen.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Insider trading,
I came to catch a thief and learnt the trade:
a smile for a door, a lie for a name, and every lie gets paid.
Insider trading,
the stranger with the key,
and whichever door I open now
comes swinging back on me.

[Outro]
Eight names on a list.
Seven honest.
And a stranger, wearing mine.

## Notes

- **Tradition:** a blend. The verses are close and uneasy, in the agent's head, in the *You Know My Name* manner; the chorus is built to be belted, Bassey-size, with the key change carrying the final lines. The angle is the title turned on the singer: to catch an insider, the agent has to become one, so the agent is the one "trading" on the inside (a borrowed name, a coffee for Lisa's gossip, a favour for Owen's spare card) and is uneasy at how easily it comes.
- **Mission references:** Wednesday at half past four and the four-hour clock (HaX's deployment line), the external auditor cover, Patricia's eight-name suspect list, the lockpicks, the print kit "brought back from Albion" (HaX's kit line, and a nod to how far the agent has come this season), the research portal as "a side door left upon the latch", the flags, HaX's "keep hunches and proof in separate piles", and the data-centre finale. "Every call they leave to ring" is the slowed 999 dispatch, kept apart from A's minutes and B's ambulance and sirens.
- **Whodunnit kept fair:** the insider is never named, gendered or described. "Seven of them are honest, and one is keeping score" leaves the pool at eight. "Each time I name the wrong one, I buy the real one time" is the agent's fear of a wrong accusation, which a player who accused Halloran will recognise (the upload is brought forward), but it points at nobody. The bridge's "someone spending all they had" gives the motive the corridor flyer and the bills already make public, with no family detail.
- **Moral choice:** "a cell, a second life, or a headline on a screen" covers arrest, turning the insider into a double agent, and anonymous public exposure, as roads seen from outside, not a list of endings. The final chorus hands the consequence back to the agent ("comes swinging back on me").
- **Mole seed (dramatic irony only):** "Hunt the pattern, I was taught, and never mind the face" is Nightshade's briefing advice ("you hunt the pattern"), credited to no one. To the agent it's a lesson from a trusted colleague; on replay after m08, the teacher is the one whose face nobody minded. The outro's "a stranger, wearing mine" means the agent's cover name. "Calm" and any pointer at SAFETYNET or home are kept out, per the seeding plan and the cross-season pass.
- **Avoided from A and B and the season list:** no "everyone has a price", rain, wedding band, minutes, mug, mirror, transfer bar, envelope, smiling name, sirens or turned back; no "darling", clock on the wall or flags as candles. About 390 words with repeats, about 310 without, so inside the brief's range on the same count A and B use.
