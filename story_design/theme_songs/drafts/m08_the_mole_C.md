# The Mole: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
I came home to the Citadel with every reader red,
a voice up in the ceiling logging every step I tread.
Nine days I've worn my name like a coat that someone sold,
and two of ours paid for it at the sites we couldn't hold.

[Pre-chorus]
He says I'm the one he can clear without a log,
since mine was the name on the bill.
HaX on the line, so professional tonight,
says, "Bear with me." And I will.

[Chorus]
There's a mark on the face of the house I call home;
I passed it so often I forgot it was there.
Three names on the paper, three doors down the hall.
I'll bring it to the light it was never made to bear,
and find the Mole.

[Verse 2]
One keeps his screen locked and his eyes on the sum,
one's gone where no reader can follow his feet,
one's in before daylight and never goes home,
and every one of them would offer me a seat.
I lift a print off silver, I lean in close for a card,
I wake the sleeping doors and ask them what they keep.

[Pre-chorus]
Say it too loud and the whole floor turns,
and the wrong one hangs while the right one walks.
I've been certain before I was sure, and it burns,
so I listen, and let the doors talk.

[Chorus]
There's a mark on the face of the house I call home;
I passed it so often I forgot it was there.
Three names on the paper, three doors down the hall.
I'll bring it to the light it was never made to bear,
and find the Mole.

[Bridge]
And when you're at the table with the tape going round,
I'll have one key to turn and one key to hand back.
I could shut the door on what you've done,
or send you home to them with my words in your mouth,
and wear your kind of smile
while I watch you walk.

[Final chorus, up a key]
There's a mark on the face of the house I call home,
and tonight it's the only thing I see.
Three names on the paper, three doors down the hall.
I'll say it out loud when the doors agree,
and name the Mole.

[Outro]
The ceiling keeps my footsteps
and the hour I came through.
Somewhere, it kept yours too.

## Notes
- **Tradition:** modern, intimate and brooding, in the *Skyfall* line, with a final chorus that lifts a key. Version B is sung by the mole; this one is the hunter, so the two songs face each other across the same corridor. Late in the season the voice has stopped being keen: the agent is tested, angry, and for the first time betrayed from inside.
- **Verse 1:** ATHENA's opening (Level Red readers, the building AI logging every route), nine days since Portland, two agents dead at the sites the one team couldn't reach, and the agent's own name sold to ENTROPY. The pre-chorus is Netherton ("the one person in this building I can clear without a log... Yours was the name they were selling") and HaX's first call ("very professional tonight. Bear with me").
- **Chorus hook:** a "mole" read as a mark on the skin, a spot on the face of the house you stop seeing because you pass it every day, then the animal's fear of daylight. Neither A nor B uses this image, and it avoids their storm, door-ajar, candle, wind and underground hooks.
- **Whodunit balance:** Verse 2 gives each suspect one line that looks guilty and one that later clears him. Vector has the locked screen and the maths, Phantom the unlogged absences, Nightshade the long hours ("I'm always here"). All three would "offer me a seat", because all three tell the agent to go and check the logs. The chorus gives the three names one line between them, and no door is singled out. The song has no teacher, lab, PIN box or calm-as-a-tell line, which were the review's spoiler traps.
- **Investigation as imagery:** the fingerprint kit on the USB stick ("a print off silver"), cloning the Director's lanyard ("lean in close for a card"), the door audit ("wake the sleeping doors", "let the doors talk"), Netherton's warning ("certain before you are sure") and the accuse-out-loud mechanic ("the whole floor turns").
- **Dramatic irony:** the bridge looks ahead to the arrest-or-triple-agent choice, sung as an open question: one key to turn, one to hand back. "Wear your kind of smile" and the outro's "it kept yours too" mean nothing yet to the agent. A player who has finished the mission hears the smile that never slips and the door log that names the mole.
