# Insider Trading: Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
Rain on the glass of a Cambridge park,
a man with a lanyard who works past dark.
The doors all open at the touch of your hand,
you wear that pass like a wedding band.
I found you pale in a waiting room,
with a bill on your knee and a cure due soon.

[Pre-chorus]
I never broke a window, never picked a lock,
I only asked the time, and then I bought the clock.

[Chorus]
Insider trading,
you sell what you're trusted to keep,
the keys to the city while the city's asleep.
Insider trading,
everyone has a price.
I pay you in cash, and they pay in minutes,
and minutes, my darling, are lives.

[Verse 2]
Now there's a stranger with an auditor's case,
a borrowed badge and a patient face.
She lifted your thumb from a "World's Best Dad",
she's reading in ink the best lies you had.
The racks are roaring, the night's gone thin,
and the man in the mirror won't let you in.

[Pre-chorus]
The bar on your screen is crawling up to whole,
ninety-nine per cent of a borrowed soul.

[Chorus]
Insider trading,
you sell what you're trusted to keep,
the keys to the city while the city's asleep.
Insider trading,
everyone has a price.
I pay you in cash, and they pay in minutes,
and minutes, my darling, are lives.

[Bridge]
And you, sweet agent, ringing back so late,
forty-seven names in an envelope can wait.
Leave them for my courier and walk out clean,
no file with your face, no thread, no scene.
And if you think your own house is safe from me,
ask the calmest man who briefed you how he sleeps.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Insider trading,
so turn him, or burn him, or call in the law,
he's three months gone, and you know what you saw.
Insider trading,
everyone has a price.
I paid mine in cash, and you'll pay in choices,
and choices, my darling, are lives.

[Outro]
I never broke in, darling.
Nobody does.
I was already inside.

## Notes

- The narrator is the Recruiter: TalentStack's "Everyone has a price" is her own line in the phone ink, and it anchors each chorus. She sings to the mole in the verses and turns to the agent in the bridge, the way her ring-back call does. The outro bends the cell's motto from the universe bible ("We don't break in. We're already inside").
- Setting and stakes: the Quantum Dynamics science park outside Cambridge; "the keys to the city while the city's asleep" for the quantum-safe key material and the night upload; "they pay in minutes, and minutes are lives" for the slowed 999 dispatch and the thirty to forty-five projected deaths.
- The mole stays unnamed, so the whodunnit holds. A player will hear him in the wedding band, the waiting room and the clinical-trial bill (Elena's treatment), the "World's Best Dad" mug the agent takes his print from for the key vault, the journal of lies he told himself, and the data-centre transfer bar at ninety-nine per cent.
- Game elements in passing: the auditor cover with kit in the bag, the cloned or borrowed badge, the lifted fingerprint, the roaring server racks, the upload terminal, and the Recruiter's ring-back deal (forty-seven names in the TalentStack envelope, the courier, "no file with your face").
- The final chorus lists the unranked endings (turn him as a double agent, burn him through public exposure, hand him to the police) and the point that he is only three months into radicalisation (Torres: "Three months ago I was trying to save my wife"). Its last couplet hands the cost of the choice to the agent.
- Foreshadowing: "ask the calmest man who briefed you how he sleeps" points at Nightshade, who opens this mission by explaining how to catch an insider ("the calm of someone who's decided the rules don't apply"). That pays off in m08 and is never said outright.
