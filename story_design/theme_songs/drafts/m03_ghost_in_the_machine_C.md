# Ghost in the Machine: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
I signed in at three as the someone we'd planned,
a hungry CV in a borrowed hand.
She reads people for a living, HaX said,
so I gave her a person to read instead.
She asked me the price of the sick and the old:
a T-shirt from the vendor, or forty thousand, cold.
I said what the one I was playing would say,
and I said it too quickly, and smiled anyway.

[Pre-chorus]
Stand by the board while she maps out her pride,
something counts down in the dark at my side,
thirty slow seconds of somebody's key,
and she stops watching my hands. She's stopped watching me.

[Chorus]
I'm the ghost in the machine,
no face to keep and no name to give,
I carry the ward through the house that sold it,
I count who died and I count who lived.
Ghost in the machine,
light as a rumour, quiet as debt,
and nobody here knows the ghost is coming,
nobody here knows I'm coming yet.

[Verse 2]
Eleven o'clock and the building's asleep,
the night lights burning low on the floor,
a torch goes round in the east wing, round again,
and my picks ask softly at a stranger's door.
A guard with a hand full of what I could spare
swears he's good at never seeing who's there.
There's a man in the dark who drew every bed,
and I know them by heart, and I've counted the dead.

[Pre-chorus]
Four quiet proofs sent off into the dark,
HaX in my ear keeping everything slow,
an invoice that priced in a premium for the sick,
and the house is asleep, as far as I know.

[Chorus]
I'm the ghost in the machine,
no face to keep and no name to give,
I carry the ward through the house that sold it,
I count who died and I count who lived.
Ghost in the machine,
light as a rumour, quiet as debt,
and nobody here knows the ghost is coming,
nobody here knows I'm coming yet.

[Bridge]
She said I'd do well here. I didn't flinch.
I've turned that over all night like a key.
If the house I am haunting is haunting me back,
then which of us is free?
Somewhere upstairs there's a call to be made,
a deal, or the cuffs, or an open door,
and I'll know which one when I'm stood before her,
and I'll carry it, like the ward.

[Final chorus, lift]
I'm the ghost in the machine,
no face to keep and no name to give,
I carry the ward through the house that sold it,
I count who died and I count who lived.
Ghost in the machine,
light as a rumour, quiet as debt,
and nobody here knows the ghost is coming,
nobody here has seen me. Nobody yet.

[Outro]
Sign me out as no one.
Nobody saw a thing.

## Notes
- Tradition: a blend. Modern for the verses and first two choruses (close, inside the agent's head), with a classic lift on the final chorus. A is already the brassy one and B the brooding one, so C sits between them. Sung by 0x00 with no name, pronouns or body details. "No face to keep and no name to give" is the cover and the nameless player at once.
- Season position: one month after St. Catherine's, so the agent is tested and carrying the m02 deaths. "I carry the ward through the house that sold it" and "I count who died and I count who lived" stay vague on purpose, because the death toll changes with the m02 recovery call. "I know them by heart, and I've counted the dead" sets the agent next to Danny Foster, who can draw the ward from memory ("a man in the dark who drew every bed").
- Verse 1 and the pre-chorus are the three o'clock interview: the freelance-pentester cover, HaX's "she reads people for a living", Sterling's T-shirt-or-forty-thousand question, the whiteboard, and the cloner's thirty-second read at the agent's side. Verse 2 is the eleven o'clock return: night lights, Terry's torch on his east-wing round, the picks, the five-hundred-pound bribe ("a hand full of what I could spare"), Danny's dark office. The second pre-chorus has the four flags sent from the drop-site terminal ("four quiet proofs"), HaX on the phone, and the invoice with its healthcare premium.
- Dramatic irony with no spoiler: the agent believes "she's stopped watching me", "nobody here knows I'm coming yet", "the house is asleep, as far as I know", that the guard is "good at never seeing", and in the outro "nobody saw a thing". Anyone who has played the mission knows Sterling clocked the pocket, the guard may well ring her, and she is waiting upstairs with a bag packed.
- The bridge takes Sterling's "you didn't flinch once. People who don't flinch do well here" and turns it on the agent: the hunter afraid of fitting in. "A deal, or the cuffs, or an open door" covers the turn, arrest and escape endings without picking one. "When I'm stood before her" echoes HaX's "that's a call for when you're stood in front of her".
- Kept clear of A and B: no window-mirror, coat, lift doors, winter, "one move", "everything breaks", pocket light, blue marker or wearing her card. No darling, no clock, and the flags aren't candles. The only technical imagery is a key, a door and the dark.
