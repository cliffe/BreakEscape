# Full Disclosure: Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
The road's been closed off and the county's asleep,
two times on a screen and a promise to keep.
You're counting my fences, your team checks its gear;
I've been counting on you since before you were here.
I left you a door in the side of the hill.
It knows you by name, and it's waiting there still.

[Pre-chorus]
Come down through the floors that were built for the end,
past faces you've met, past an enemy, a friend,
and one at your shoulder who laughs like the rest
has propped me a door. I won't tell you. Guess.

[Chorus]
Full disclosure,
every weakness they were hoarding, here, beneath the hill.
Full disclosure,
every door they never mended, standing open still.
At six it goes to everyone, the careful and the cruel,
and someone has to turn the key. I kept a place for you.

[Verse 2]
You've stood by a bedside where monitors sang,
you've stood at a clock while a whole city rang.
You pressed it, or didn't, that first day of all;
I've kept every answer. They're pinned to my wall.

[Pre-chorus]
I wrote down the dead and I rounded them low;
there's a note in the margin in a hand that I know.
She'll tell you: let it hurt afterwards, not during.
I say let it hurt now, and watch the hoarders burning.

[Chorus]
Full disclosure,
every weakness they were hoarding, here, beneath the hill.
Full disclosure,
every door they never mended, standing open still.
At six it goes to everyone, the careful and the cruel,
and someone has to turn the key. My file says it's you.

[Bridge]
The voice in your ear goes to static, then still:
one walkway, one window, one door in the hill.
I've stopped all the clocks, so there's no need to run.
Take your time. I'd rather it was you than anyone.
Keep it, or give it, or burn it to the ground.
My hand's under the table. Don't look down.

[Final chorus, up a tone]
Full disclosure,
I have read you like a file since you were new.
Full disclosure,
there is one line on the page I couldn't prove:
at six they read the map, unless your hand says no.
My file says it's you.
My file says it's you.
Don't make me wrong. Or do.

[Outro, spoken low]
Afterwards, then.

## Notes

- Narrator is Tesseract, waiting at the core: "Take your time. I'd rather it was you" and "My file says it's you" lift his core lines ("I've paused it. I'd rather it was you", "My file says it will be you"). "I left you a door... It knows you by name" is the invitation to HaX and the blast door keyed to 0x00. "Two times on a screen" is the 04:00 head start and 06:00 Publication on the forward post's display, and "the road's been closed off" is the sheriff's roadblock.
- The WREN twist is hinted without a name or pronoun: someone "at your shoulder who laughs like the rest" (Kowalczyk laughing with Arakawa in the briefing) "has propped me a door", the core's service door propped at 02:10. Players who don't find WREN watch that door open at the core.
- Verse 2 looks back over the season: the hospital bedside (m02), the city under a clock (m07's countdown), "you pressed it, or didn't, that first day" (m01's launch choice, one of his three record lines), and "pinned to my wall" for the file of choices he argues from.
- The second pre-chorus gives away the talk-down's first rebuttal as a slip in his own voice: the casualty sheet he signed knowing it was low, corrected in the margin by his systems lead. "Let it hurt afterwards, not during" is the season's recurring line, here turned round into his creed. "She" is HaX, his old student.
- The bridge covers the gallery (HaX's line jammed, one walkway, one door), the clock he stops, the database choice (secure, disclose, wipe: "keep it, or give it, or burn it to the ground") and the dead-man switch under the console. "Don't make me wrong. Or do" nods to the Reading and his "Then I was wrong about one thing". The spoken outro is the card he leaves on the console in Scorched Earth.
- Hacking appears only as imagery: doors, a key, a map of weaknesses, a file.
