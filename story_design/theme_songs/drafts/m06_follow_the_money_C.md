# Follow the Money: Version C (agent)

[Verse 1]
I knew your voice in a hospital by generator light,
a price on every darkened screen, a deadline through the night.
I watched you buy a frightened man a reason to betray,
and every time I reached for you, you'd changed and slipped away.

[Pre-chorus]
Tonight I wear a borrowed name and a regulator's grey,
the guard runs down his list and finds a name that isn't mine.
We both came in disguised tonight, we both know how to play,
so take my hand across the floor and lead me down the line.

[Chorus]
Follow the money,
you change your coat at every door,
you come in as a ransom and you go out as a war.
Follow the money,
the only honest thing I've found:
you always go where you were sent, and you write the dead ones down.

[Verse 2]
The trader shrugs, "Depends who's asking," watching six charts climb,
the analyst draws streams that meet the sea at the same time.
I ask Irina of her lattices and let my pocket listen in.
She flagged the very sums I'm after. She knows where they've been.

[Pre-chorus]
A pad that gives me one guess, and a safe that keeps his year,
the minder slips his watch off, and his hand's the size of a spade.
HaX lays out what each road costs and leaves the choosing here,
so take my hand across the floor and show me what you've made.

[Chorus]
Follow the money,
you change your coat at every door,
you come in as a ransom and you go out as a war.
Follow the money,
the only honest thing I've found:
you always go where you were sent, and you write the dead ones down.

[Bridge]
He leans against his desk and asks if I have ever wondered.
Sometimes. I chose my side the night the first ward's lights went under.
I could close my fist on you tonight, or tie a thread and let you fly,
and both of them cost someone. Both are mine to justify.
I follow every step I'm taught, and every step comes true,
as if the one who wrote the steps had walked this road with you.

[Final chorus, up a key]
Follow the money,
you're signed and waiting at the door,
you came in as a ransom and you're going out a war.
Follow the money,
you're the only honest thing I've found,
you always went where you were sent. Now I write the dead ones down.

[Outro]
Depends who's asking.
I'm asking.
Follow the money.

## Notes
- **Tradition:** a blend. The verses and bridge are intimate, in the *Skyfall* manner; the chorus is a classic belted hook. Agent 0x00 sings to the money itself as a fellow spy: something that wears disguises, changes coats and slips away, the way the agent does under the FCA cover. Neither A (addressing the mark) nor B (inside Irina's head) has this voice. Nothing fixes the agent's name, gender or body.
- **Season position:** halfway through, tested and starting to doubt. The agent recognises the money from earlier missions. Verse 1 gives the m02 hospital's generator light and ransom deadline (Ransomware Incorporated's pooled hospital ransoms) and the m05 payment that bought "a frightened man" (Torres, via the Insider Threat Initiative). Bridge line 2 ties the agent's choice of side back to that first hospital.
- **Mission references:** the FCA supervisory cover, and the checkpoint guard's list. The chorus's "change your coat at every door" is the Bitcoin-to-Monero mixer. "Ransom… war" covers the $12.8M fund paying for "The Architect's Masterpiece", and "write the dead ones down" is the casualty projection. Also from the ink: Dani's "Depends who's asking" (reused for the outro) and the six price charts; Priya's graph of many wallets meeting one, timed together; the RFID cloner in the pocket while Irina talks about lattices; and Irina having flagged the transactions herself. After those come the one-try custody pad, the safe holding Satoshi's meaningful number, and Kemal taking off his watch with his shovel-sized hand. HaX's "I'll give you the cost of each. The call's yours" is in there too.
- **The choice:** freeze or watch appears only as "close my fist on you, or tie a thread and let you fly". "Mine to justify" answers Satoshi's "Everything justifies something". Bridge lines 1–2 are his "Do you ever wonder if we're right?" and the player's option "Sometimes. But I chose my side." "The only honest thing I've found" echoes Kemal on Satoshi: "He at least says what he is."
- **Twist (dramatic irony only):** "I follow every step I'm taught… as if the one who wrote the steps had walked this road with you." The agent means it as pride in good training. A player who opened Satoshi's safe knows Dr Tesseract, the likely Architect, wrote that training ("He wrote the training I gave you"). It names no one, and the agent sees through nothing. Nightshade is left out entirely, so there is no m08 hint.
- **Traps avoided:** none of A's or B's hooks or images (no "honey", drowning coins, "dark", cold slot, dead "in a cell", lanyard, martyr or "freeze it / watch it", "footprints lead back home", "the one who taught"). No Irina-turns outcome stated. No clock on the wall, no "darling", no flags as candles, no cursor. "Signed and waiting" is Satoshi's own line.
