# First Contact: Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
Eight floors of glass and a mural of gold,
A badge on a lanyard, a story you told.
You walked in with coffee, you walked in with lies,
An auditor's manners and nobody's eyes.
Every pin in the tumbler falls into line,
Every door has a number, and you've learned mine.

[Pre-chorus]
Somebody whispered. Somebody's scared.
A voice in your pocket says, "Careful in there."
And the clock on the wall's running down to the day:
Six on a Sunday. Who's going to pay?

[Chorus]
First contact!
One touch and there's no turning back.
I send you a warning, you open your heart,
And a whole frightened city comes tearing apart.
You'll believe me, believe me, the way that you do.
First contact,
And nobody checks if it's true.

[Verse 2]
There's a briefcase still waiting where Patricia stood;
She asked the right questions, they said she's no good.
The chalkboard keeps chattering, *think election year*,
The safe door goes quiet and the numbers come clear.
There's a name on the papers for when the walls fall,
And darling, it isn't my name at all.

[Pre-chorus]
The cursor is blinking, the flag's in the drop,
The voice in your pocket says, "Don't you stop."
And the clock on the wall's running down to the day:
Six on a Sunday. Who's going to pay?

[Chorus]
First contact!
One touch and there's no turning back.
I send you a warning, you open your heart,
And a whole frightened city comes tearing apart.
You'll believe me, believe me, the way that you do.
First contact,
And nobody checks if it's true.

[Bridge]
So here we are, darling, the lights burning low,
A box in my hand and a code you don't know.
Take me in anger, or take me in chains,
Or sell me to papers who'll spell out my name.
But I never drew up the plans that I keep;
There's an architect somewhere who never will sleep.

[Final chorus, up a key]
First contact!
You found me, and there's no way back.
You read all my letters, you counted my dead,
Forty-two, eighty-five, and you stood in their stead.
So go on and believe it, the way that you do.
First contact,
This time somebody checked it was true.

[Outro]
First contact...
They never forget the first.

## Notes
- The narrator is Derek Lawson, singing to the undercover agent, which mirrors his confrontation line "I've been watching you... You're not an IT contractor." The opening verse sets the arrival: eight floors of glass, the paid-for mural, coffee and lanyards, the visitor badge and office key from Sarah, the lockpick pins, and keypad codes ("every door has a number").
- The chorus sings Operation Shatter's method as seduction. A warning lands, people open their hearts, the city panics. "Nobody checks if it's true" is Derek's "verify everything" thesis. The final chorus turns it round: the agent did check, which fits the mission's lesson and SAFETYNET's win.
- In the pre-choruses, "Somebody whispered. Somebody's scared" is Maya's anonymous tip. "A voice in your pocket" is Agent HaX on the phone. "The flag's in the drop" is the drop-site terminal. "Six on a Sunday" is the launch time.
- Verse 2 runs through the investigation: Patricia's briefcase left behind after the "performance issues" firing, the chalkboard safe hint ("think election year"), and the casualty projections (42 to 85) coming clear. The bridge has the launch device and the memorised code, and its three lines offer the confrontation's choices (fight, arrest, expose) in passing.
- Twist hints: "There's a name on the papers for when the walls fall, / and darling, it isn't my name at all" foreshadows Derek's contingency plan to frame Kevin, without naming him. "There's an architect somewhere who never will sleep" points to The Architect behind Derek and the cells still out there.
- The title sings at the head of each chorus as a belted two-note hit ("First con-TACT!"). It also carries a double meaning: the agent's first mission, and the first touch of a lie that people believe.
