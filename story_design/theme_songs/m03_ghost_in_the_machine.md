# Ghost in the Machine

## Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
You came in at three with a borrowed name,
A visitor's badge and a hungry aim,
I drew you my network in marker blue
While the light in your pocket drank me through.

[Pre-chorus]
So stand a little closer, darling, by the board,
Ask me about the subnet, let my voice be your reward,
Everything breaks, love, and I've set a price on you.

[Chorus]
You're a ghost in the machine,
Wearing my name where my card has been,
Every door that I own swings open when you pass,
Cold as a whisper, and clean as glass.
Ghost in the machine,
Silent as money, sharp and unseen,
You can walk through my walls, you can take what you find,
But a ghost leaves the cold behind.

[Verse 2]
Eleven o'clock and the hallway's grey,
A guard going round in his torch-lit way,
You picked what was keyed and you wore what was carded,
You turned every page of the books I kept guarded.
A man in the dark with his coat on, alone,
Staring at a photo and a silent phone.
Twenty-five thousand, the hospital priced in,
And I sleep like the dead, love. That's my only sin.

[Pre-chorus 2]
Don't linger in my doorway, stranger, I can see,
At night my window's a mirror and it gives you back to me,
There's a car in Callaghan Square, and you've exactly one move.

[Chorus]
You're a ghost in the machine,
Wearing my name where my card has been,
Every door that I own swings open when you pass,
Cold as a whisper, and clean as glass.
Ghost in the machine,
Silent as money, sharp and unseen,
You can walk through my walls, you can take what you find,
But a ghost leaves the cold behind.

[Bridge]
I sold one Ghost a key, and it opened a ward,
Now another walks my halls with a copy of my card.
Turn me or take me or watch the lift doors close,
I'm only a desk, and the Architect knows
St. Catherine's was a rehearsal, my dear.
Wait for the winter, when the lights disappear.

[Final chorus (up a key)]
You're the ghost in the machine,
Wearing my name where my card has been,
Every lock that I own lets you through like a friend,
But a ghost never chooses the end.
Ghost in the machine,
Somebody's hand on a switch unseen,
You can haunt my house, darling, take all you can find,
But a ghost leaves the cold behind.

### Notes
- Victoria Sterling (Sable) sings to the agent throughout. Verse 1 is the three o'clock interview: the visitor badge, the cover as a hungry freelance recruit, the network diagram on the whiteboard, and the cloner's light through the pocket reading her card (her night-scene line about the pocket lighting up). "Ask me about the subnet" is the player keeping her talking at the board while the card reads.
- "Wearing my name where my card has been" and "you wore what was carded" come from Nightshade's "we copy it, we wear it" and HaX's "picks for anything keyed, the cloner for anything carded". Verse 2 is the night half: the eleven o'clock return, the night lights, Terry Mott's torch and his rounds, and the paper trail of the transaction log and catalogue.
- "A man in the dark with his coat on, alone / a photo and a silent phone" is Danny Foster's office before the player's choice. "Twenty-five thousand, the hospital priced in" is invoice ZDS-2024-0847's healthcare premium. "I sleep like the dead" twists her "I sleep perfectly" against the St. Catherine's deaths.
- The title works both ways in the bridge: Zero Day sold the m02 Ghost the key that took down the ward, and now the agent is a second ghost inside Zero Day's own building. "Everything breaks" in the first pre-chorus is her "everything breaks in the end... I call it stock", which nods to ENTROPY.
- Hints with no spoilers: the window-as-mirror and "exactly one move" foreshadow that she has seen the agent coming and is waiting, coat on. "Turn me or take me or watch the lift doors close" lays out the turn, arrest or escape choice without picking one. "I'm only a desk", "the Architect knows" and "wait for the winter, when the lights disappear" hint at the Phase 2 directive (a winter grid attack) and her "proof of concept" line. "Somebody's hand on a switch unseen" points at the Architect.
- No technical detail. The flags, terminal and services only show up as doors, walls and "books".

## Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
You came in on a visitor's badge
with a story you'd rehearsed,
a freelance hand who'd sell a hole
to whoever asked it first.
I drew my network on the board
in blue and patient lines,
and you stood so close to hear me talk
I could almost hear your mind.

[Pre-chorus]
Something in your pocket hums
like a moth against the glass.
I keep talking. Let it come.
Every lock forgets at last.

[Chorus]
Everything breaks, and I get there first,
I put a price upon the fall.
I know your face, I don't know your name,
you read the writing on my wall,
a ghost in the machine, a ghost in the machine,
and the machine was me.

[Verse 2]
Eleven strikes on Callaghan Square,
the night guard walks his round,
my borrowed card in a stranger's hand
lets the doors give without a sound.
There's a man down the hall with his coat still on
beside the map he drew;
I paid him well to call it work
and never ask me who.

[Pre-chorus]
Little flags go up in the dark,
vigils for a ward.
I read that bill down to the pound
and I'd sign it all once more.

[Chorus]
Everything breaks, and I get there first,
I put a price upon the fall.
I know your face, I don't know your name,
you read the writing on my wall,
a ghost in the machine, a ghost in the machine,
and the machine was me.

[Bridge]
To the one upstairs I'm a line on a page,
a desk they'll fill by Monday.
Sable's a coat I can leave on a chair.
I've a car below and a bag by the door
and a single move to play,
and winter's coming down the wires
whatever deal you weigh.
So choose, little ghost: will you bar the door,
or let me walk away?

[Final chorus, lift]
Everything breaks, and I got there first,
but nobody prices their own fall.
I know your face and I'll learn your name,
you took the writing off my wall,
a ghost in the machine, a ghost in the machine,
and the machine runs on without me.

[Outro]
Everything breaks.
I call it stock.

### Notes
- Sung from inside Victoria Sterling (Sable), WhiteHat's CEO, to the recruit she half-suspects; the "ghost" is first the agent haunting her building, then her own realisation that she is one replaceable part of the Architect's machine.
- Verse 1 and the first pre-chorus are the afternoon meeting: the Visitor Badge, the freelance-pentester cover, the conference whiteboard network diagram, and the RFID cloner reading her card while the player stands close and keeps her talking ("something in your pocket hums").
- "Read the writing on my wall" nods to the encoded whiteboards; Verse 2 is the night run (eleven o'clock, Callaghan Square in Cardiff, Terry's patrol ("the night guard walks his round"), the cloned card opening doors) and Danny Foster in his dark office with his coat on beside the GHOST hospital folder he drew.
- "Little flags go up in the dark, vigils for a ward" ties the VM flags at the drop-site terminal to St. Catherine's; "I read that bill down to the pound" echoes her line that she read what the ward cost and it changed nothing.
- The bridge carries her late-night ending without saying which way it goes: "a desk they'll fill by Monday", "Sable's a coat", the car downstairs and packed bag, "one move", so she could be turned, taken or gone. "Winter's coming down the wires" foreshadows Phase 2 and the grid.
- The outro quotes her recruitment pitch ("Some people call that entropy. I call it stock."); the listener supplies the missing word, ENTROPY.

## Review

- **Version A:** a proper brassy Bassey address to the mark, with the title sung outright twice per chorus and a lot of canon packed in (three o'clock and eleven, the torch, keyed and carded, Danny's dark office, the lift doors, the winter). Two lines ran long and the second chorus had been cut to its back half to save words. Score 7 → 8.
- **Version B:** close and cold, from inside Sterling's head. "And the machine was me" turns the title onto the singer, and the last chorus ("runs on without me") pays off her "I'm a desk" line without quoting it. The chorus sticks after one hearing, the outro gives the listener the word ENTROPY to fill in, and canon is accurate down to Danny's map and the raise she paid him. One filler line in the bridge and one repeated word. Score 8 → 9.
- **Changes made:**
  - A, Verse 1: "While the light in your pocket drank me through and through." → "While the light in your pocket drank me through." The line ran about four syllables over the couplet above it, and "through and through" was padding.
  - A, Pre-chorus 1: "Everything breaks, love, and I've a price put by for you." → "Everything breaks, love, and I've set a price on you." "Put by" landed off the beat. The new line keeps the you/move echo with the second pre-chorus's tag line.
  - A, second chorus: restored the full eight-line chorus where the draft had only the back half ("Ghost in the machine, / Silent as money..."). It had been cut only to save words, and repeated chorus lines don't count towards the limit.
  - B, Pre-chorus 2: "I read the cost down to the last" → "I read that bill down to the pound". The first pre-chorus already ends "at last", and "bill" points at the twenty-five-thousand invoice. It still echoes her "I read what that ward cost, down to the number".
  - B, Bridge: "whatever you decide to say." → "whatever deal you weigh." The old line was filler (the choice is made in action, not speech). The new one brings in the turn-her option, so the bridge now holds all three endings (deal, bar the door, walk away) without picking one.
- **Stronger version:** B. Its title hook does double work (agent as ghost, Sterling as a part the Architect will replace), and its restraint suits a villain who "sleeps perfectly". A is a fine, louder companion piece.
- **Left for the user:** both versions have Sterling *drawing* the network diagram for the agent. In the ink the diagram is already on the whiteboard when the agent asks about it, so this is poetic licence, left in. A's bridge line "St. Catherine's was a rehearsal" paraphrases her "proof of concept" parting line, which only plays on the escape ending. It hints at Phase 2, which the briefing announces anyway, but cut it if you want that line kept for players who earn it.

### Cross-season pass

- A, Pre-chorus 2: "Don't linger in my doorway, darling, I can see," → "Don't linger in my doorway, stranger, I can see,". This takes A from three uses of "darling" to two. "Stranger" is Sterling's view of the agent (B's "I know your face, I don't know your name").
- B, Pre-chorus 2: "candles for a ward." → "vigils for a ward.". Candles come up in m06, m07 and m08 (Nightshade's "candle in a hurricane" is canon there). "Vigils" keeps the image of mourning St. Catherine's.
