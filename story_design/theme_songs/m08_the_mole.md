# The Mole

## Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
Every reader's blinking red inside the Citadel,
And nobody trusts the face at the next chair.
Four fires lit in Portland, one pail to fight them,
Someone sold the map before we got there.
Two of ours lie where the cold rain finds them,
And the moment he hears you hunting, he's thin air.

[Pre-chorus]
Oh, he's calm, so calm, when the whole floor's shaking,
He made his peace with how it ends,
And the colleague at your shoulder
Is the one you'll call a friend.

[Chorus]
The Mole!
He's the stillness at the centre of the storm,
He's been digging underneath you fifteen years.
He'll hold the door, he'll keep your welcome warm,
Then sell your name to the dark and dry your tears.
He leaves one door ajar and lets the whole night through,
The Mole... and he's waiting there for you.

[Verse 2]
Three names typed upon the Director's paper:
A token, a ticket, and a flower that blooms at night.
Pick the lock, lift the thumbprint off the silver,
Hold the door's own diary to the light:
It knows who walked in at ten-fifteen, love,
But it never says what happened in between.

[Pre-chorus]
Oh, he's calm, so calm, when the whole floor's shaking,
He kept the thing he signed out,
He'll smile across the desk at you
While you're picking out the doubt.

[Chorus]
The Mole!
He's the stillness at the centre of the storm,
He's been digging underneath you fifteen years.
He'll hold the door, he'll keep your welcome warm,
Then sell your name to the dark and dry your tears.
He leaves one door ajar and lets the whole night through,
The Mole... and he's waiting there for you.

[Bridge]
A warning in the safe is sealed and turned to the wall.
He'll say order is a candle in a hurricane,
And he only helped the wind to take it all.
Don't rule out the one who wrote the list.
Choose the cell, or choose the leash:
He'll hand you a tomb beneath the Montana snow
And never lose his peace.

[Final chorus, up a key]
The Mole!
He's the stillness at the centre of your storm,
He was digging underneath you all those years.
He held the door, he kept your welcome warm,
He sold your name, and now he drinks your tears.
He left one door ajar and let the whole night through,
The Mole... and he's sitting next to you.

[Outro, near-spoken]
Every reader's burning red, love,
And somebody still walked through.

### Notes
- Verse 1 sets the scene from ATHENA's briefing: Level Red badge readers, nobody trusting the next desk, Portland's four sites with one team ("four fires, one bucket"), the two dead agents, and "he's thin air" is ATHENA's "the moment they know you are hunting, they vanish".
- Verse 2 runs through the investigation as imagery: the Director's three names (Vector's hardware token, Phantom's travel pack and ticket, and a night flower for Nightshade, all three given equal weight), lockpicking, the thumbprint lifted from the USB stick, and the door audit, the door's "diary" (in at 10:15). "It never says what happened in between" turns his own defence ("A door says who walked in") against him.
- Pre-chorus 2: everyone on the planning list keeps something signed out that they shouldn't (ATHENA), so "the thing he signed out" fits all three suspects. The calm is the tell that ATHENA and the insider briefing flag, and the game flags it openly too. The "door ajar" line is the threat-database theft hidden behind the Portland attacks.
- The bridge covers Dr Okafor's psych evaluation sealed in the Director's safe, Nightshade's "candle in a hurricane" philosophy (presented as his pitch, not endorsed), the arrest-or-triple-agent choice (cell or leash), and Tomb Gamma, the Montana bunker. A mole going underground gives the double meaning.
- Whodunit: before the mission, "the one who wrote the list" reads as the Director, who typed the three names, which makes it a red herring. After the mission it's Nightshade's own interview line. The final chorus ends on "sitting next to you", which could mean any colleague. The outro goes back to the Level Red readers of verse 1: the building is locked down and the leak still got out.
- The chorus is a knowing nod to the "Goldfinger, he's the man" shape, with the title belted on its own as the downbeat.

## Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
Every badge light in the building's burning red,
and no one meets the eyes across the desk.
I keep my hands flat on the table, coffee warm;
a quiet man's the one they never test.
The thing I never handed back sleeps beside my lamp.
I chose which doors you'd open the long way.
Forty-seven minutes with a plan nobody signed,
and a cursor that remembers where I stayed.

[Pre-chorus]
You'll come and find me here tonight
and ask me where I've been.
I'll give you nothing but the truth
and leave out what it means.

[Chorus]
So who is the mole?
Go on and ask me. Everybody asks.
Fifteen years I kept the candle cupped
and wore my calm the way you'd wear a mask.
Who is the mole?
Every word I've told you has been sound.
I never said where I had learned it.
I learned it underground.

[Verse 2]
Four fires in the dark and one bucket to throw;
I did the sum of who, and didn't look away.
At the wake I passed a tissue to the one who cried the most
and let her grieve the friends I gave away.
While you chose which fire to fight, the vault walked out the door,
the one I left unlatched and didn't mention.
The storm was always going to win. I only helped the wind,
and called it honesty, and called it vision.

[Pre-chorus]
A door can tell you who came in,
not what he did inside.
I passed that little light each dawn
and never dreamed it testified.

[Chorus]
So who is the mole?
Go on and ask me. Everybody asks.
Fifteen years I kept the candle cupped
and wore my calm the way you'd wear a mask.
Who is the mole?
Every word I've told you has been sound.
I never said where I had learned it.
I learned it underground.

[Bridge]
I wiped the stick and left my thumb.
Fifteen years, and I forgot my thumb.
There's a passport in the lining and a flight I never took;
a man who runs confesses, so I stayed to watch you look.
Now choose: a cell, or a leash.
Keep me as your ghost inside their machine
for just as long as it suits me.
I'll give you a bunker in the snow
and the man who dug me in.

[Final chorus, lift]
So who is the mole?
You finally asked. You'd known it all somehow.
Fifteen years I kept the candle cupped.
Look, the wind is in the room with us now.
Who is the mole?
Every word I've told you has been sound,
and here's the last of them for free:
some things grow underground.

[Outro]
Let it hurt afterwards,
not during.
Close the door gently.
The whole floor's listening.

### Notes
- The narrator is the mole, never named, and nothing in the song fixes which suspect he is. "Every word I've told you has been sound" echoes his confession ("Every word I taught you was true") for anyone who has played it, but before the mission it could be any of the three men the player interviews.
- Verse 1: ATHENA's Level Red opening, calm hands flat on the table, the kit everyone on the planning list keeps and shouldn't (ATHENA's line, true of all three), and the forty-seven minutes on the Portland plan, sung as a cursor rather than a log.
- Candle and wind come from his confession ("Order is a candle in a hurricane... I helped the wind"). "Four fires, one bucket" and "the sum of who" are his Portland arithmetic. The tissue at the wake is HaX's line about the debrief. The vault going out an unlatched door is the threat-database theft hidden behind the four attacks.
- Pre-chorus 2 is the door audit: "I walked past that reader every morning... never once thought of it as a witness."
- Bridge: the fingerprint on the USB stick ("forgot my thumb"), the go-bag with a passport and the flight he never took (before the mission this leans towards Phantom, the traveller, which is fair misdirection), the moral choice (police cell or triple-agent leash), his "ghost inside their machine" warning, and Tomb Gamma as a bunker in the snow along with the Architect who placed him.
- The outro takes his parting line to 0x00 and the floor that hears every accusation, which is the tip-off mechanic.

## Review

- **Version A:** big, brassy and singable, with a chorus that lands the title on the downbeat and a Verse 2 that names all three suspects equally. But it pointed at the mentor three times ("the lessons that he gave you", "the key you went without" at his "bench", "he taught it all to you"), and choruses 1 and 2 had lost the "door ajar" line. **7/10 → 8.5/10**
- **Version B:** the better poem: a close first-person voice, real double meanings (candle and wind, "I learned it underground") and a quietly chilling bridge. As drafted it named Nightshade before the mission started. The narrator is the mole, and he teaches the player (Netherton says "He taught your intake" during the briefing, and m05 sets it up), he keeps the PIN box (ATHENA ties it to Nightshade in the opening), and he works in a lab. **7.5/10 → 9/10**
- **Whodunit check:** both drafts handed the player the answer before the mission. The game reveals early that Nightshade is the one who taught the player, so any "teacher" line names him. Calm alone is fine, because the game flags it openly as a clue that can mislead. The lines that remain all fit Vector and Phantom as well, or point at the Director or Phantom as red herrings. They only point at Nightshade once you've played.
- Changes made (A):
  - "And the lessons that he gave you / Are the ones you'll need again" → "And the colleague at your shoulder / Is the one you'll call a friend" (removes the mentor pointer; the menace now covers anyone at the next desk).
  - "He kept the key you went without" → "He kept the thing he signed out" (the PIN cracker is Nightshade's; ATHENA says all three keep something signed out).
  - "across the bench" → "across the desk" (the bench puts him in the lab).
  - Final chorus "and he taught it all to you" → "and he's sitting next to you" (the most direct pointer in A; the new line keeps the sting and points at no one in particular).
  - Choruses 1 and 2: restored "He leaves one door ajar and lets the whole night through" (it was cut only to save words; repeated chorus lines don't count).
  - Kept "a flower that blooms at night" (one of three equal name-tokens, not a guilt marker) and "Don't rule out the one who wrote the list" (in the song it points at the Director, a red herring).
- Changes made (B):
  - Chorus "Go ask the one who taught you how to ask" → "Go on and ask me. Everybody asks." (the direct mentor pointer; "asks/mask" still rhymes, and the shrug keeps the menace).
  - Chorus "Every lesson that I gave you was sound" → "Every word I've told you has been sound" (fits any suspect the player interviews; it still echoes his confession for anyone who has played).
  - Final chorus "You finally asked the one who taught you how" → "You finally asked. You'd known it all somehow." (the same spoiler repeated; the new line rhymes with "now").
  - Final chorus "here's the last of them for free" kept; "them" now refers to words.
  - "hands flat on the bench" → "on the table" (his own line in the ink, and no lab).
  - "The little box that cracks a keypad sleeps beside my lamp" → "The thing I never handed back sleeps beside my lamp" (the PIN box is Nightshade's by name in ATHENA's opening).
  - "You'll come down to my lab tonight" → "You'll come and find me here tonight" (the lab puts him in the Crypto Lab).
- **Stronger version: B.** Its inside-the-mole voice suits a whodunit best now that the clues no longer settle the question, and the "underground" turn is the best hook in either song.
- **Left for the user:** A still says "ten-fifteen" (the true door-in time, and a wrong answer to the audit question, so it misleads if anything); cut it if you'd rather the song quoted no evidence values. Both songs still lean on "calm", which the game itself treats as a fair, misleading clue.

### Cross-season pass

The m08 review's whodunit balance is unchanged. With m05's "calm ones back at home" gone, nothing earlier in the season ties calm, a teacher or a briefer to the leak, so the earlier songs no longer add up to the answer before m08. Pre-m08 "teacher" lines all point at HaX and Tesseract, and the player only learns that Nightshade "taught your intake" from Netherton in this mission.

- A, Outro: "Let it hurt when it's over, / Not while you're in the room." → "Every reader's burning red, love, / And somebody still walked through." This was nearly the same couplet as m07 B's bridge ("Let it hurt you when it's over / Not tonight…"), and HaX's line was in six songs. B's outro keeps it word for word as Nightshade's parting line. The new A outro goes back to verse 1's Level Red readers: the lockdown held and the leak still got out.
- Noted, not changed: "He'll hold the door" (chorus) echoes m02's insider. "One door ajar" and B's "unlatched" go with doors left open in m04, m10 and (before this pass) m09.
