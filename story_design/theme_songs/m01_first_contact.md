# First Contact

## Version A (classic)

[Verse 1]
Eight floors of glass and a mural of gold,
A badge on a lanyard, a story you told.
You walked in with coffee, you walked in with lies,
An auditor's manners and nobody's eyes.
Every pin in the tumbler falls into line,
Every door has a number, and you've learned mine.

[Pre-chorus]
Somebody whispered. Somebody's scared.
A voice in your pocket says, "Careful in there."
And the clock on the wall's running down to the day:
Six on a Sunday. Who's going to pay?

[Chorus]
First contact!
One touch and there's no turning back.
I send you a warning, you open your heart,
And a whole frightened city comes tearing apart.
You'll believe me, believe me, the way that you do.
First contact,
And nobody checks if it's true.

[Verse 2]
There's a briefcase still waiting where Patricia stood;
She asked the right questions, they said she's no good.
The chalkboard keeps chattering, *think election year*,
The safe door goes quiet and the numbers come clear.
There's a name on the papers for when the walls fall,
And darling, it isn't my name at all.

[Pre-chorus]
The cursor is blinking, the flag's in the drop,
The voice in your pocket says, "Don't you dare stop."
And the clock on the wall's running down to the day:
Six on a Sunday. Who's going to pay?

[Chorus]
First contact!
One touch and there's no turning back.
I send you a warning, you open your heart,
And a whole frightened city comes tearing apart.
You'll believe me, believe me, the way that you do.
First contact,
And nobody checks if it's true.

[Bridge]
So here we are, agent, the lights burning low,
A box in my hand and a code you don't know.
Take me in anger, or take me in chains,
Or sell me to papers who'll spell out my name.
I chose every line of the plans that I keep,
But there's an architect somewhere who never will sleep.

[Final chorus, up a key]
First contact!
You found me, and there's no way back.
You read all my letters, you counted my dead,
Forty-two, eighty-five, and you stood in their stead.
So go on and believe it, the way that you do.
First contact,
This time somebody checked it was true.

[Outro]
First contact...
They never forget the first.

### Notes
- The narrator is Derek Lawson, singing to the undercover agent, which mirrors his confrontation line "I've been watching you... You're not an IT contractor." Verse 1 sets the arrival from the opening narration (eight floors of glass, the paid-for mural, coffee and lanyards, nobody looking twice at the auditor), Sarah's visitor badge, the lockpick pins and the keypad codes.
- The chorus sings Operation Shatter's method as seduction: a warning lands, people open their hearts, the city panics. "Nobody checks if it's true" is Derek's "verify everything" thesis. The final chorus turns it round, since the agent did check.
- Pre-choruses: "Somebody whispered" is Maya's anonymous tip, "a voice in your pocket" is Agent HaX on the phone, "the flag's in the drop" is the drop-site terminal, "six on a Sunday" is the launch time.
- Verse 2 covers Patricia's briefcase after her firing, the chalkboard safe hint ("think election year") and the 42–85 casualty projections. The bridge has the launch device and the memorised code, with the fight, arrest and expose endings in passing.
- Twist hints: "it isn't my name at all" foreshadows the frame-up of Kevin without naming him. The architect line points to The Architect, who approved the plan Derek chose for himself, and to the cells still out there.
- "First contact" means both the agent's first mission and the first touch of a lie that people believe.

## Version B (modern)

[Verse 1]
You came in with the morning crowd,
a clipboard and a borrowed name,
the auditor that no one saw.
I saw you all the same.
I watched you kneel beside the locks
and ease the pins in one by one;
you listen like a lover would.
I listened too, when I was young.

[Pre-chorus]
Every keypad has a number,
every number has a hiding place:
a voicemail on a desk phone,
a safe behind a smiling face.
Go on and find them. I don't mind.
I wrote *verify everything*
and buried it among the lies.

[Chorus]
This is first contact,
a message in the dark,
a voice you've never heard before
that knows just where to start.
It says the hospital is closed,
it tells you where to run,
and you send it to everyone you love
before the night is done.

[Verse 2]
I did the sums in pencil,
forty-two to eighty-five.
I rounded it to sixty
and I've never felt so alive.
The last one who looked closely
was walked out before noon;
her briefcase still sits waiting
in an empty corner room.
There's a tired man behind a keypad
with my sins beneath his name,
and someone whispering through the walls
I've never thought to blame.

[Pre-chorus]
Plant your little flags, contractor,
on the hill I've mined.
Your handler's in your pocket.
Mine is further up the line.

[Chorus]
This is first contact,
a message in the dark,
a voice you've never heard before
that knows just where to start.
It says the hospital is closed,
it tells you where to run,
and you send it to everyone you love
before the night is done.

[Bridge]
Just you and me and the server's glow,
one code between my teeth.
You can take me in, or take the stage,
or take me underneath.
Show the world the numbers.
Let them read what I designed.
Trust is the one lock left unbolted,
and it can't be patched in time.

[Final chorus]
This is first contact,
your first day in the field,
and I'm the first you've met, my love.
I won't be the last revealed.
There's an architect who drew this room
before you learned to pick,
and every cell's another window
when the glass begins to crack.
This is first contact.

[Outro]
Verify everything.
Trust no one.
Not the voice on the phone.
Not this song.

### Notes
- The narrator is Derek Lawson ("I've been watching you... the lockpicking"), watching the agent under the IT-contractor cover ("a lanyard and a borrowed name").
- Pre-chorus 1 points at the number trail: the IT room PIN on the voicemail at Sarah's reception phone, keypads, Patricia's safe. "Verify everything" is an entry in Derek's own password list as well as his creed. Pre-chorus 2 has the flags submitted at the drop-site terminal, Agent HaX on the phone, and The Architect as Derek's handler further up the line. "The hill I've mined" works both ways: data mined, ground rigged to blow.
- The chorus tells Operation Shatter as imagery: fake hospital-closure alerts, forwarded without checking, going out before dawn on Sunday.
- Verse 2 holds the 42–85 projection Derek "rounded to sixty", and Patricia Wells walked out with her briefcase left behind. "A tired man behind a keypad / with my sins beneath his name" hints at the frame-up of Kevin (IT room keypad, reports filed in his name). "Someone whispering through the walls / I've never thought to blame" is dramatic irony about Maya, the informant Derek never spots.
- The bridge is the confrontation with the launch device and its memorised code. The three "take" lines map to the arrest, expose and fight endings. "Can't be patched" comes from The Architect's letter ("trust, unlike encryption, cannot be patched").
- The final chorus and outro foreshadow the season: other cells, The Architect at large, and "trust no one" turned on the song itself.

## Review

- **Version A:** a proper belter. The title lands as a two-note hit, "nobody checks if it's true" sticks after one hearing, and the final chorus flips it into a payoff. Canon is accurate throughout. The faults were one short line in pre-chorus 2 and a bridge line that let Derek off the hook. Score 7.5 → 8.5.
- **Version B:** intimate and chilling. "I rounded it to sixty / and I've never felt so alive" is the best moment in either song. It was let down by an identical rhyme (name/name), an unrhymed pre-chorus that put The Architect *below* Derek, a bridge opener copied almost word for word from A, and a cut-down second chorus. Score 7 → 8.
- **Changes made:**
  - A, pre-chorus 2: "says, 'Don't you stop.'" → "says, 'Don't you dare stop.'" The line was a beat short of its partner "says, 'Careful in there.'"
  - A, bridge: "But I never drew up the plans that I keep; / There's an architect somewhere…" → "I chose every line of the plans that I keep, / But there's an architect somewhere…" In the ink Derek says "The Architect opened my eyes. But I chose this path myself", and the casualty sums carry his signature. The draft made him a mere carrier.
  - B, pre-chorus 1: "Every door here has a number" → "Every keypad has a number". A uses "Every door has a number" in verse 1, so the two voices sounded alike. "Keypad" is also more concrete.
  - B, verse 2: "who thinks I've never heard her name" → "I've never thought to blame". This fixes the name/name identical rhyme. The draft line also implied Derek knows about Maya, which contradicts both the ink and the author's own note.
  - B, pre-chorus 2: "on the hill I made" → "on the hill I've mined". The draft had no rhyme (made/line); the new word rhymes and carries a double meaning.
  - B, pre-chorus 2: "Mine is further down the line" → "further up the line". The Architect is Derek's superior.
  - B, second chorus: restored the dropped "It says the hospital is closed, / it tells you where to run," per the coordinator's ruling that repeated choruses don't count toward the word limit.
  - B, bridge: "So here we are, the lights turned low" → "Just you and me and the server's glow". A's bridge opens with nearly the same line. The new one also places the scene.
  - B, final chorus: "your first night in the field" → "your first day in the field". The mission opens with the morning crowd arriving, and no ink puts it at night.
- **Stronger version:** A, by a little. Its chorus is the more memorable hook and its final chorus has a real turn, which a title song needs. B has the best single verse and would suit an end-credits slot.
- **Left for the user:** A quotes the chalkboard hint "think election year" word for word. It's the clue, not the answer, so I kept it, but you may want it vaguer. Neither song touches the recruit ending, which seems fine for a title song.

### Cross-season pass

Read alongside the other nine missions. Changes cut repeats that build up over the season.

- A, Bridge: "So here we are, darling, the lights burning low," → "So here we are, agent, the lights burning low,". "Darling" turns up in eight of the ten missions. A keeps the better one ("And darling, it isn't my name at all"), and "agent" is Derek's "You're not an IT contractor" reveal.
- B, V1: "a lanyard and a borrowed name," → "a clipboard and a borrowed name,". Lanyards turn up in seven songs. A clipboard suits the auditor cover, and A already has "a badge on a lanyard".
- B, V2: "I did the sums by lamplight," → "I did the sums in pencil,". Lamps recur (m02 B, m07 A, m08 B). Pencil is a better image for a man about to round 42–85 down to sixty.
- B, Pre-chorus 2: "Plant your little flags, my darling," → "Plant your little flags, contractor,". The B versions otherwise avoid "darling", and Derek sneering at the cover is in character.
- Noted, not changed: "the clock on the wall" stays here and was removed from m07 A and m10 A.
