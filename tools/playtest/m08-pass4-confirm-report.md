## Notes for the orchestrator
1. C4 wording: a wrong card pick alone does not send HaX's "A print off the stick..." text. The lift is recorded (event `fingerprint_collected:nightshade`) only when the player logs it: "Log as unidentified" after a wrong pick, or "Log the print" after a match (biometrics.js:88-99). Behaviour matches the scenario comment (any lift gets the hedged text, only a match sets `nightshade_print_lifted`); the script's "pick a wrong card" should say "pick a wrong card, then Log as unidentified".
2. The confrontation chat cannot be closed with the x (it is a cutscene-style chat) and the opening lines auto-advance, so a tester that polls slowly loses the first lines. Under load this made the first look at C7's opening lines unreliable; hence game 1414.
3. C6 "before any guide is offered" was observed right after the three interviews (before the server room), where the HaX hub has no field-guide option; the option appears once a guide has been offered (server room entry) and disappears after all offered guides are taken.
4. Reload behaviour: the first reload went straight back into the game; the second showed "RESUME SESSION?" and the harness's bootstrap kept clicking "Prev" on the "OTHER SESSIONS" block for 7 minutes (load average 30-40 from other agents' browsers). Clicking Resume through the DOM fixed it. Saved-card and Emulate items in the RFID flipper are not harness controls (DOM click only).
5. The Phantom portrait now shows shirt and tie in both the chat bust and the world sprite; the off-duty agent in the break room is a grey hoodie.
