## Puzzle experience (Custody Console, game 1370)

- Time: files first opened 02:07:55, correct slot accepted 02:09:09, so about 1 min 15 s of wall clock, including reading all three files, one HaX nudge and one decoy. That is an agent with arithmetic on tap; a human with pen and paper should expect something like 4 to 8 minutes. The pre-reading (Priya, HaX hints) took about 6 minutes of the run.
- Clues used: Priya's conversation (method: "the right amount less the cut, landing after the hold... the same slot doing it three times"); her write-up (three deposits with date, time, amount); mixer_pool_config.yml (1.5% fee, 6 h minimum dwell); mixer_settlements.log; custody_index.csv only to confirm 4471 exists. Working: 2,400,000 x 0.985 = 2,364,000, which appears at 15 Jul 01:15 slot 3815 (3 h 35 min after the deposit, fails the hold) and 15 Jul 04:20 slot 4471 (passes). 1,182,000 on 30 Jul 05:05 and 669,800 on 04 Aug 03:35 both slot 4471. Decoys: 3815 (right amount, too soon), 2207 at 01:50 (right amount, too soon), 6120 exactly $1,200,000 (no fee taken).
- Nudge: asked "Which cold slot is the fund?" once. It restated the method and ended with "One slot keeps turning up." That is close to the answer already; it is more a confirmation than a hint ladder. Priya's talk and HaX's data-centre text and recap had already said the same thing three times, so by the time I reached the files I knew the method and the nudge was not needed.
- Decoy 3815 rejected with the standard "PIN Rejected: Failed to enter correct PIN." toast. The harness `lock` helper typed it 3 times (it retries); no lockout occurred and the keypad came back with 0 attempts.
- Fun or tedious: fun and short. The decoys (too-early credit, gross-amount match) make the dwell check matter, and "same slot three times" is a satisfying confirm. Tedious parts are the hand arithmetic (three multiplications by 0.985) and cross-reading three files plus a write-up from the inventory; there is no in-game calculator or notepad prompt. It is hard to get lost because the method is spelled out; it is more a worked exercise than a deduction. If the design intent was "discover the method", the clue density is high; if it was "apply the method", it fits.

## Top 5 player-experience notes

1. The follow-the-money puzzle works and is fair: decoys are meaningful (dwell check, gross vs net amount), the answer is verifiable three times, and a wrong slot costs nothing. But the method is stated by Priya, by HaX's data-centre text, by the recap and by the nudge, so it reads as a guided exercise rather than a discovery. Consider letting Priya give the principle only and leaving the dwell/fee wording to the config.
2. The first nudge ("One slot keeps turning up") is almost the answer. A two-step ladder (method, then "start with the 2.4M deposit") would help someone who really is stuck without spoiling it.
3. Turning Irina is cheap and bypasses the puzzle entirely: write-up plus one dialogue and she gives slot and safe code. That matches the design (credits name it), but there is no visible trust gate; a first-time player could skip the headline puzzle by accident.
4. HaX's texts are well timed and well written this pass: the cracking guide stays hidden until the terminal, the flag-3 text bundles "export, north door, finish the estate", the flag-4 and wing-entry texts land once each, and the reload recap is accurate. The phone hub is now long enough (15 options) that the useful ones sit below the fold.
5. The debrief and credits react to the route (traced vs given, lend vs clone, notes, Monero letter correction, freeze vs watch, Dani and Priya variants). The seams show only on the KO branch and in a few doubled lines.
