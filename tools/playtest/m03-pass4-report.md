## Player-experience notes (top 5)
1. **The night cutscene reads cleanly but sits oddly.** It auto-advances in about 4 s per line with Skip available; the narrator is drawn with the female_spy talk portrait (woman in a fedora and trench coat) on a magenta matrix background with a large empty framed box on the left. A new player may ask who she is. The words themselves ("walk into the afternoon with the last of the visitors", "Eleven o'clock that night...", "a guard's footsteps go round, and round again") are good. It played once, did not replay after reload, re-entry, or a second reload (run A2).
2. **HaX's "You're in" text arrives about 10 s after the scene closes** (run B) and in the toast only; it is easy to miss while walking. After the cutscene the player has no on-screen prompt about where the server room is until that text lands. In run A there was a 6 s gap too.
3. **Guard flow is the clearest tutorial in the mission but the excuse ladder's "I must have left it at my desk" is a dead end that leads straight to "Be honest"**, which is good; what reads badly is that honesty buys no safety at the door (defect 2) and HaX's pick warning ("Wait until his back's turned...") only arrives after the player has already spoken to him and walked up.
4. **Directive question is guessable.** The wrong answers cost nothing ("You're still a layer down. Run it again."), the right one is one of three on screen, and "I haven't got it to read yet" is always available. A player can brute-force it without decoding. Nothing is gated on it, so this only matters for the "took both layers off it yourself" credit line.
5. **Hint menu works well.** Every topic stays offered and repeats cleanly (asked the password topic twice, identical text, menu unchanged). The password hint says the founding year is on the plaque; I never read the plaque, so the 2010 clue chain was not earned in this run.
