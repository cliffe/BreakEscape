## Player-experience notes
1. Phantom's portrait is a hi-vis-vest worker, which reads as facilities, not an intelligence analyst. It is distinct from the Director's sprite, as asked.
2. After a reload the "RESUME SESSION?" overlay lists Resume / Restart / New Session beside Load session #1-#6 (other games). A real player has to pick Resume; nothing says the clone survives it, but it does.
3. The server-room reader after a reload opens as a Flipper menu (Read / Saved). A player who has forgotten they saved a card has to know to go Saved > Director Netherton Keycard > Emulate. No in-game prompt points there.
4. HaX toasts persist until "Clear all" and sit on top of the dusting panel and the printer panel; at small sizes they hide the buttons. They also made `interact` fail once with `blocking-ui` when I went to submit flag 3.
5. The dusting compare step only offers two candidate cards (Director, Nightshade) after the pattern is classified as a whorl, so the "who" is nearly a coin-flip once the pattern is right, and the match is also announced. Fine for "fun, not hard", but the script's four candidates are not what shows.
6. Flag 4's HaX text ("Put whatever's left through the relay") reads wrongly when all four are in; and "I need a field guide" appears in HaX's menu before any guide has been offered.
7. Nightshade in the suite stands between two desks rather than sitting at the table the intro text describes.
