## Summary

The plan is strong and largely accurate. Every load-bearing engine claim I spot-checked holds against
the code (board modes, grantFact/evaluateReadiness, mail landing globals, scheduler delay, pretext
session, influence lockout/recoverTo, triage starter, playerSent tells, objectives/eventMappings, the
Tesseract patterns). The critical path is solvable with no hard dead ends. The main defect is that the
orchestrator's accepted resolution to Q1 (add the `test_account` task, 23 tasks) was never folded into
the plan's authoritative objectives table, which is what P6-E builds from, and the task it adds has a
solvability/clarity edge that the plan does not close.

Counts: **0 blocker, 2 major, 8 minor.**

## Verdict

**Changes required before build.** Resolve R1-M1 (fold `test_account` into the authoritative §4 table
and the counts) and R1-M2 (close or make legible the stuck state it creates), then apply the minors —
R1-m1 (the §5 opening contradiction) and R1-m7 (playtest coverage of the accepted task and the two
recoverable traps) are the ones most likely to bite a blind build or run. The design is otherwise sound
and solvable, and the engine audit is accurate. Not clear to start the P6-E/P6-F build while the two
majors are open (the brief's §11 gate: "no blocker or major open before the build starts").
