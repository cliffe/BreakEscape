## Route and stage-by-stage teaching check

Played the whole arc without the walkthrough recipes, using only the visible hints and the Case File /
composer "Stuck?" pointers. Each stage taught what the sheet says:

1. **Authorise.** RoE sheet sorts (work email In, personal addresses Out, service-desk line In), stop-rule
   = call the emergency contact, Gareth signs. CMA content accurate: Ruth — "That signature is the
   difference between a test and a crime. Without it, you're committing an offence under the Computer
   Misuse Act." Gareth's "no names" condition set up the measure lesson.
2. **Plan.** Objective = find how far email + pretext get and report the gaps; measure = how far each
   attempt gets. The decoy measure "whether staff reported the attempts" is present and correctly
   taught as unmeasurable here.
3. **Recon.** Case File cards show gap hints with no answers named; "Stuck?" points to the source.
   Selecting a line records the fact. Careers-inbox line reveals Chris's Friendface. Hunter gives the
   address pattern; Q3.pdf Author (`mpierce`) gives the username convention. Decoys present and clear:
   the J. Baker plumbing firm in Leeds (name search), the Archive. All four OSINT tool concept cards
   exist (Hunter, theHarvester, Recon-ng, exiftool) and read accurately.
4. **Easy sends.** Careers inbox from a throwaway freemail (T. Test); Melanie from the Consultant
   account spoofing Jed Baker, greeting "Mel", Q3 balance sheet Friday, .xlsx attached. Both "landed".
   The "you can only send as yourself from this address — switch to the Consultant account" hint fires
   on the freemail account.
5. **Prepare for Sofia.** Dev pretext call: influence meter with thresholds (Wary -40, Refuses -50,
   Reports -70), opener verifies intern + team against the new-starter list (+10 CALM), two separate
   asks (portal/sender, then date). Page Builder: URL-reading teaching is strong (typo-squat, homograph
   shown as `xn--`, read-right-to-left subdomain, hover, padlock proves nothing). 100/100 with the
   homograph look-alike, crisp logo, matching wording, Email+Password only, padlock.
6. **Hard send.** Sofia as LedgerPay <payroll-notices@…>, greeting "Sofia" (the full "Sofia Lord" is
   rejected with a steer toward a shorter form), payslip pretext with LedgerPay + Monday the 6th,
   portal link attached. Landed.
7. **Report.** Results table + two questions (reporting can't be measured; "first send landed" shows
   whether it took several tries). Three findings each paired to a cause and a control; the blame
   options ("careless staff", "operator should not have", "intern too green") and the busy-looking
   controls (training, SPF-alone, password expiry, check the padlock) are all present as wrong answers.
   Gareth's payoff ties back to the opening near-miss, no names.
8. **Defend.** Triage of 8 messages — my two sends (SPF/DKIM/DMARC fail), a look-alike-domain phish
   that passes all three (`ledgerwell-hr`), a typo-squat (`office-suppiies`), a careers-application
   phish, a genuine list-forwarded mail (SPF fail but DKIM aligns → DMARC pass), the genuine supplier
   (`office-supplies`), and a genuine internal newsletter. Scored 88% "Sharp", 5/5 phish caught, 0 false
   alarms, with a per-skill breakdown. SOC board: the two recon exposures (address pattern → role
   inboxes; username metadata → strip metadata). Tomasz debrief: SPF/DKIM/DMARC recap accurate, the
   "two of those were mine" mirror, the Dev gap (callback, not blame), the ending warning, the budget
   choice, credits.

**Teaching accuracy for a technical audience:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC (glossary, triage explanations and
Tomasz's recap), the forwarding-breaks-SPF-but-DKIM-aligns case, the look-alike-domain-passes-all-checks
