## Harness notes

- `bootstrap` on a fresh game cleared the cutscene, repeat menu and tutorial prompt in one call and handled Ruth's opening. After the reload, `bootstrap` with `resume:"resume"` after clicking Resume through the DOM closed the title screen and notes in one call.
- `lock` bare (no key hint) tried each held key in turn: Recon door selected the Recon key; Ops door tried Recon then Operations; Blue Team door tried all three in order (seq 1109). All worked.
- `mg getState` text is cut short for long overlays (report findings, case file, triage message body); I read those with `eval` over `document.body.innerText`.
- Controls with no verb used: the mail account switcher (`select` with `create:freemail`), the compose `<select>`s by id, blank pickers through `clickControl` (they appear as `mail-picker-option`/`pretext-picker-option`), and the phone via an inventory-icon `eval` click.
- `clickControl` by label failed for labels containing a newline; use a substring from one line (a time stamp such as "08:06" selected the right triage row).
