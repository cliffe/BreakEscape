## Findings

**B1 (blocker). Ruth never hands over a key after her first open.** `ruth.ink` puts the three `#give_item` lines in `keys`, reached from `resume` "once per open". The engine does not re-enter `start` on a reopen: `person-chat-minigame.js` ~L509-533 restores Ruth's saved story and re-navigates to the same knot, which is `hub`. In 3072 and 3083, with `plan_done` true, her first reopen said "Need me?" and inventory held no Recon key; later with `osint_done` and `se_intel_username_convention` true she said "Something else?" and no Operations key. Ink variable `recon_key_given` stayed false. Calling `inkEngine.goToKnot("keys")` then `showCurrentDialogue()` from the console ran the real knot and gave the real key ("Plan holds up. Here's the Recon Room key."). So there is no in-game route to any of the three keys. Fix is in the ink (put the key hand-over inside `hub` as a gated first line or choice) or the engine, not the console. Same trap will hit the Blue Team key in L5.

**B2 (blocker). Ruth's phone hint hub is stuck on stage 0.** With `roe_signed`, `plan_done`, `osint_done`, both landings, the page and the call all true (story VARs confirmed current), the phone still opens at "On the sheet still?" with the stage-0 rungs ("Where do I start?", "A card won't go where I expect.", "What's the stop-rule about?"). Her thread was preloaded at game start; the saved story sits in knot `stage0`, whose rungs loop `-> stage0` and never return to `hub`, so the hub's stage routing never runs again. Every later stage's hint ladder is unreachable. Same root cause as B1.

**B3 (blocker). The `payroll_login` page can never pass.** `scenario.json.erb` ~L976-1010 has no `pretext` object on the landing page (`brandId`, `purposeId`, `facts`) and no `fields` or `extraSensitiveFields`; the slice's page (`test_social_slice/scenario.json.erb` L619-625) has them. In `landing-page-model.js` `scoreBrand` passes only when `choices.brand === pageDef.pretext.brandId`, so Brand always fails and the Page row says "this isn't what the email said would be here". Best score reachable is 75 (typo-squat or homograph domain, padlock on) with `gatesPassed:false`, `meetsThreshold:false`. The builder's "What Sofia is expecting" panel reads `pretext.facts`, so it says "You haven't found out what Sofia would expect to see yet" even after the call has granted both facts (checked in both orders). Lord's send with the best page fails `linkCredible`. Add the slice's `pretext` block to the lab page; L5 cannot pass until then.

**M1 (major). First screen of a pretext call shows empty blanks.** On the first call in 3072 the opening choice read "I'm helping , your new starter in ." (and option 2 "Sorry, she's in  — ..."), and the player's own bubble in the transcript read the same. `fillPretextVars` does run (story VARs held "Sofia Lord" and "Finance" when read), but after the first choices were rendered; after one pick the labels corrected ("I'm helping Sofia Lord, your new starter in Finance."). A second call in 3083 showed the right text. Not seen on the second call; the first-render case is the one to fix.

**m1.** Dev's portal answer is "Course. It's the staff portal, notices come from the payroll address." He never says a name or address. The student gets "Ledgerwell Payroll Portal" and `payroll-notices@...` only from the composer's picker, so the call reads as less useful than it is. The prep card still shows "Sofia uses a staff payroll portal (name not given)" after the fact is held, beside the new line "The staff payroll portal and how its notices read."

**m2.** The date ask comes back PENDING once ("I'd just want to check her name on the new-starter list before I read that out") even when the student has already given Sofia's name as the opener (-10 influence). Repeating opener 1 then asking again clears it. It works but is easy to read as a rebuke; the hint needs to say "say her name again".

**m3.** The Dev call can start with a cold ask (no opener): portal ask granted at -10 with no verification. Minor, but the opener is optional in practice.

**m4.** The board's Sofia entry says "Ready to approach — you have enough to craft something she'll believe" while the portal name and date are still unknown.

**Not bugs.** About 20 console "400 Bad Request" lines per run are the keyless server's TTS endpoint. `lock` printed `select key "Recon Room Key"` at the Operations door in 3072; the same door took the right key in 3083 and the Blue Team door refused Operations key with "Wrong key!", so keys are checked. `minigame_failed` appears on `End Conversation` of a person chat, phone chat and a refused lock; that is how the engine records those closes, not an overlay failure.

## Player-experience notes

- **Stage 4 before the call (L4a).** Score came back 62 to 75 with two FAIL rows (Brand, Page) that no menu choice can fix, and the only hint is the panel saying Sofia's expectations are unknown, with a "Case file" button. Nothing names the call as the way to find out. A student could easily conclude the builder is broken, and with B3 they would be right. The aim still completed because Attach anyway exists, which the student will probably do.
- **Stage 5 call.** This is the strongest part. The influence bar, the +10/-10 tells with reasons, and the end debrief ("You got what you came for ... Calling back on the number already on file defeats every line here.") all land. Dev is likeable and the learning point is clear. Asking out of order (cold ask, then verify) works.
- **Opening and sheet.** The briefing hub loops on "Need me?" until the student picks "I'll get on", which ends it. Space advances lines in dialogue; the harness's text clicks do not.
- **Mail client.** The "Create account" lives inside the account drop-down, easy to miss; the address options are radio buttons inside labels, so a text click misses them (harness issue, not necessarily the player's). The From/Greeting/Pretext blank pickers tell the student exactly which fact fills each slot ("a specific, insider detail"), which makes the Jed spoof nearly self-solving once `pierce_trusts_jed` is held.
- **Pacing (AI run).** Stage 3 finished at 23:43:51 and `payroll_page_attached` at 23:45:05 then `call_dev` at 23:46:15 in 3072 (about 2.5 minutes for stages 4 and 5 up to the Lord send); about 1m45s in 3083. A student will take longer, mostly reading the builder cards. The 7 and 11 minute budget lines are not tested here.
- **Page builder UX.** Case file opens as a layered overlay and Close returns to the builder (checked). Esc closes the builder as a normal exit.
