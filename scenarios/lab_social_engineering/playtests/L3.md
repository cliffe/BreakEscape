## Player-experience notes

- The opening reads well and the Fairbairn choice is arguable; the honest option is also the one that sounds right, so it is not much of a test.
- The scope cards and plan picks teach through their bounce-backs. I hit the bounces by accident and the text told me why in one line.
- OSINT is the satisfying part: each click prints a plain "Noted: ..." sentence, and the board filling is clear. The two traps (name search, archive) are easy to avoid by reading, which is perhaps too easy; I did not open either.
- The "see Ruth for the key" instruction is the weak link: there is no sign that you need to leave and re-enter the game. Ruth just says "Need me?".
- Creating the throwaway address is clear once found. The mail composer is dense (From, Subject, Greeting, Pretext, Ask, Attach, plus per-blank pickers), but each blank offers only facts you hold, which keeps it solvable.
- The Pierce spoof lands in one go and the checklist explains why in four lines. The advisory about DMARC `p=none` is the best teaching line in the stage.
- Reply timing is unclear to the player: after Done, nothing says a reply is coming except "A reply may land in your inbox in a moment" inside the checklist.
