## Blockers and findings

**B1 (blocker). Ruth does not hand over the Recon key in the same session in which the plan is finished.**
After `plan_done` I talked to Ruth three times (open, topic, "I'll get on", close with X, open again). Every time
she printed a hub line ("Need me?", "Something else?", "Still here. Good.") and the inventory stayed
`Notepad, Your phone` (seq 247 to 310). After the reload, the first talk opened with "Plan holds up. Here's the Recon
Room key. Three people, public sources only." and the key appeared (seq 333).
Cause: the engine resumes a saved person-chat at the knot of the last choices
(`person-chat-minigame.js:507-560`, "Re-navigating to current knot"). Ruth's opening conversation always ends in
`hub`, so a reopen lands in `hub` and never runs `resume`, `recheck` or `keys` (`ruth.ink`). A reload loses the
position, so it falls back to `start`, and then the `keys` block runs. A student who does not reload cannot get any
of the three keys (Operations and Blue Team use the same route) and Ruth's phone also sends them back to her
("Come back to me in person for the Recon key"). Fix is in the ink: run the `keys` check from `hub` itself (or make
each hub pass call it), not only from `resume`. The same applies to the backup `#complete_task` block in `recheck`.
This is not fixed here.

**F1. Ruth's phone shows stage 0 chatter after stage 1 is done.** The contact preview read "On the sheet still?" with
an unread badge while `plan_done` was true, and opening it offered the stage 0 topics ("Where do I start?" answered
with "The rules-of-engagement board, in this room"). The routing only re-evaluates after the player picks "Thanks.";
the next open then showed "Recon, is it? You'll need the key from me in person if you haven't got it." Same cause as
B1 (resume at the last knot), lower severity.

**F2. Feedback quotes a section that is not on the sheet.** The personal-accounts card says "Section 4 limits the test
to work accounts". The sheet on screen has no numbered sections, only In scope and Out of scope lists.

**F3. The blame measure does not quote Fairbairn.** PLAN §5 stage 1 says the blame measure's reason quotes him. It
reads "Naming people suppresses reporting; the report is about process, not blame." Accurate, but not his
voice, and the through-line is weaker for it.

**F4. Wrong picks are saved in the plan board.** `se_board_state.plan.picks` held `disrupt_payroll` and
`did_staff_report` while they were wrong. Harmless unless a reload shows the wrong pick still selected; I did not
reload at that point, so not tested.

**F5. Player speaker label is `demo_player`.** In every chat the student's own lines are attributed to
`demo_player` in the dialogue state. I did not check whether the label is visible on screen (headless, no
screenshot of a chat). Check by eye.

**F6. Disambiguation menu at the sheet and at both NPCs.** A chair sits within 32 px of `roe_sheet`, and Ruth's and
Fairbairn's sprites sit near other objects, so `interact` went `via-interaction-menu` each time. A student gets a
pick-one popup before the sheet opens.

**F7. The whiteboard and the sheet read as one grey wall of controls.** Not a defect; see player notes.

## Player-experience notes

- **First minute.** Ruth's three lines, then Fairbairn's question, is a good start: it is a real choice and the wrong
  answer gets a short, fair telling-off from Ruth. The cost is small (nothing is gated on it), and nothing later
  mentions which one you picked. Fairbairn says nothing after the wrong answer, and a few lines later signs and
  thanks you with no change in tone. The choice reads as arguable but its consequence does not show.
- **Repetition.** After the choice Ruth says "Read the rules of engagement on the board. Properly." and the hub's
  first line is "The rules of engagement are on the board. Start there." Two near-identical lines back to back.
- **Fairbairn introduces himself twice.** He asks his question in the briefing, then on first direct talk says
  "You'll be Tarnside's. Gareth Fairbairn. This is my firm", followed straight away by "Right. I'll sign it."
  The jump from introduction to signing is abrupt.
- **Where the next step is.** After `roe_signed` nothing points at Fairbairn. The task list moves on to the plan, and
  I only visited him because the PLAN says he has a scene. A student could skip his condition entirely and still
  reach the plan, where it matters.
- **Scope sheet.** Clear. The sheet, the five cards and the stop-rule question are one scroll; the stop-rule question
  is below the fold and a student might not find it until the cards are done, which is the intended order. The
  bounce text is specific and teaches ("Physical entry is out of scope for this engagement."). The why appears twice
  on a wrong sort (a banner at the top and under the card); the banner is off-screen once you have scrolled.
- **Plan board.** The right objective is the first option and the right measure is the first too. A student who
  reads top to bottom picks both with no thought. Four of the five wrong picks teach something real (disruption,
  passwords, blame, "did staff report"), and the "cannot observe this in the lab" reason on the last is honest.
- **Pace.** Stage 0 plus stage 1 took me about 5 minutes of wall clock with deliberate wrong answers, well inside
  the stage budget. No timers, no dead ends other than B1.
- **Reload.** After a reload the title screen and the Mission Brief note ("Tarnside Assurance has been hired...")
  show again before play resumes, and the player is back at the spawn tile.
