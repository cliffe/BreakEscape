## Player-experience notes

- The route reads well when the pieces work. The board's "Missing" rows, the "From the call" lines and the pickers that appear only once a fact is held make it clear what to go and find next. The Pierce and Hampshire sends each took a handful of clicks once I knew which sender to pick.
- The pretext call is the most fun stage: Dev reacts to what you say and the influence bar moves visibly. It would be stronger if the name check were signposted and the blanks were filled on the first menu (findings 4 and 5).
- The builder is the point where a student would give up. They do everything right, score 75, and see two red crosses they cannot fix, with a card telling them they have not found the facts they have just found (findings 2 and 3). Lord then rejects the link. There is no hint of what is wrong with the page.
- A student who works in order hits the Ruth problem at the very first door: the sheet and plan are done, Ruth says "Need me?" and the Recon room is locked. There is nothing in the game that tells them where the key is.
- The report stage is clear and the "blaming staff" answer teaches well. Three findings fit one screen. The "No names in it" line from Ruth on the Blue Team key hand-over never reaches a student, because of finding 1.
- Time: about 19 minutes wall clock for the whole run, including 12 minutes of debugging and workarounds. An uncomplicated pass would be nearer 12.
