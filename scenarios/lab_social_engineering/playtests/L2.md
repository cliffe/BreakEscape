## Findings

**B1 (blocker for a normal player): Ruth does not hand over keys in-session.** After the opening chat, her story sits in the `hub` knot. Every later open resumes in `hub`, so the `keys` knot (ruth.ink lines 136 to 152, reached only through `start -> resume -> recheck -> keys`) never runs. Seen twice in one game: after `plan_done` (the Recon key was not given; Ruth only offered "What does the board want from me?") and after `osint_done` plus `se_intel_username_convention` (Ruth said "Something else?" and offered only "I'll get on."). After a page reload her story restarts at `start`, and the key lines fire at once ("Plan holds up. Here's the Recon Room key", then "Operations is yours"). So the lab only progresses past stage 1 if the student reloads, which no student has a reason to do. This is the same loop P6-T0 noted for `bootstrap`; it is a game problem, not only a harness one. The recheck knot also never completes tasks in-session for the same reason (the event mappings did it instead). Suggested fix: have `hub` (or the exit path) divert to `recheck`/`keys` on each entry, or open Ruth at `resume` each time. Evidence: session log, Ruth conversations around steps 150 to 160 and 300 to 310; verify-run inventory shows both keys only after the reloads.

**B2 (gate bypass): the Recon key opens the Operations door.** Before the Operations key existed I tried the Operations door holding only the Recon key. The lockpick screen offered "Recon Room Key", the key-mode minigame completed with `success: true`, and `door_unlocked` / `door_opened` fired for engagement_office -> operations_room (log seq 132 to 138). Scenario data is correct (`requires: operations_door`, keyPins [15,26,37,48] against [11,22,33,44]), but the cuts are only 4 apart on every pin. I did not read the key-check code to the end; the lockpicking module uses a pin threshold of 8 (`minigames/lockpicking/pin-management.js` around lines 944 to 963), which would accept it. Effect: Operations can be entered with the Recon key, skipping the OSINT gate and Ruth. Blue Team's key differs by 8, 6, 6 and 3 from Operations, so it may bypass too. Fix is in the scenario key pins (spread them apart) or the key check, not a play instruction. Not retried against the Blue Team door. The `enter_operations` task was not completed in this run (I did not walk in).

**M1: wrong key gives no message.** The lock screen says "Select a key to begin" with no hint about which key belongs; since a wrong-looking key opens the door anyway (B2), a student gets no signal. PLAN section 5 and F17 describe a "see Ruth for the key" locked-door line; I saw none (the lock minigame opens directly, no text beyond "Door to Operations Room").

**M2: a third tool appeared unannounced.** Under Tools, "Handle search: @mpierce_official" showed up (returns "No public results.") after the staff addresses and usernames were noted. Not in PLAN section 5's list. Harmless; looks like a decoy for the username convention. Flagging in case it is not intended.

**M3: Path field of the PDF properties.** The properties list Author, Software and Path (`C:\Users\mpierce\Documents`). Only Author gives the username. A student who clicks Path first gets "Nothing here you can use" although it plainly shows `mpierce`. Could confuse; the Author-only gate is as designed.

**M4: harness only.** `bootstrap` with `maxSteps: 2` leaves Ruth's opening chat open (as P6-T0 said). I played the chat by hand. My own Esc test dispatched the key to both `document` and `window`, which closed the browser and then opened the game pause menu; that was my probe, not a game bug.

## Player-experience notes

- The browser is easy to read. Click a block, get a one-line "Noted: ..." in plain words, and it goes on the board. Task ticks arrive while the browser is still open, which feels good.
- The stage is quick: about 3 minutes from the Recon door to `osint_done` for someone who knows the sources. A first-time student reading everything will take longer, probably near the 10 minute budget.
- The team page showing every address up front, with the Hunter lookup supplying the pattern, is a good "why does publishing this matter" beat. The "What is this?" cards on tools are short and useful.
- The PDF page looks empty apart from a "Document properties" button. A new player may not realise there is anything on the page; Ruth's stage-2 line ("read the published PDF's properties") rescues them, but only through the hub option, which is itself only offered while `plan_done` and not `osint_done`.
- The stale facts carry a clear "Old or unverified" label on the board, which helps learning but removes the chance of being fooled by them (the plumber note says outright it is unconnected).
- The biggest experience problem is B1: after the board goes green, Ruth just chatters "Something else?" and the Operations door is still shut, with nothing telling the student to do anything different. Combined with B2 a student may walk through the door with the Recon key anyway and never notice Ruth was meant to give them anything.
