## Dialogue notes

Lines and states that jar. D1 to D3 are the ones to fix first.

- **D1 (major for the dialogue pass). Fairbairn's scene knots only play on a fresh open, so the sign scene and the payoff are fragile or dead.** `fairbairn.ink` runs `resume` only from `start`. The engine reopens a saved story at the hub (`isResumedConversation`), so:
  - Game 3198: I opened Fairbairn before signing ("Why bring in a firm to attack your own people?"), signed the sheet, reopened him. He said "Something else?", `fairbairn_condition_heard` stayed false, and Ruth gave the Recon key anyway ("Plan holds up."). The condition, the Computer Misuse Act line and the whole signature moment are skipped. Ruth's phone stage 1 still tells the student to go and hear his condition, with no way to do it.
  - Game 3197: after the report was filed, Fairbairn (reopened twice) said "Yes?" then "Something else?". The ink variable `report_filed` was true and `said_payoff` false when I read the story state, so the payoff "I've read your report. Not one name in it, just like I asked." never plays on the route Ruth sends you down ("Gareth's reading it now, go and see his face"). The `payoff` and `sign` scenes need a re-check on every hub pass (as Ruth's hub does with `-> recheck ->`), not only at `start`. DIALOGUE_REVIEW_C2 item 4 already anticipated the payoff being skippable; this is a harder form of it.
- **D2.** "That signature is the difference between a test and a crime. Without it, this is the Computer Misuse Act. Same goes elsewhere." The second sentence is not a sentence a person says: "this is the Computer Misuse Act" should be "this is an offence under the Computer Misuse Act", and "Same goes elsewhere" reads as a dangling clause. Suggested: "Without it, this is an offence under the Computer Misuse Act. Other countries have their own versions."
- **D3.** Fairbairn: "Not one name in it, just like I asked." / Tomasz: "Whichever it is, the report names nobody." / the report's results table lists Chris Hampshire, Melanie Pierce and Sofia Lord by name. Either the table uses roles or the lines say "no one is blamed". (The payoff is moot until D1 is fixed, but Tomasz's line plays every time.)
- **D4.** Dev's second call opens with the student's first line ("I'm helping Sofia Lord, your new starter in Finance.") and Dev says nothing. `team_checked` is an ink variable kept from the first call, so the acknowledgement is skipped and the call loses its first beat. It needs to reset per call, like the other call state.

- **D6.** Lord miss 2 hint "She only trusts the portal's own notices." shows when the sender already passed and the link is what failed. The ladder rung should either be about the failing factor or the first repeated miss should show the keyed hint for a target whose failing factor is not the first.
