## Counts and verdict

**0 blockers, 8 majors, 12 minors.**

**Verdict: another round needed.** The structure is the strongest of any mission in the season. Ghost, Victoria, Voltage, Gary, Nightshade, Vector and Phantom, Mercer and the m09 Recruiter each change who stands in a room, what they say and what opens. All six endings close with an epilogue, a card and credits. The weak point is the user's test. The close shows the player very little of their own season:
- the player is missing from their own credits on most saves (A1);
- three endings' last beats arrive under or after the credits (A2);
- m05 lands only in the credits (A3), and m01 gets one sentence from the villain (A4);
- the people standing at 06:00 are there because of earlier choices, but nobody says so (A5);
- the only voice that joins tonight to the season is Tesseract's, delivered as a recital (A6).

Music doesn't mark the climax or the epilogue (A7), and two endings don't say at 06:00 what they bought (A8).
