# m10 Full Disclosure: lighting design review, round 1

Reviewer: fresh, read-only. Plan under review: `scenarios/m10_full_disclosure/LIGHTING_PLAN.md` (2026-10-22). Checked against `scenario.json.erb` (rendered with the validator's ERB binding), the map JSON in `public/break_escape/assets/rooms/room_m10_*.json`, `scripts/room_gen/m10_rooms.py`, the R4 map renders and R3 in-game shots, stage 5 `room_layout.md` §2.1–2.8, and `systems/lighting.js`.

## Verdict

**Approve once the five majors are fixed.** No blockers. The plan has the right shape. Night ambient and reuse by role are right, and the power room is the only motion room, which is the right call: everyone in it is `initiallyHidden` and revealed on `room_entered:power_room` (erb:3087, 3106–3107, 3127, 3146, 3164; raid copies erb:2434). So the room is dark through the door and strikes as the player walks in, which is the m09 lesson applied correctly. The server's `hasPresentNpc` also skips hidden NPCs (`app/models/break_escape/game.rb:782-784`), so no light leaks under the door early. Every emitter key in the plan matches a texture that the maps place and that a scenario object claims, or one that decor renders (table below). Nothing a puzzle needs depends on light: the lock guard's drawn cone sits above the light map (`npc-los.js:386-391`), and the isolator lamp and hut window repeat what their text and sprite already say. No flicker, no `above` blinks, and the strike is the stock one, so flash safety holds.

The majors are about where the light lands. A caged bulb sits on the back wall (feet y 24, the wall runs to y 64), so an emitter there pools on the wall, not the floor. In the three `panels: false` rooms built on bulbs, the floor gets ambient only. Two of those ambients are below the readability floor. In the power room, the bulbs glow before the tubes strike. The workshop's bulb is not configured and falls to the engine's r 80, 0.9 lamp. In the forward post, the vans, which stage 5 calls "the only warm thing", are the weakest warm source of four.

Luma check (floor `#a8b0c0` = 175.5): forward `#aab2c4` 177.6; tunnel `#aaaeb8` **173.9**; workshop `#b4b8c4` 184.0; power `#b0b4bc` 179.9; hall `#a8b0c4` 175.8; gallery `#a8aebc` **173.7**; core `#b0b2b8` 178.0.

## Assumptions

- The map JSON in `public/break_escape/assets/rooms/` (working tree, uncommitted) is what the game loads, and its object x/y (Tiled bottom-left) matches `m10_rooms.py`. I took positions from the JSON.
- The R4 map renders are at 2×. I read the door and window positions in m1, m2 and M5 from them by eye, to about ±3 px.
- I did not run a lighting tour. The pool-on-the-wall and bulbs-on-in-the-dark findings come from reading `lighting.js`, not from screenshots. The round 1 tour should confirm them.
- I left out the hall cctv camera (no red recording light), to keep red off the critical path.
