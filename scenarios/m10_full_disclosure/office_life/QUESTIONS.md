## Q1. Tier: Tier 0, or a small forward-post treatment?

- **a. Small forward-post treatment (recommended).** Two support staff at a kettle on a crate south of the folding table; pool chatter, three once-only exchanges, two short conversations; hidden at `closing_done`; a trouble global stops the tea if the player hurts anyone. The bunker stays Tier 0. No levers, no movement across the yard, no change to any existing NPC. It makes the yard read as a working operation at 03:00 for little build cost.
- **b. Tier 0 everywhere.** What the missions plan expected (`OFFICE_LIFE_MISSIONS_PLAN.md:345`). Nothing to build. The yard already holds 6 to 10 story NPCs, so this costs the least crowding.

## Q2. Casting

No free course character has a voice that m10 does not already use. The voices m10 leaves free (Autonoe, Erinome, Gacrux, Vindemiatrix, Achernar) belong to characters taken by the demos or other missions. m10 already shares one voice among speakers who are never heard together (Iapetus: Vector, Mercer and the ENTROPY operative), so a share is not new here; the question is which share costs least.

- **a. Darren Price and Sofia Marchetti (recommended).** Field support (white, 40s, Bristol) and welfare and logistics (Italian-British, 30s, southern English). Darren's Orus is Robert Vance's, and Vance is heard only on the phone, deep in the bunker. Sofia's Despina is Victoria's, and Victoria is not phone-only: besides `victoria_line` (phone) she has `victoria_gallery` and `victoria_threat`, both in person in the gallery after the breach, the same act in which Sofia's `ex_fp_door_open` plays in the yard. They are never in one room and chatter is muted while any conversation is open, so they are never heard at once, but a player walking from the gallery back to the gate can hear the same voice in both places within minutes. The style lines keep each accent well apart (Vance is Teesside, Victoria is RP; Darren Bristol, Sofia southern English with an Italian-British lilt). Full atlases, visemes and headshots; in no mission yet. Darren's age: 40s per `BRAND_SET.md:84` (`VOICE_CAST.md` says 50s; the style line would say 40s).
- **b. Anjali Rao or Nilesh Solanki in either slot.** Anjali's Kore is Quintero's and Nilesh's Iapetus is Vector's; both stand in the yard beside the kettle. Not recommended.
- **c. Ama Owusu and Lorna Easton.** Their voices (Autonoe, Erinome) are free in m10, but both are in the Access Tour demo, and Ama hosts both demos.
- **d. One extra only (Darren or Sofia).** Halves the voice share, but the kettle exchanges need two speakers, so the treatment becomes pool lines from one person.
- **e. As (a), but Sofia gets a voice m10 leaves free, in m10 only.** Vindemiatrix or Achernar instead of Despina, removing the Victoria overlap. Costs consistency: a course character keeps one voice everywhere by default, so Sofia would sound different here from the course and any later mission.

## Q5. How far the kettle talk carries

Two of the three exchanges are set off when the player is likely to be at the far end of the yard: the radios one when Arakawa's gate scene closes (often with the player at the gate console), and the door-open one as the player walks in from the east door after the breach. An exchange starts only if the player can hear it.

- **a. Hearing radius about 320 px, ten tiles (recommended).** Covers the table, the vans, the gate console and the terminal. Pool lines are heard across most of the yard, but they are short, at least two needs-minutes apart, only while the pair are at the kettle, and muted during every conversation. Through the open blast door the tunnel hears faint kettle talk, quietly.
- **b. Keep 200 px, about six tiles.** The kettle talk stays local to the table; the radios and door-open exchanges play only when the player happens to be near the kettle.
