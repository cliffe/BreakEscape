## Verdict

**Approve after three majors.** Nothing blocks play. Every room is in the mode the plan asks for, readables are legible, doorways in the dark power room are findable, and no lit room bleeds into a dark neighbour. The power room (dark and lit), the workshop, the security office and the core already look right. Three of the plan's mood lines don't land on screen: the forward post's yard reads neutral brown-grey, not cold blue; the gallery's "cold light up through the slit windows" shows as a white hot spot on the back wall; and the tunnel's bulb pools sit on the wall line, so the floor the player walks along is evenly lit.

Coordinates below are tiles (32 px). The shots are at 2x zoom, so 64 screen px per tile, measured from each room's outer wall edge.
