# m01 First Contact: office-life dialogue sample

How the new lines sound in play. Bracketed lines are stage directions, not spoken text. Tier 1 covers sections 1 and 2; tier 2 adds 3, 4 and 7.

## 1. First entry to the main office

[The player takes the key from Sarah and steps into the main office. Two seconds later Fatima, seated at the west desk, glances up from her screen.]

**Fatima:** Morning. Busy week.

[Typing carries on behind her. Four seconds later HaX's first call rings.]

## 2. Talking to them

[The player walks up to Graham at the east desk. He looks round on a chair.]

**Graham:** Graham, finance. If it's about expenses, I'm not in.

> Choice: I'll leave you to it.

**Graham:** Only joking. Not that I know much about computers, mind.

[The conversation closes. He turns back to the screen.]

Fatima's version:

**Fatima:** Sorry, I'm halfway through a candidate's file.

> Choice: Don't let me keep you.

**Fatima:** If it's the network, I only press the buttons. Nice to meet you.

## 3. At the cooler (tier 2)

[About two seconds after the player comes back into the office from a hallway (the mapping sets her thirst low at once), Fatima gets up, walks to the cooler against the south wall and fills a paper cup, standing behind it with her face to the camera.]

**Fatima:** Every CV says 'passionate' now.

[Graham follows a few seconds later.]

**Graham:** Quarter close. Nobody speak to me.

[Heard once, if both are at the cooler together.]

**Fatima:** Did finance sign off my two interns yet?

**Graham:** One intern. The other's in next year's budget.

**Fatima:** Next year. You said that last year.

**Graham:** And I meant it both times.

## 4. Later (tier 2)

[On a second visit, the biscuit exchange plays instead.]

**Graham:** I've brought biscuits for quarter close.

**Fatima:** Bribery, Graham?

**Graham:** Morale. It's in the budget, under sundries.

**Fatima:** I'm telling everyone it's under sundries.

## 5. After the reveal

[The player picks up the file in the server room and walks back through the main office. Both desks are empty and the typing has stopped. The two of them left while the player was in the server room, so nobody was seen going. Reception is quiet too. Derek is waiting in the break room.]

No lines. The silence is the cue.

## 6. If the player attacks

[Fatima backs away from her desk, hands up.]

HaX, by text: *Stand down, Agent. She runs recruitment. She's not part of this.*

The music switches to the threat track until she is down or the fight ends.

## 7. Maya's trip (tier 2)

[The player finishes the first conversation in Maya's office, where she has just told them about the operation. Two seconds after the conversation closes she gets up, walks out through the east hall and across the main office to the cooler, and fills a paper cup. Later she walks home.]

No new lines. The trip is silent. If the player talks to her on the way, the only choice offered is "Not here. I'll come to your office." (player text, not voiced), which ends the chat, and she picks up her trip afterwards.
