# m02 office life: dialogue sample

For the user's calibration before the dialogue cycles continue (missions plan 6.13, open item 3). Lines are drafts from `PLAN.md` section 6, after seven plan review rounds. All are new; no existing m02 line changes.

**Who.** Ernesto Bautista, night porter: early sixties, Filipino heritage, thirty years at St Catherine's; warm, unhurried, dry. Hodan Warsame, night domestic: early twenties, British-Somali, from Birmingham; quick, wry, tired but sharp. They wait in the Night Handover room for radio calls between runs, while the hospital runs on paper. They know nothing of the operation, give no clue and set no task.

**How they are heard.** Pool lines are spoken one per speaker per shared break (at the staff-room boiler for `coffee`, the cooler for `cooler`), only when the player can hear them, with a 2-minute cooldown. Exchanges are short scripted back-and-forths, each heard once. Pool lines are capped at 40 characters.
