## The improvement pass

### 1. The hub's early branches (the new finding)

R2-2 fixed the hub's late order. Round 3 checked the early order, and the same weakness is there. `report_progress` judges each step by how the player got past it, not by whether they did. The gate step reads `guard_admitted` (set only by the guard's sign-in), and the card step reads `vance_provided_keycard` (set only by Vance's clone or his KO drop). The mission offers a third way past each, and the hint text names both: walking off from the guard's challenge (erb:602-605) and the locker spare (phone ink:284, 816). Because the branches are "first unmet wins", one stale early step hides every later one. The fix is the same for both: one latch for "got past it" and an extra guard on the branch. The cost is two globals, one mapping, one `onPickup` and four condition edits.

I walked the other branches for the same problem. `vance_met or robert_vance_ko` covers every way past Vance (vance_met is set on every exit from the first meeting: Vance ink:336, 349, 628). `server_room_reached` is set on workshop entry whichever card was used. `plant_room_entered`, `voltage_neutralised` and `esd_authorized` are all set by events, not by one route. Nothing else is masked.

### 2. Boss-key audit and chain depth

Unchanged from round 1. The plant reader is seen at depth 4 before the kit (depth 4, behind the Level 2 door) and the print (depth 3, a back-track). The workshop door is seen at depth 3 before Relay's card (depth 4). Retiring the paper gate removed one parallel branch from `confirm_the_lie`. It didn't take away a boss-key: the evidence never sat behind a lock it opened. The dial is now the only thing that gates the act-two aims, and it's the mission's real deduction, which is the right thing to have there.

### 3. Softlocks and order dependence (SOFTLOCK_PATTERNS S1/S2)

- **S2, what else could the player hold?** A player can read the dial before talking to Vance: Hall 1 is reachable through the control room without a card. The identify task then reveals and completes `confirm_the_lie` while `get_inside` is still open. This isn't a regression (one evidence document plus the dial did the same before) and it's harmless: Vance's hub and the reveal options don't need the aim.
- Evidence read after the aim completes: no repeated toast and no double score (objectives-manager.js:849; game.rb:2132).
- ESD refusals: the order is dial, then cause, then Voltage, and every unmet state has its own variant (erb:2178-2182).
- No softlock found. R3-1 and R3-2 cost guidance, not solvability.

### 4. Capability arc

Unchanged. Lockpick (m01) is spent on the locker, tool case and go-bag. The RFID cloner (m03) is spent on Vance and Relay. The fingerprint kit is m04's grant, felt at the plant reader first. m05 carries it on.

### 5. Pacing and clocks after R2

The normal clock starts on `map_the_attack` (erb:1124-1128). With the paper errand gone, the only post-flag errand a flags-first player can have is the dial, next door in Hall 1. The hub (ink:835), the 2.5 s text (erb:1152) and the ESD refusal (erb:2179) now all name it. At a struggling player's pace that fits easily inside 14 minutes.

## Verdict

**The phase shouldn't close until R3-1 and R3-2 are fixed.** Both are small mission-local edits (two latches, one mapping, four hub conditions, one route) and don't touch the engine. Everything round 2 changed holds. The dial alone completes "Confirm the Telemetry Is Lying" on client and server, later aims appear in a sensible order, the debrief and credits are right with no evidence, and no reader of the eleven removed globals is left, engine included. R3-3 to R3-6 are tidy-ups the same fixer can do in the same pass (R3-4 and R3-5 touch voiced text, but no audio exists yet). Round 4 can then be a short confirmation, which fits the phase's aim of about five cycles. If the orchestrator would rather close now, close with R3-1 and R3-2 fixed and checked in the browser (checks 1 and 2 below), and record round 4 as skipped with the reason.

Rounds are narrowing. Round 1 found design questions, round 2 one root cause (an engine change that dissolved a gate), and round 3 one pattern in the hub (branches keyed on the route rather than the result). It's convergence.
