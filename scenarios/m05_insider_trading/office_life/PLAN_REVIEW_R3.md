## Minors

**m1. A hidden NPC's needs still decay, so the claim in 2.13 is wrong.** `_tickNpc` calls `_decay(s)` first (`systems/npc-needs.js:1404`) and only afterwards returns for an unavailable (hidden) NPC (`:1418` to `:1426`). `_decay` has no hidden check (`:1546` to `:1571`). PLAN.md:202 says "both needs start counting when she appears". In fact Bernadette registers hidden when the break room first loads by day, and by the turn both `tidy` (50, decay 8) and `caffeine` (60, decay 3) are at 0. On reveal she makes two trips back to back, while the player is in the server room. That does no harm, but the "2.5 needs-minutes" estimate and E7's "within about 4 needs-minutes" rest on the false premise. PLAN.md:135 makes the same claim for the tick in general. Fix: add `"needs": { "set": { "caffeine": 60, "tidy": 50 } }` to her three show mappings. A mapping runs `needs` before `setVisible` (`npc-manager.js:1639` to `:1640`, `:1656` to `:1660`), so her timing then starts at the reveal. Then correct PLAN.md:135 and :202.

**m2. The sync note on repeated plain mappings is incomplete.** Every `setNPCVisible` call also posts `update_room` / `update_npc_state` to the server (`npc-behavior.js:440` to `:447`; `systems/room-state-sync.js:220`). Thandiwe's plain hide and Bernadette's plain show therefore send one small, idempotent request on every server-room entry. The plan's "adds nothing" (PLAN.md:191) is true for saved keys but not for traffic. Section 7 also leaves out `npcHostility`, which is keyed per NPC (`state-sync.js:43`): a strike adds up to four bounded keys (three newcomers and Lisa). Fix: list both in section 7 as bounded. Optionally give the plain mappings a condition such as `globalVars.thandiwe_ko !== true` for the hide (and the same with `bernadette_ko` for the show), so that a repeat on a KO'd body does not re-enable its physics body (`npc-behavior.js:440`). After a KO the body keeps `checkCollision.none` (`systems/npc-hostile.js:405`).

**m3. E-L4 ("first meeting at post") can fail on the peek route.** A room loads when its door opens (`doors.js:883`), and Lisa's trip mappings register then. A player who opens the break-room door from the corridor without walking in, goes into the open office and comes back meets her at the kettle on first entry. The 2A fallback (a longer delay, or a third room away) does not cure this. Fix: either reword E-L4 as accepted under D84 b (talk at the kettle is allowed, and E-L1 already covers talkability), or gate the trip on the first close of her conversation. That second route is the skill's preferred "first-talk" pattern; it costs a declared global set by a `conversation_closed:lisa_park` onceOnly, which adds one key and must be re-counted.
