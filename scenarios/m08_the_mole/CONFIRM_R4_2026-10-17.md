Judgement on busyness: not too busy. A burst is two quick dips about 120 ms apart, which reads as a single twitch; at one every 3-7 s it is visible if you look at her and easy to ignore if you are doing something else. In a 3 s run of 6 fps frames while she lay on the floor, 1 frame in 20 was visibly dimmer. I would leave the numbers as they are. If playtesters find it fidgety during long conversations at the desk, widen the gap to 4-9 s and keep the depth; I would not make the dips shallower, since 50% is what makes it read as a projector glitch. ## Findings

1. **Minor.** ATHENA's tip-off text never fires if the off-duty agent has already caught you at a locker. In game 2680 the locker pick set `nightshade_tipped_off`, and the KO mapping on HaX (`npc_ko:receptionist_ai`, condition `nightshade_tipped_off !== true`) correctly stays silent; HaX's earlier text was "Someone saw you at the lockers. He'll tell the floor he saw nothing, which here is the same thing. If one's the mole's locker, he knows we're looking." So no missing text in practice, but a tester who does both will see only the first. By design, noted.
2. **Minor.** The "Add to Notepad" button in the text viewer shows a tiny plain white square as its icon (`04_textviewer_light.png`, `04_textviewer_dark.png`). Not part of this fix; looks like an unscaled or placeholder image.
3. **Minor, not confirmed.** In game 2685 after a Restart (opening briefing person-chat up, progress overlay still shown on reload), keys left everything unchanged, but the single Enter that closed the overlay was followed by the dialogue moving from "Welcome back, Agent 0x00. Security posture..." to "I have also logged your kit. One pick set..." and the second Enter changed nothing. It may be the dialogue's own timer, not the key. I did not reproduce it on a game with a title screen under the overlay (4 of 4 such runs: Enter did not advance the title). Worth a look in a headed run.
4. **Minor, cosmetic.** After a reload ATHENA is restored in `death-down`, where she fell in `death-right` or `death-down-right`. She is lying down, translucent and glowing; only the facing differs.
5. Observation, no count: punching with her diagonally up-right of the player (dx 29, dy -35) landed no hits in 50 presses; she registered once I lined up on one axis. Probably the facing cone; fists need the axis facing.

Counts: 0 blockers, 0 majors, 4 minors.
