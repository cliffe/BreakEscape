## Checked and passed

- **Deduction rule.** Break-room lockers lit/unlit ratios a 0.857, **b 0.842**, c 0.837 (base and named identical): no panel pool singles out 0x47. Crypto lab: all three screens on the built-in screen glow, none keyed by id. Gain at the screen: **pc5 1.26**, pc12 1.23, CyberChef workstation 1.37. pc5 is the brightest screen in absolute terms (181), but the ranking is the same unlit (143 vs 134 vs 104), so the cause is the art, not the light. No change at naming in either room (`named/`).
- **Story states.** The spriteVariants follow the textVariants' order. Evidence wall: closed (flags + named), named (named only), default (flags only = three "?" silhouettes, matching "PERSONS OF INTEREST (3)"). Disposition: retained / handover / awaiting / default. The named face's "0X47 PENDING" appears only after mole_identified. The RECORDING state repeats "The interrogation room records everything" (`ink:68`). `after/` and `triple/` sit on variant 1 at #c0c4cc and differ only in the disposition face. The `fate_decided` `mode: on` variant does something: it keeps the room lit after a reload.
- **Flash safety.** No flicker, no pulses. One `breathe` (alert screen, ~0.23 Hz). Rack blink is the built-in 0.7-1.0 shimmer. Room variants switch once, with no strike, behind the naming conversation or the flag submission. No `above` blink anywhere.
- **Tour validity.** Every `--check` line shows the map redrawing (300-367 ms advance in 300 ms). The validator passes (schema, no lighting warnings).
- **Lit rooms are moodier, not grey.** Interior mean lit vs unlit: lobby 57 vs 65, director 58 vs 75 (the darkest step, warm lamp pool, reads as an office at night), ops 83 vs 93, intel 69 vs 81, servers lit 106 vs 115, archives 114 vs 130, crypto 152 vs 172, break 130 vs 145.
- **Dark rooms.** Server room: racks, both terminals and the suppression panel read, and the reader LED marks the west door (36 red px, against 16 unlit). Archives and interrogation strike on entry, so nothing to find is lost in the dark. No lit-to-dark bleed visible: ops→servers, ops→archives.
- **Working config.** The lobby alert screen red reaches the floor below (G 62→52 under the screen, `floorTint` 0.3). The memorial is held at its unlit colour (48,45,44) while the lobby drops 12%. The badge readers' red point doubles in lit rooms (16→32 red px). Fire call points are off. Ops board light on the walkway below (+7-12% relative). `exec_laptop` +18%. Suppression panel steady.
