### Major

**M-01. `set_up_drop_terminal` completes only on a click on the Field Laptop.** ERB:126-128 (the `object_interacted` mapping with `objectType === 'vm-launcher' && roomId === 'reading_room'`). A player who already has the attack VM open goes straight to the drop terminal and submits flag 1 without touching the laptop. That covers a Hacktivity player with the console in another tab, or a player back after a break with the VM still running. For them the task never completes and `get_in` never completes. Aims 2 and 3 still open, because task progress reveals them (`objectives-manager.js:722`), so the night goes on. But aim 1 shows an unticked task until the credits, its score share is lost, and `get_in` stays "active" under the ledger. **Fix**: add a HaX mapping `{ "eventPattern": "global_variable_changed:flag1_submitted", "condition": "value === true", "onceOnly": true, "completeTask": "set_up_drop_terminal" }` beside the flag-1 `unlockDoor` mapping (ERB:138). The existing mapping keeps the guide text.

### Minor

**m-01. The salvage line arrives on `flag1_read`'s last line, in a call that isn't forced.** ink `m09_phone_agent0x99.ink:173`. A player who closes the call at its first choice may never get the `catalogue_salvage_line` note. Whether it arrives then depends on how a reopened thread resumes (checklist §4.5). The drop terminal screen and hub topic 5 both quote the line, so the night isn't stuck.
