## Findings

### M1 (major): the three floor needs NPCs have no hostile chase leash, so a punched extra follows the player out of the room
Files: `scenario.json.erb:1974` (`trader_npc`), `:2019` (`lerato_dlamini`), `:2059` (`tomasz_wisniewski`). None of the three has a `behavior` key.

Evidence:
- SKILL.md:115: "Hostile ambient staff get the chase `leash: true` (with `chaseRange`) so a punched NPC stays near its post." The skill's class table gives optional flavour as "yes, leashed" (SKILL.md:63). m02's needs extras carry `"behavior": { "hostile": { "leash": true } }` (`m02_ransomed_trust/scenario.json.erb:3510`, `:3552`).
- Without the leash, a hostile chases while the player is within its aggro distance (160 px, `npc-behavior.js:657`) and ignores rooms (`npc-behavior.js:1054` to `:1067`, `npc-chase-leash.js:4` to `:13`).
- Game 8283: one punch on seated Lerato, then the player walked to the checkpoint. She followed through the door to the reception lobby edge and knocked the player out. A punched office worker can therefore trail the player into the checkpoint, the lobby and beyond, where the guard and HaX stand, until the player respawns.
- Bystanders hit by the arc turn hostile in the same way (m1, game 8275), so one punch at the coffee point can set two unleashed chasers loose.

Fix:
- Add `"behavior": { "hostile": { "leash": true } }` to all three records. The engine's default `chaseRange` of 400 px (`config/combat-config.js:74`) is wider than the 320 px floor, so the leash works as "this room only". Set `"chaseRange": 160` if they should give up sooner.
- Dani is the moved free NPC, so a combat key on his record is in scope. Behaviour-only `hostile` config is compatible with the needs layer (only `behavior.patrol` is barred), and m02 is the precedent.
- Record the change in PLAN 2.4 (leash column) and 2.8.
- Re-run game 8283's case on a fresh game. Pass: when the player crosses into the checkpoint she logs "Gave up the chase" and walks back to her post area, still hostile. Re-run one KO case to confirm nothing else moved.
