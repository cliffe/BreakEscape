## Fixes made during the run

1. Mapping conditions can compare two operands (`data.level < data.previous`), in
   `npc-manager.js`, with tests; `w3_eased` now ticks.
2. The report queue takes `settled` from GET /scenario on load (`NpcReports.restore`), so a
   settled NPC sends nothing after a reload until an ease.
3. Scenario: `w4_cupboard` is an `unlock_object` task; the breaker is named "Share breaker";
   every W work station authors `"restores": 10` (with none, a use restores 0 and the owner works
   back to back, so W1's "about every 15 seconds" and W7's rest could not happen).
