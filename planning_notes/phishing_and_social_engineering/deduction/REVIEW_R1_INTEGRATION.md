## Verdict

Not ready to build. One blocker: the worked example that doubles as the clean validator fixture fails
the plan's own N7/N13 and the closed schema. Seven majors cover bridge scripts that run-suite cannot
execute, a fixture B bridge order that cannot exercise the witness, the `triggers` key missing from
`known_top_level` in an unowned, in-flight file, triggers lints that never fail a run, regression
gates that come too late for a shared tree, missing SE doc updates, and an unworkable validator-test
route for schema and ink-reading lints. The packet structure, the wiring order, the numbering and the
engine hook points are otherwise sound and integrate cleanly with the SE build.

Counts: **1 blocker, 7 major, 15 minor** (23 findings).
