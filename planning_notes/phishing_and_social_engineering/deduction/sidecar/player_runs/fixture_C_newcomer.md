# Player run: fixture C "Safe house", newcomer persona

Persona: office worker doing this as part of an awareness session. Doesn't play games much, knows nothing
about security. Gets lost when a word isn't explained. Played from `player_packets/fixture_C.md` only.

## Before I move

The brief says one of three "field contacts" leaked a secret and I'm looking for a "Sleeper". I had to
guess at that word. I think it means someone who looks like one of us but is secretly working for the other
side. The packet never says so; I worked it out from "Trust no one".

The profile: "Carries a cult-issued burner, or went to the quarry site and has no verified alibi."

- "Burner": I've heard it on TV, a throwaway phone. "Cult-issued" threw me. Which cult? Nothing in the brief
  mentions one.
- "The quarry site": no idea what happened there, or why going there is bad. I just took it as "the bad
  place".
- I read the "or" as two separate ways to be guilty. That was right, but on my first read I almost took it
  to mean the quarry alone was enough. That nearly caught me out later.

The clue counts looked like maths homework at first. Later I saw they were a cheat sheet: "No verified
alibi: 1 in this case" told me exactly one person had no alibi. "Cult-issued burner: 1" meant one person
had the phone. I only noticed this halfway through.

Inventory: lockpick (fine), fingerprint kit (I never used it), RFID cloner (no idea what that is),
safe-house keycard (fine).

Board marks so far: everyone Undecided.

Another SAFETYNET phone. Now I'm sure that's the normal phone. A bit dull: two clicks with no news.

Baptiste says Ahmed went out to the quarry site. The quarry is in the profile, so my stomach dropped. I

I asked one more gossip topic to see if there was dirt on Novak. It cost goodwill and gave me nothing

- Ahmed: went to the quarry. Suspicious. Marked Sleeper (note only).
- Baptiste: normal phone, chatty. Seems friendly, so probably fine (I know that's not real evidence).
- Novak: haven't met her.
- Make a call? Tempted to send Ahmed to wait. I held off because the profile also says "no verified

Field kit, Ahmed's. Two of two field kits. Normal. I was a little disappointed it was nothing juicy.

## Source 7: Novak's locked drawer

Lockpick. Inside: a **cult-issued burner**, marked with the star. That's the "1 in this case". This was the

## My mistake: sending people to wait

Here's where I went wrong. I talked to Novak and picked "Ask them to wait in the comms room". Fine. Then I
went back to the lounge and talked to Ahmed. In my head the quarry was confirmed by two sources and "Trust
no one" was ringing in my ears, so I sent him to wait too. I forgot the second half of the rule ("and has
no verified alibi"). I hadn't been to the garage yet and didn't even know there was alibi evidence there.

## Source 9: garage (cloned badge)

I was stuck here for a long time. The garage reader "takes only Novak's badge" and I had an "RFID cloner".
I didn't connect the two, because I didn't know what RFID meant. I asked the judge and was told I could
copy Novak's badge with the cloner when I was near her.

Problem: Novak was already in the comms room, because I'd sent her there. Could I still clone her badge?
I had to ask the judge. If I can't, I've locked myself out of the garage by acting too early, and the game
gave me no warning. [Question for the judge: can you clone a badge from someone who is waiting in the comms
room?]

Assuming I could (judge said yes, I walked into the comms room with my keycard):

- Reader: "Last used 03:40". The board puts it on Novak: no verified alibi. Why does 03:40 mean "no
  alibi"? I didn't get it. I think it means her badge opened the garage in the middle of the night, so she
  was out, but nobody spelled it out.
- Door log terminal: "filter" meant nothing to me, but I pressed it. Ahmed: verified alibi. Baptiste:
  verified alibi. Novak: no verified alibi.

My heart sank. Ahmed went to the quarry, but he HAS an alibi. By the rule he isn't a Sleeper. The count
line agreed: only 1 "no verified alibi", and that's Novak.

## Endgame

Ahmed and Novak are both sitting in the comms room. Talking to HaX offers "Lock the comms room with whoever
is waiting inside". That locks both of them. The exit phone says it locks in anyone waiting too. So there's
no way to lock only Novak. I couldn't find any way to let Ahmed out.

Options as I saw them:

1. Lock the room: Novak caught, Ahmed wrongly locked up with her.
2. Punch Novak (counts as accusing her), then leave. Ahmed is still in the comms room and gets locked in
   when I use the exit phone anyway.
3. Never lock and never leave? That isn't really an option.

I picked option 1, through HaX, feeling guilty. Then the line "Everyone has been dealt with" appeared...
except I never "dealt with" Baptiste. I think leaving him in the field counts as "Not a sleeper". The
packet says verdicts show "(knocked out / acted on)", and I wasn't sure leaving someone alone counted as
acting.

## Confusing or stuck moments

  quarry site". I knew they mattered but not why.
- "RFID cloner": the biggest blocker. I didn't know the tool fitted the garage. A one-line tool
  description ("copies someone's door badge when you're near them") would have fixed it.
- "Last used 03:40" means "no alibi": I didn't see the link until the door log said it plainly.
- "Verified alibi" vs "no verified alibi": fine once I saw them, but the board's labels are placeholders,
  so I'm guessing at the final words.
- Asking Ahmed or Baptiste to open doors I could already open with my own keycard. Why is that there?
- "Goodwill": I didn't know what I was spending or what happens at zero.
- The ✦ "marked": marked by whom? I thought it meant I had flagged it.

## Fair or unfair

- **Unfair-feeling:** one early send-to-wait can't be undone, and locking condemns everyone in the room
  together. I made one jumpy decision and couldn't fix it after I found the proof. That's a harsh lesson,
  but maybe it's the lesson: don't act before you check the alibi. I'd accept it if the debrief explains
  it kindly.
- **Possibly a trap:** sending Novak to wait before cloning her badge might cut off the garage. If it
  does, that's very unfair to a newcomer.
- **Too easy, once noticed:** the count line gives a lot away ("1 burner", "1 no alibi"). An experienced
  player could solve it by counting.
- **Boring:** three "Show me your kit" in a row, all the same normal phone. I get that they set up the
  contrast with Novak's hidden phone, but the first two felt like busywork.

## Fun or satisfying

- Finding Novak's burner after she'd shown me her "normal" phone. Best moment by far.
- Codes hidden in bags and under desks, then a safe. Proper escape-room feel.
- Seeing counts fill to "3 of 3" and "2 of 2".
- Being asked "What rules them out?" made me think about my reasons.

## What I learned about spotting an insider

- Someone can show you exactly what you expect (a normal work phone) and still be hiding something else.
  Check what they don't show you.
- Gossip is a lead, not proof. Baptiste's tip about Ahmed was true, but it was only half the story.
- Being somewhere suspicious isn't enough on its own. Check whether they can account for their time (the
  alibi) before you act.
- Building access logs (badge times) can show who was out when they shouldn't have been.
- Don't act on a hunch when you can't take it back. Gather everything first.
