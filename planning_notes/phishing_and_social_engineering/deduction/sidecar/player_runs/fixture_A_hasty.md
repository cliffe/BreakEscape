Persona: wants to finish fast, skims, accuses as soon as something fits a profile. Read only
`player_packets/fixture_A.md`. No judge was available in this run, so every action the packet says to
"ask the judge" about is marked **[judge: no answer]** and the run carries on as the persona would.

- No judge, so I never learnt what the favour, accusations, monitor call or altar did. Can't say
  whether stakes bit.
- The burner SIM has no source anywhere in the packet. I couldn't tell if I'd missed a room or if it
  is meant to be deduced by counting slots. If deduction is intended, a hasty player will never do it.
  They'll guess.
- Two ways to get the archive code (Venn or the note). Fine, but the note made Venn's topic pointless,
  so I never opened her coat or talked to her.
- "Courier ... carries no ward charm" is a negative condition. When I skimmed, "I haven't seen a
  charm" turned into "she has none". The board shows Hidden carry slots, but I wasn't looking at the
  board at that moment.
- The stakes line under the counts ("a wrong call will change what you can do next") is vague. I
  didn't know what I risked.

- **Fair:** Okafor as Keyholder is clean and satisfying: one key, footage, done.
- **Fair but harsh:** the Ishikawa trap. The decoy works well (Archive + Codex + a creepy line from
  the intern), and the clearing clue exists, but a hasty player walks straight into it. If the board
  had stopped me when I tried to mark her Courier with a carry slot still Hidden, I'd have gone back.
- **Too hard / hidden:** Marsh's third clue needs slot arithmetic that the game never hints at.
- **Boring:** the umbrella question and the "Say something kind" option felt like dead air.

## Wording that confused me

- "Has three or more marked clues": took a moment to link "marked" to the ✦ in the table.
- "Badged into the Archive" vs the profile's "Was in the Archive": I assumed these are the same, but
  it wasn't stated.
- "Revisiting the coats after any accusation" is listed as a hidden-result action. That suggests coats
  change after accusations. I'd skipped them anyway, so it never came up, but it makes me suspect I
  lost the chance to clear Ishikawa once I'd accused someone.

## What I learnt about spotting an insider or agent

- Being in the right place and wanting the right thing isn't enough. You have to rule out the
  thing that would clear them (the ward charm) before calling it.
- Hard evidence (footage of the key) beats gossip (someone asked where the Codex is).
- Doing a favour for a suspect you haven't verified is exactly what HaX warned against. I did it
  anyway because it looked like a quick win. That's the social-engineering lesson, and I fell for it.

## What I think the stakes were

Name an innocent and the lodge closes ranks. That probably locks off sources or witnesses (possibly
the coats) and costs score. Miss a cultist and they turn up at the ritual, which I'd guess means a
harder ending or a fight. Once in the sanctum you can't come back out, so anyone left unnamed goes free.

## Suggestions from this run

1. Give the burner SIM a real source (e.g. the phone, once handed over or examined, or a dropped item)
   or have the board show slot totals so counting is visible.
2. Before marking or accusing someone as Courier while one of their carry slots is still Hidden, have
   the board warn: "You haven't seen everything they carry."
3. Make the note and Venn's topic give different extras, so taking the shortcut still costs something
   or Venn's coat still matters.
4. Give "Say something kind" a visible reason to use it, or hasty players will ignore it.
