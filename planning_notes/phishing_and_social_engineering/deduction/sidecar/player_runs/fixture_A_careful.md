## Decisions

### Marsh's favour
I did not give Marsh her phone. She's a cultist on my count, and HaX said no favours for anyone
unchecked. I'd guess the phone holds the burner SIM, which might show up as a clue if I handed it over
or looked at it. **Wording note:** I couldn't inspect the phone myself. A careful player wants to
"look at the phone" before handing it back, and the packet offers no such action. I deduced the SIM
by elimination, which felt clever. A less careful player would never find it and might stop at
"2 ✦, not enough".

### Accusations
1. Accuse Okafor (Keyholder). "There is no taking this back." Confirmed. Judge reply assumed: he
   breaks, or is detained.
2. Accuse Marsh (Devotee). Confirmed. I was more nervous here because her third ✦ was never shown to
   me, only counted. I checked the grid twice more first.
3. Venn, Ishikawa, Delacroix: marked "Not a cultist" with reasons.

I didn't use combat. The packet warns that knocking someone out counts as accusing them, so it only
adds risk.

HaX's message comes "after you have dealt with every guest". I didn't know what dealing with an
innocent guest means. Marking them "Not a cultist" is explicitly "your notes only", and I didn't want
to accuse them. I assumed the three cleared marks count, but I'd have been stuck if the message never
appeared. The game needs to say what clears a guest for this message.

## Feedback

**Confused or stuck**
- Whether a charm sewn into a coat left in the cloakroom counts as "carries". It decides Ishikawa.
  Suggest the coat text says outright that it's "her ward charm" and the rule reads "has no ward
  charm".
- What "dealt with" means for innocent guests before HaX will open the sanctum.
- Witness pacing: I couldn't tell how many questions I'd get before answers turned vague, or whether
  the kind word fully covers the third question. The placeholder note says the influence number may
  not be shown. Please show it, or a mood face. Careful players want to plan.
- Whether the burner SIM can ever be seen directly. I never found it.

**Unfair / too easy / too hard / boring**
- Marsh's third ✦ comes only from slot counting. That's fair, and the board gives the counts, but
  it's the one step that will lose most players. Some players will see "2 ✦" and clear her, which is
  the worst outcome (a missed cultist). Consider a soft route to the SIM: inspecting the phone, a
  witness line, or a HaX hint about counting.
- The note and Venn both give 2291, so Venn's topic does nothing if you read the note first.
- The Courier profile goes unused. That's fine as a decoy, but the Ishikawa decoy depends on the
  charm wording, so make that wording solid.
- Nothing felt boring. The case is small, which suits a tutorial.

**Fun or satisfying**
- Filling the carry column and seeing that three clues fit exactly three slots.
- Ishikawa (Archive + Codex) looked guilty for a moment, and the coat cleared her.
- The monitor finally settling key vs SIM.
- Refusing Marsh's favour and then finding she was a cultist: HaX's warning paid off.

**Wording**
- "Carries" vs a coat in the cloakroom.
- "Dealt with every guest".
- "Anyone still standing here stays standing" at the altar. I read it as "unaccused guests stay
  free", but "standing" also sounds like knocked-out vs not. Ambiguous on purpose?
- The profile card uses "✦ marked:" in the label, and the board also uses "Marks" for the player's
  notes ("Undecided/Cultist/Not a cultist"). Two different meanings of "mark". Easy to mix up.

**What I learned about spotting an insider/agent**
- Look for a combination: access (Archive), motive (Codex) and no protection (ward charm). One
  suspicious trait isn't enough, and the decoy had two.
- Count what's still unaccounted for. The hidden item has to be on somebody.
- A suspect who asks you for a favour before you've checked them is a red flag. Marsh's harmless
  phone request was the social-engineering hook.
- Their own word clears nobody. Physical evidence and independent witnesses do.

**What I think the stakes were**
- Name an innocent and the lodge closes ranks: the remaining guests stop talking, and I lose score
  and options.
- Miss a cultist and they're at the ritual, presumably a fight or a failed ending in the sanctum.
- Handing Marsh her phone probably gives a cultist her burner SIM back, which would let her tip off
  the lodge or call for help. That's my guess at the hidden consequence of the favour.
