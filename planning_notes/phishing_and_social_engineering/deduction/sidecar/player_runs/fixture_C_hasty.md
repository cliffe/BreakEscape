## Confused or stuck

- The **"or"** in the profile. Skimming gives "burner OR quarry". The real condition is "quarry AND no
  verified alibi". The second half sits at the end of a long line. A hasty player will miss it.
- **Keycard vs asking someone to open the door.** It wasn't clear whether my own keycard opens the bedroom and
  comms doors, or whether I need Ahmed or Baptiste. Sending Ahmed away early might lock me out of something.
- **Knocking out Novak cuts off the garage.** The badge clone is the only way to the alibi evidence. Nothing
  warned me that punching her first would cost me evidence. This could be a good lesson (gather before you
  act) if the debrief says so. Otherwise it reads as an unfair dead end.
- Not knowing if **more than one sleeper** is possible. "There is one" burner made me think "one sleeper".
  The quarry clause suggests others could be. I couldn't tell from the board.
- **"Trust no one"** and **"The stakes are not known"** didn't help me decide how careful to be.

## Wording that confused me

- "Carries a cult-issued burner (there is one), or went to the quarry site and has no verified alibi..."
  Splitting this into two bullets ("Either: ..." / "Or: both ...") would stop the misread.
- "Ask them to wait in the comms room. You can't call this back." Then "nothing is final until the comms room
  is locked". These two lines seem to contradict each other. Which one is true?
- Exit phone: "anyone else you haven't dealt with stays in the field." I didn't know if leaving someone
  in the field counts as "Not a sleeper" or as no verdict.
- Clue-count lines with "[label placeholder]" were skipped. If they're meant to help (only 1 "no verified
  alibi" exists), they need to stand out more.

## What I learned about spotting an insider/agent

- Physical evidence (the burner) is strong. Gossip about where someone went is not enough on its own.
- Being somewhere suspicious isn't guilt. You need the "and no alibi" half before you act.
- Collect evidence before you take irreversible action, because acting can close off evidence (the badge).
- Next time I'd read the clue counts. "No verified alibi: 1" would have told me only one quarry-goer could
  fit.

Unclear. A secret leaked from the network. Leaving a sleeper in the field presumably lets the leak continue,
and locking up an innocent contact presumably costs trust or an asset. The packet says only "Trust no one" and
"The stakes are not known", so I guessed and played fast.
