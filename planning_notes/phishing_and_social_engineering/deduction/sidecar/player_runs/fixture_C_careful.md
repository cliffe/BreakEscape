## What I learned about spotting an insider

- One hard indicator (a cult-issued device) beats a pile of soft ones.
- Being somewhere suspicious isn't enough; you check the alibi before accusing. Ahmed was at the quarry
  and innocent.
- Gossip can be true, and it still needs corroboration (travel log).
- Badge logs record when a credential was used, which can break someone's cover story.
- Don't confine people just because they were near the problem.
