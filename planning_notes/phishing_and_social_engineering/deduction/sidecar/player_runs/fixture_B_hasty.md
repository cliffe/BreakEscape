## What I learned about spotting an insider
- One or two warning signs isn't the profile. You need all three, and a single clean row (no personal device)
  can rule someone out.
- Witness stories about "being there" are weaker than logs; check the times.
- Being helpful (handing over a code) is not a sign of guilt.
- Grievance alone flags people, it doesn't convict them.
