## Verdict

The round 1 fixes are in the plan and mostly match the code. Debrief first, globals synced before
tasks, solved-task skip on a loss, lazy imports for the copied files, no end card, the re-entrancy guard
and `lose_ally` hidden-on-register all hold against the engine.

Two round 1 fixes don't work as written. The pending hostile turn needs the foe's room, and the client
doesn't have it (R2E-1). The KO reconcile runs at a moment when the KO it is meant to catch hasn't been
restored yet (R2E-4). The round 1 edits also brought in one new ordering problem: the ending global now
runs before the tasks, so the credits' conclude races them (R2E-3). The other findings are gaps a builder
would otherwise fill by guessing: the timing of restore events, awaiting tasks, a failed `syncConfirmed`,
the shape of retry, the success-screen text, which packet owns the pending list, installer idempotency
and `doFavour`'s imports.

All of these are small, local changes to §5.2, §5.5, §5.7 and DL14/DL22. None needs a new spike. With
R2E-1 fixed the plan is ready for wave 1. R2E-1 and R2E-3 to R2E-7 should go into §5 before P10-E and
P10-U start.
