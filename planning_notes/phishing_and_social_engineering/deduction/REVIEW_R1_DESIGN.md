## How the fixtures work out by hand

- **B (Night shift).** Counts and truth check out (Okoro guilty; Lindqvist the red herring at 2 of 3). N8
  passes as the plan says. Count elimination does happen in play if Tomás is visited before the HR cabinet.
  Solvable and unique. Fair, but thin: each source reveals a whole column (R1D-26).
- **A (Open evening).** Counts and truth check out (Okafor Keyholder, Marsh Devotee; Courier matches nobody,
  so Ishikawa's ward charm is the negative-evidence lesson; Venn is cleared by count once Okafor's key is
  seen). Solvable without a KO via the key case. But the plan's N8 hand check leaves out Marsh's favour,
  which the validator places in `a_hall`, and that convicts Marsh from the opening room (R1D-2). "Accuse
  everyone" beats deduction (R1D-5).
- **C (The sleeper).** Truth checks out (Novak guilty by both branches). The N8 hand check is wrong twice:
  both `arkham_trip` cells are lounge facts, so `remaining(arkham_trip) = 0` clears Baptiste by count at the
  start, and Baptiste's ink is a lounge route for Novak's `no_alibi`, which convicts her (R1D-1).
- **D (Night train).** Truth and counts check out (Sato Courier, de Vries Devotee, Volkov Keyholder; Chen and
  Mensah clear; counts identical to A). The route table only lists the three tampered facts, so solvability
  and N8 cannot be checked from the plan (R1D-13).

## Teaching delivered?

- Profiles as indicators: yes (brief, board, debrief truth line).
- Negative evidence: set up well (Ishikawa in A, Lindqvist in B), but the debrief does not show it back
  (R1D-7, R1D-8).
- Threshold: undercut in A by the opening-room favour (R1D-2).
- Don't stop at the first match: delivered by A's missed-guilty stakes, but "accuse everyone" dominates (R1D-5).
- Proportionality: costs exist, but they are too weak in A (R1D-5) and only a label in B (R1D-6).

## Verdict

Not ready for build as written. The data model, rule language and verdict/stakes split are sound and
flexible enough for the four use cases. Two fixtures fail their own early-collapse lint by hand (C outright,
A through the favour), the evaluator's incompleteness points the unsafe way for N8, and the debrief does not
yet deliver the reasoning lessons the design promises. Fix R1D-1 to R1D-15 before round 2.

Counts: 1 blocker, 14 major, 13 minor.
