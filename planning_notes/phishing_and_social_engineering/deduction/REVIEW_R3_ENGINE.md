## Verdict

Every round 1 and round 2 engine finding is closed in the plan, and the fixes match the code. R2E-13 is deferred with a reason, but the approval-log entry it promises doesn't exist yet. R3E-2's full fix (replaying the matching credits on reload) is an engine change for the user's approval log. The five minors are the Finish failure path, overlay order, retry on reload, the new pending-reply flush, and pointer drift.
