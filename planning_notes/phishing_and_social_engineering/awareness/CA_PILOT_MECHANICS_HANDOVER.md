- Why side gates are declined today: `door-gates.js:18-20` and `register()` `:241-247`. A side doorway is two 1x1 sprites a tile apart, one per room (west sprite at room.x + 16, east at room.x + width - 16; `doors.js:241-300`, `seating.js:1834`), and gates group sprites by rounded position (`_key`, `door-gates.js:225`), so the far sprite never joins.

  - a doorway key for side doors on the shared wall boundary (east sprite x + 16, west sprite x - 16, same y), used by `register`, `groupAtPosition` (`:334`, called at `:598` and `doors.js:668`), and g.x/g.y set to that centre;

  - open/close: side doors carve with `pathfindingManager.markSideDoorCorridor` (`npc-pathfinding.js:997`) where N/S uses `markDoorWalkable` (`door-gates.js:451-452`); `unmarkDoor` already handles side corridors (`npc-pathfinding.js:985-995`);

  - `walkThroughTarget` already has a side branch (`:608-613`);
  - drop the decline; update the validator's side-gate warning (`scripts/validate_scenario.rb:8247-8251`) and its gate filter (`:2523`), and whatever `scripts/generate_dungeon_graph.rb` mirrors;

So: no `creditOnPass`.
