## How popups and values were read

Page-side hooks installed by `eval` after bootstrap (and reinstalled after the reload):

- A `MutationObserver` on `document.body` records every node with class `influence-popup`
  added (text, class, `currentConversationNPCId`, ms since hook).
- `console.error`/`console.warn` wrappers plus `error` and `unhandledrejection` listeners.
- Snapshot: `window.gameState.globalVariables` (all), and per-NPC ink variables from
  `npcConversationStateManager.conversationStates[npc].variables` (saved at conversation close).

The popup is the engine's own DOM element: `<div class="influence-popup influence-increased|decreased">`
with text `+ Influence: <displayName>` / `- Influence: <displayName>`, shown about 2.3 s.
m05 uses the unspaced `#influence_increased` / `#influence_decreased` tag form throughout.

### Increments that applied with no popup (harness turn limit, not proven engine)

`converse` closes the chat when it reaches `maxTurns`. A choice made on the last turn applies its ink
`+=` before the line carrying the tag has been shown, and the tag then never fires. Three cases, all
with `maxTurns` reached on a choice:

- Lisa, "How's the office mood these days?": `lisa_influence` 15 to 20 (+5) with no popup at that moment. The rest of the topic ran on re-open: +5 more (to 25), one popup (#4).
- Dr Halloran, "I'm not here to judge. Whoever did this might be a victim too.": +10 (to 5 after the -5), no popup. On re-open the story was back at the hub; the popup never appeared.
- Owen: all three increments produced popups.

So final ink totals can exceed the popup count. For a later comparison, use the same `maxTurns` and
choice list so the same lines are dropped, or note that this baseline has this gap. Whether the real
(human) chat would drop the tag on close in the same way was not tested.
