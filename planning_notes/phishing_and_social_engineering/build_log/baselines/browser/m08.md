## Method notes that affect comparison

- Reads of game state used `__test` plus `eval` on `window.gameState.globalVariables`, `window.npcConversationStateManager.exportNpcInkVariables()` and `window.npcManager.timedMessages` (read only; nothing was written through the console).
- Popups were captured with a `MutationObserver` on `document.body` for `.influence-popup` nodes, installed by `eval` (`hook.js` in the scratch folder). It was installed before the first conversation and installed again after the reload (a reload drops it). The same hook wrapped `console.error`, `console.warn` and recorded `error` and `unhandledrejection`.
- The harness also returns browser `pageerror` and console `error` messages inside `drain`. My first drains (up to the reload) used a filter that discarded that field, so **the harness page-error list for the pre-reload part of the run was lost**. The in-page hook caught 0 `console.error` calls or uncaught errors in that part. Every `drain` from the reload on printed the field (see "Console errors").
- Ink values read through `exportNpcInkVariables()` lag the live story during a conversation and settle when it closes (see "Observations").
- An accusation was done on purpose (Phantom), to record the `influence_decreased` form, and reversed with the apology option. It has side effects on later state, listed below, that a later run must repeat to compare line by line.

2. 5. After the reload the player is put back at the lobby spawn and the title screen and Mission Brief show again, as in earlier m08 reports (`PASS5_BLIND_A.md`).
6. `lock` on the badge printer (a `pc` container with a PIN) returned `ok:false` with `steps: ["tap 0311 on the keypad","tap 0311 on the keypad"]`, but the PIN minigame had succeeded (`PinMinigame success, attempts 1`) and the container opened.
