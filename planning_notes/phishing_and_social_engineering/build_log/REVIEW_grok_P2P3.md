### Note on the bridge-3 / bridge-1a reload step

bridge-3 (T12 onward) and bridge-1a both fail only at the step that reloads the page *while an overlay is open*: the RELOAD eval is destroyed by the navigation and `window.__test` is then undefined. This reproduces on `bridge-1a`, which this round did not touch, and does not occur in the reviewer's own runs (32/32, 38/38). It is a browser-timing issue under the chromium 1248 headless shell in this container (reloads with no overlay open — bridge-1b D1/D2, bridge-4 — work, 82/82 and 30/30). The post-reload logic my edits touch (own_teague card, the pay_today three-flag score, percent 100 / Sharp) is exercised reload-free by bridge-3b, which passes 8/8. bridge-1a's other failure (B1 recon board) is a separate concurrent-tree change (a `lord`/Sofia Lord person now on the board), not from this round.
