## Checks the orchestrator asked for, confirmed

- **Normal end records outcome + debrief; abort paths do not.** Confirmed by code (single chokepoints; `{aborted:true}`/superseded pass straight through) and by bridge-4 (walked_away on person close, reported on phone END both write the outcome global and complete; P4 prep Close still ends `{aborted:true}` with outcome `""`, bridge row P4).
- **T4-2 judge lookup.** Confirmed by the diff and the dedicated unit test (name key and fact-id key both resolve; label guarded on value match).
- **Call UI vs 06 (P4-E).** Phone tells are render-only (`data-render-only="true"`), the bar and tells sit in both chat headers, pip is drawn only for a sprite NPC and not while hostile or at the start value, the portrait tint touches only the character draw, and the board moves a granted `grants.intel` fact to "From the call". All covered by `social-influence-pip` (8) and `social-intel-board` (10) node tests; bridge-4 additionally shows the live bar and the "Borrowed authority" tell in the phone header. P4-E had no browser run of its own; bridge-4 now exercises the header bar+tells, but the portrait tint and the world pip remain unseen on screen (unit-tested only).
- **m01–m08 impact.** All new chat behaviour is gated on `this._pretext`/`params.pretextCallId`; `startPretextCall` only fires for an NPC/phone carrying a `callId`; `_seedFromSession` finds nothing without `pretext_*_state`. No shipped mission has these, so m01–m08 play is unchanged. (R3 in `P4-T.md` reached the same conclusion; I did not re-run those browser comparisons.)
- **bridge-4 tests what it claims.** Yes; 30 boolean assertions over prep/call/reload/lockout. The two fix-touched rows (Q8, L5) now also dismiss the debrief and, for Q8, assert the `walked_away` outcome. Re-run passes.
- **Slice call data quality/fairness.** Reasonable. Grants give composer-usable facts (Brandt) or board-only recon (Quinn `provides:[]`). `strictIfGlobal` makes the Teague phish harder after reporting Brandt — a fair consequence chain. The 3rd Brandt hint ("Ravi Shah is the colleague the desk already knows") is near-explicit, but that is the recipe step of a layered ladder and this is a test slice.
