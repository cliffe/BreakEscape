## The one DIFFERENT line: Resume overlay after reload

After the reload I clicked the title prompt, closed the Mission Brief with "×", and the next `brief` showed `blockingUi: "OTHER SESSIONS"` with Load session #1 to #6, Prev, Next. The `#session-resume-overlay` was `display:flex` with Resume, Restart and New Session buttons. `enter`, `interact` and `interactInventory` all returned `blocking-ui` until I clicked Resume with an `eval` (`#session-resume-btn`). After Resume the game carried on with inventory and state intact (no rewind).

Probably not a regression:
- The overlay is driven by `show.html.erb` (DOMContentLoaded block, about line 318 to 331) and `Game#has_progress?` (`app/models/break_escape/game.rb:38`). Commit 91c1ee03 changed neither file.
- The baseline game also had progress at reload (8 NPCs met, objectives persisted per its verify-run), so `hasProgress` was true there too.
- The baseline's sequence at that point included `clickAt 400,300` while "Continue?" showed. If the overlay was up, that click would land on its centre and could have hit Resume (or the title prompt, or both), so the baseline most likely clicked it away without noticing. I could not prove this.

If the orchestrator wants certainty, re-run the baseline commit's reload step and watch for the overlay. Nothing in this run points at a Phase 0 file.
