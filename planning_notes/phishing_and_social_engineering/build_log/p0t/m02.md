## Differences

- **A (line 4, includes the extra talk):** Doyle's first `converse` ended after one choice at 28 turns (`exhaustedBranches: true`) and the other four choices were taken by the second `converse`. End state (ink 6, 3 popups, four flags true, not hostile) is the same as the baseline. Likely cause: the harness's "nothing new said" stop firing while the dialogue was waiting for the choice list, on a keyless server where each line waits on a failed TTS fetch. Not a game-state difference. Nothing in the Phase 0 diff is an obvious suspect; if the orchestrator wants it settled, rerun only the Doyle talk twice on a fresh game and compare turn counts.
- **A2 (extra Doyle talk):** because of A, I made a third pre-reload Doyle `converse` to reach the baseline's "exhausted hub" state. It played the farewell line before the reload, where the baseline played it after. Post-reload Doyle then played "Still here, then." and the same farewell, 6 -> 6. Sequence difference only.
- **B (line 26):** the superseded-minigame warning did not fire. It is a benign guard message in `PersonChatMinigame` that depends on timing between a conversation closing and the next one starting (it fired in the baseline on the Bernie talk that hit the 60-turn limit; mine hit the same limit with no warning). Absence of a warning is not a regression.
