# core_13 concepts: Removable media, cloud storage and shadow IT

This file is the source of truth for the module. The game, the explainers, the knowledge check and the reference card must all match it. Sharing settings and classification are core_06; the law on personal data is core_05; downloads and fake sites are core_08; AI tools are core_15; device security and personal devices are core_09; people taking data on purpose is core_14.

## Key messages

1. **Found a USB stick? Hand it to IT. Don't plug it in anywhere**, not even to find the owner, and not into a home computer.
2. **Work stays in work places.** Not your personal email, personal cloud storage, a personal messaging group or a free online service. If it's there, the organisation can't protect it, back it up, or take it back.
3. **Free isn't free.** An unapproved app or online tool comes with no contract, no control, no backup and no off switch when someone leaves.
4. **If the official way is too slow or doesn't work, ask IT.** There's usually an approved way, often ready in minutes. Tell them about the friction too: they can't fix what they don't know about.
5. **Already using a workaround? Tell IT.** No blame. They'll help you move it somewhere safe.
