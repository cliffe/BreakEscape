Each row is recorded through BE-01 and shown in the debrief and the learner's record. "How soon" is always measured in story events (the stage an incident had reached when it was reported), never in minutes, so BE-04's time scale and "timers off" don't change anyone's rating.

The **before and after** for each learner is C04-B1 against C04-B5: a colleague's incident each time, unannounced, measured by whether and how soon it got reported, and, in Apply, how kindly.

LO6 (why speed matters and what happens next) is evidenced by the stage at which each incident was reported (C04-B2, B3 and B5) and by check items C04-Q8 and C04-Q10.

## Critical behaviours

To complete the module the learner must show:

- **C04-CB1** Reports the incidents they meet: at least three of the four Apply incidents (C04-B2 to C04-B5) reported before the Apply beat ends.
- **C04-CB2** Gets a colleague's mistake reported: C04-B5 not at Not yet.
- **C04-CB3** Keeps the evidence and says what they did: C04-B8 not at Not yet.

A learner who misses a critical behaviour is taken back to it, following the remediation loop in `features/assessment_and_evidence.md` (section 4):

1. Ama says what happened without blame (*"That one slips past lots of people. Let's try it once more."*) and replays the matching Learn chunk and Practise item (one to two minutes).
2. A fresh incident of the same kind arrives in a short replay of the afternoon (one to three minutes), drawn from the bank (BE-13): a different colleague's mistake for CB2, a different sign in the shared systems for CB3.
3. **Shown:** recorded as "shown after practice", and the outcome it supports is capped at Developing for this sitting.
4. **Not shown:** a second loop, with a different incident and a more direct prompt from Ama.
5. **After two loops** the learner goes on to the check anyway. The record says "practised, not yet shown", a private practice drill is scheduled for two to four weeks later (HA-03, HA-21), and the learner is offered help from the organisation's training contact. Nobody else is told by name.

Every attempt stays in the record.
