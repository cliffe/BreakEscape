## New findings

**N1 (MI7 residue). The defend-only `defendIntro` still frames "leave the device as it is" as the one universal move.** Minor, episode-local (generator).
`defendIntro` (`scenarios/awareness_e8_after_the_click_defend/scenario.json.erb:306`; source `gen_e8_e17_shells.py:263`; spec §8b/§13): "... the one move that works for every real sign is to report it and **leave the device as it is**. Take them at your own pace." This is the exact conflation MI7 named: for the account-takeover signs in this very set (row 1 sent item, row 2 reset, row 3 MFA push) the first move is report **and change your password on the real site**, not "leave the device as it is", which fits only the ransom-note/device sign (row 5). The C1 fix widened the completion lines to "let the team act" and the Monday card already pairs both ("report ... change your password on the real site, and leave the device as it is", `:294`), so the `defendIntro` is now out of step with both. It shows only to defend-only learners (the full shell has no `defendIntro`), which is why it slipped the C1 pass. Fix: widen it the same way, e.g. "... the one move that works for every real sign is to report it and let the team act" (and keep "leave the device as it is" for the device signs, where the per-item feedback already says it). Change the generator source and regenerate, not the shell by hand. Low impact; not a ship blocker.

## Notes (not findings)

- **`skillNames` is correct and load-bearing**, not dead plumbing. The set-level `skillNames` block (full `:121-125`, defend `:82-86`) is read by `triage-flags.js:213-216` for the result breakdown, and `triage.rb:284` *warns* if a local tell lacks an entry. All three local tells (`unexpected_sent_item`, `unrequested_reset`, `ransomware_note`) have entries, so the breakdown shows skill names, not raw ids. Good.
- **The by-design scoring note from C1 still stands**: under `scoringProfile: "awareness"` a genuine item scores full whether trusted or reported, so refusing everything still scores 100. The "awkward genuine so refusing everything doesn't win" criterion is met pedagogically (the row 5 ransom-note / row 6 maintenance mirror, both "about IT and your systems", and the per-item feedback), not through the number. Shared with the whole built set; not an E8 defect.
- **Timing unverified.** The bridge does not time a run. Six items keeps the full episode tighter than the C1 seven, so 15 minutes is plausible, but a timed full-episode playtest is still the honest way to confirm the 15-minute / 8-minute budgets.

## Verdict

**Ship-worthy as the pilot.** All three C1 majors and both C1 engine findings are cleared (MA3 in code, with the one caveat that no rendered screenshot was captured because the suite has no screenshot command — the ENG-01 owner may want one look to be certain on contrast). The lesson is single and repeatable, the no-blame register is clean, the facts match core_04 and core_01 LO6, both shells validate under `--strict-social --no-graph --skip-ink`, parity and dialoguelint pass, and the full bridge is 13/13 with the flip aftermath rendering the right DOM. The one open item, N1, is a minor wording residue in the defend-only `defendIntro` that should be widened to match the completion lines and the Monday card, via the generator; it does not block ship. Recommend fixing N1 and running one timed full-episode playtest, after which E8 is done.
