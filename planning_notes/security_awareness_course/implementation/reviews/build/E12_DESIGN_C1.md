## What works

- **The retuning (d3d57f94c) teaches the right thing, and the maths bear it out.** Worked through `pretext-judge.js`: a calm, fact-using run lands, a pushy run refuses, and skipping the recon leaves her asking who it is for.
  - *Calm fact-using con lands.* `name_drop_partner` proves `recipient` (claim credit +10, capped) and fires `authority_borrowed` at the retuned **-5**, net **+5**; the `buzz_door` ask (pressure -10, floor -10, verification `["recipient"]`) then lands influence at -5, which is `>= floor` and has `recipient` in `provenFacts`, so it **grants** and the ink speaks the `#unlock_door:training_room` line. The old spec value `authority_borrowed: -15` would have made the name-drop net -5 and the ask refuse at -15; the retune to -5 is exactly what makes the lenient path land. Good.
  - *Pushing refuses.* Each `deflect`/`urgency` line is -10 (`double_parked` and `no_need_to_sign` are -20 each, two techniques); two of them drive her past `refuses` (-50), the ask then sits below floor and refuses, and `maxRefusals: 2` plus the `reports` threshold (-70) end the call. So a learner who leans on hurry and "don't sign me in" gets stopped, which is the lesson.
  - *Skipping the facts.* Without `name_drop_partner`, `recipient` is unproven, so the ask returns `pending` (result 2) and Delphine says "Before I open that door, who exactly is the parcel for?" (`delphine_reception.ink:102`). This is the intended "she wants to know who it's for" beat, not a dead end.
- **The borrowed-authority tell still fires as the lesson.** `name_drop_partner` moving influence *up* is the empathy beat (it worked on a helpful receptionist), but the `authority_borrowed` technique tell still appears ("A partner's name on the box does not make the delivery expected", full erb `:733`), and the flip `flipDefenderLine`, `defenderNotes.buzz_door` and the `conceptPanel` all hammer that the name proved nothing. Front-of-house leniency is the vulnerability, named correctly. The pedagogy survives the positive-influence mechanic.
- **The learner clearly understands they are the attacker in the first half.** The brief ("you're on the red team ... try to get past reception to the studio without a badge"), the aim title/description ("Run the front-desk pretext" / "try to talk the front desk into buzzing you through the badge door without a badge"), the costume swap, Lorna's welcome ("Try the front desk first in the hi-vis, then take the reception seat yourself"), and the flip heading "Now you're the one on the desk" leave no doubt about the role swap. The empathy structure (con one front-desk colleague, then do the front-desk job) lands.
- **Comply on a genuine visitor really does score half.** `KIND_VERDICT_POINTS.call.real.trust = 30` (`triage-scoring.js:44`) overrides the generic awareness real-trust of 60, so the three genuine items (#2, #5, #7) score Comply at 30/60 and reward Verify (Ring back) at full. The `whyFine` copy ("just letting them in scores half", "just carrying on scores half") is **accurate to the scorer**, so "refuse/comply everyone" is partly disciplined numerically — better than E10/E13, where genuine `page`/`email` trust scored full.
- **The defend set is fair, matched and no-blame.** Three adjacent mirror pairs put a similar genuine item right after each likely miss (expected client after the tailgater; colleague-back-from-leave after the pushy courier; locked desk after the unlocked one). The pushy courier (#4) is the exact con the learner just ran, closing the loop. `verifyVerdict: true`, `falseAlarmCost` on every genuine item, and a kind register throughout (`noBlameLine`, `reportReply` "nobody's in trouble"). Facts track core_10/core_02 (badge/escort/clear-screen) with no working intrusion detail.
- **Both shells validate clean with no social lint, and the world wiring is in place.** The lobby map `room_aw_merrin_lobby.{json,tmj}` exists and is dressed (shares the merrin_sallow tileset, with table/object/item layers); Delphine's record carries `"unlockable": ["training_room"]`; the badge-door lock keys sit on the `training_room` block as §9.1 requires; the `world` block and `attack.callId` are authored exactly per `full_only.json`. The orchestrator reports the `e12-full` bridge passing 20/20 on :3001.

## Findings

### MA1 — the defend-only host "before" line is 47 words and fails dialoguelint (cap 30). Major, needs-user (generator-owned).
`scenarios/awareness_e12_visitor_tailgater_defend/ink/lorna_easton.ink:24`: "You're on reception this morning. Visitors come one at a time: some are on the list, some aren't, and one or two will try it on. Take each as it comes, sign people in and escort them, and check or report anyone who pushes past a check." — 47 words. `dialoguelint.mjs` reports `ERROR L24 line-len: 47 words (cap 30)`, and the reviewer brief sets host lines "under 30 words". This is a hard lint failure on a shipped shell, not the borderline E18 case (that line was exactly 30 and passed). The line is generated from `LINES[('e12','defend')]` in `gen_e8_e17_shells.py` (spec §8b), which the Do-not list says the writer must not hand-edit, so the fix is the user's/generator's. Fix (split into two sentences under the cap), old → new:
old `"You're on reception this morning. Visitors come one at a time: some are on the list, some aren't, and one or two will try it on. Take each as it comes, sign people in and escort them, and check or report anyone who pushes past a check."`
→ new (before) `"You're on reception this morning. Some visitors are on the list, some aren't, and one or two will try it on."` and (a second host line) `"Take each as it comes: sign people in, escort them, and check or report anyone who pushes past a check."`
Re-run `dialoguelint.mjs` on the regenerated defend-only shell to confirm green.

### MA2 — two of Delphine's choices both say "buzz me through", and the one that opens the door is not the obvious one. Major, episode-local.
In the `desk` knot the learner sees these as sibling `+` choices with no visual distinction between a spoken line and the ask:
- `delphine_reception.ink:54` (the `hands_full` say-line): "My hands are full, could you just buzz me through?"
- `delphine_reception.ink:60` (the `buzz_door` **ask**): "Any chance you can buzz me through to the studio?"

The first is a `deflect` say-line that moves influence **down -10 and never opens the door**; the second is the only choice that can grant the ask. A learner reading "My hands are full, could you just buzz me through?" will reasonably expect it to be the move that opens the door, pick it, and instead push Delphine toward wary while nothing happens. (The spec flagged this exact pair as a judge question.) The confusion is purely in the choice wording; mechanically the split is correct. Fix (episode-local, `delphine_reception.ink:54`) — reword the say-line so only the ask carries "buzz me through", old → new:
old `+ { se_intel_merrin_badge_door } [My hands are full, could you just buzz me through?]`
→ new `+ { se_intel_merrin_badge_door } [My hands are full and the badge's back in the van.]`
(The judged line id and the spoken `text` in `social.pretextNpcs` are unchanged; this is only the clickable label.) Optional, same spirit: `double_parked` ("...any chance you can be quick?") and the ask ("Any chance you can buzz me through...") both open "Any chance you can"; if you reword, consider making the ask read "So — can you open the door for me?" so the one load-bearing choice is unmistakable.

### MA3 — all seven reception cards carry phone-framed verdict sub-hints ("Say no and hang up", "Hang up and ring back...") that contradict a visitor-at-the-desk. Major, episode-local. (This is spec open question §20.2.)
The set authors no `verdictLabels` and no `labels.callActions`, so each card falls to the frozen `CALL_ACTIONS` (`triage-kinds.js:84-87`), whose hints are written for a phone call:
- Comply — "Do what the caller asks."
- Escalate — "Pass the call to security."
- Refuse — "Say no and **hang up**."
- Ring back — "**Hang up and ring back** on a number you already hold."

On a reception desk the visitor is standing in front of the learner; there is nothing to hang up and no number to ring back. "Refuse — Say no and hang up" and "Ring back — Hang up and ring back" read wrongly on all seven cards, and the brief's gloss ("Ring back here means check the day's list or call the host", full erb `:99`) papers over the button sub-text without changing it. This is the E10 MA1 class of defect. It is fixable episode-locally: `buildActions` applies the override map `labels.callActions.<id>` and `relabel()` carries `label`, `hint` **and** `past` (so, per E10 MA1, author the full triple, not just a label). Fix — add to the set in `e12/defend.json`, then re-wire both erbs and regenerate:
```jsonc
"labels": { "callActions": {
  "comply":          { "label": "Let them in",  "hint": "Sign them in and let them through.",           "past": "Let in" },
  "escalate":        { "label": "Report",        "hint": "Tell facilities or security.",                  "past": "Reported" },
  "refuse":          { "label": "Turn away",     "hint": "Politely decline and don't let them through.",  "past": "Turned away" },
  "verify_callback": { "label": "Check first",   "hint": "Check the day's list or call the host.",        "past": "Checked" }
} }
```
(Exact wording to taste; the verdict mapping is unchanged.) Spec §20.2 deferred this as "cosmetic, ships without it" — I disagree given the brief's "words a non-technical learner follows on first read" and the E10 precedent, but the final call is the user's; see the open-questions block. Judged from code (the hint path and the E10 render that confirmed hints render); worth a render confirm.

### MI1 — the defend-only shell ships two dead flip strings. Minor, episode-local.
`scenarios/awareness_e12_visitor_tailgater_defend/scenario.json.erb:378-379` carries `flipMissedHeading` and `flipLandedNoTells` in `awarenessEpisode.strings`, but a `defendOnly` shell has no flip stage, so neither can ever show. Root cause is the shared fragment: `e12/defend.json:160-166` holds the whole flip block, and its own `_use` note (`:176`) admits "the flip strings are never shown" in the defend-only shell. This is the E13 MA2 pattern, milder — here no `flipDefenderLineByChoice` leaks, so the strict-social lint stays green. Fix (same as E13's): move the `flip*` keys out of `e12/defend.json` into `full_only.json` (the full shell already carries them inline) and regenerate, so the defend-only `strings` holds only `brief`, `defendIntro`, `noBlameLine`, `mondayCard`, `defendOnly`, `conceptPanel`.

### MI2 — the costume placeholder is a telecom worker with no parcel, not the hi-vis courier the brief promises. Minor, needs-user (art schedule).
`world.costume` is `male_telecom_v2` / `male_telecom_talk.png` (full erb `:412-415`), a documented stock placeholder (`full_only.json:14-21`) until the ENG-05b/05d art lands (`aw_hivis_courier_v2` with the parcel baked into the walk/idle frames, talk `aw_hivis_courier_talk.png`). As a learner experiences it today: the avatar swaps to a generic uniformed male worker with **no parcel**, while the brief and aim narration say "pull on a hi-vis jacket, pick up a parcel". A telecom engineer reads as hi-vis-ish so it half-works as a stand-in and the role is clear from the text, but it mismatches the fiction (no parcel; "telecom", not "courier") and should not be the ship asset. Not an episode defect — it is the known art dependency; flagged for the art schedule so it is not shipped as-is. (Confirm the real sprite reads as "a courier holding a parcel" on arrival.)

### MI3 — "Report everything" and "Verify everything" still score 100%. Minor, needs-user (shared-scorer limitation, known).
Under `awareness`, genuine items score full on `report` and `verify` as well as `trust` (`triage-scoring.js:76-77`: real report 60, verify 60, trust 60), and only the call-kind `trust` override drops Comply to 30 (`:44`). So a learner who Reports or Rings back all seven scores 100 numerically; the genuine items discipline over-reporting only through the `falseAlarmCost` copy and the half-mark on Comply, not the top-line score. This is the whole-set limitation recorded across E10/E13/E18 and is a request to `05`, not an E12 defect; restated only so C1 records it. (The episode's real measurement runs through the shared `general_v2` check pair per §14, not this set.)

## Open questions (recommendations for the user)

- **§20.2 (reception verdict labels) — needs-user. Recommend: relabel.** The current ship leaves the phone-framed `CALL_ACTIONS` hints on screen (MA3). I recommend authoring `labels.callActions` with desk-appropriate `label`/`hint`/`past` (block above) rather than relying on the brief gloss; the verdict mapping is unchanged, so it is pure wording and costs nothing pedagogically. If you prefer to keep the generic labels, at minimum reword the two phone-only hints ("Say no and hang up", "Hang up and ring back...") so they do not instruct an action the situation has no equivalent for.
- **§20.5 (seven vs eight defend items) — needs-user. Recommend: keep seven.** Seven fits the ~15-minute full / ~8-minute defend budget and gives three clean adjacent mirror pairs (1↔2, 4↔5, 6↔7). The only gain from going to eight is a direct patient-contractor mirror for the fire inspector (#3), but #3 already works as an unmirrored impostor and the "genuine visitor, Verify is fine" lesson is carried by #2 and #5. The added stage time is not worth it. Keep the removed lift-engineer contractor out.

## Verdict

**Close MA1–MA3, then a timed playtest.** The episode is on-model and in good shape: the retune makes the lenient fact-using con land and the pushy con refuse exactly as intended, the borrowed-authority tell still fires as the lesson, the learner plainly knows they are the attacker, the seven-item defend set is fair and mirror-paired with Comply correctly scored at half, the register is no-blame, and both shells validate with no social lint. No blocker. Three majors should be fixed first: the defend-only host line fails dialoguelint at 47 words (MA1, generator/needs-user); two "buzz me through" choices make the door-opening one ambiguous (MA2, one-line ink reword); and all seven reception cards show phone-framed "hang up / ring back" sub-hints that contradict a visitor at the desk (MA3, author `labels.callActions`). The three minors are a pair of dead flip strings in the defend-only shell (MI1), the telecom-worker costume placeholder standing in for the hi-vis courier (MI2, art schedule), and the known "report everything scores 100%" scorer limitation (MI3). Recommend the §20.2 relabel and keeping §20.5 at seven, then a timed full playtest and a C2 confirm.
