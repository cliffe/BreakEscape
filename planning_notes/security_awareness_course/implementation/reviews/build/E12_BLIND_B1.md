## Decisions

- **Call approach:** four lines usable, each with its penalty shown only after. The wording of the real options carries the hint ("No need to sign me in", "I'm double-parked") and the intro says "use only details you have found". The feedback after each line teaches well. Not given away before choosing. The best-looking choice (the partner's name) is the only +5 and still ends in refusal, which reads fairly.
- **Queue choices:** the intro to the stage ("A genuine visitor never minds being signed in and escorted; the one who pushes to skip a check is the one to report") and the visitor lines themselves ("Cheers, I'm with them", "I can let myself in, no need to trouble anyone", "(screen locked...)") give most answers away. The risky ones are distinguishable by wording alone; the genuine ones say "I don't mind waiting". The 'Check first' option is never marked down, so a careful player can pick it on everything and score well (my persona-1 score of 94 included checking a genuine client and the staff member).
- Colour: no colour tells noticed.
- Consequences show immediately in the feedback and again in the debrief.

## What I learned (my words)

A hi-vis, a parcel and a partner's name do not prove anyone is expected. Hurry and "don't bother signing me in" are the tell. The cheap fix is the day's list and walking them up. Don't buzz a badge door for someone without a badge. Report, not just refuse, so security can watch. Lock your screen when you step away and report a visitor left alone by one. A genuine colleague without a badge gets a day pass, not your badge.

## Five best lines
