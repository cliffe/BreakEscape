### 3.2 The argument, three players

**A thoughtful player with an ordinary m09 save** (`should_have_told`, no hardened, no m02 record). Exchange 1: the rebuttal comes from the workshop sheet, which is on the critical path for everyone. Exchange 2: the letter rebuttal comes from the Albion papers in m09. Exchange 3: four buttons. **A thoughtful cold-start player.** Exchange 1 is fine. Exchange 2's rebuttal says "You wrote the notice and didn't send it", but on a cold start the only Albion fact anyone gives is HaX's one line off his box, "he argued for the vendor nine years ago, and lost" (arc L321), and his case's default line, "I argued against that, once" (SE L79). A careful listener has a fair case for either decoy, and both miss (R3-02). So a listening cold-start player can come out with one point.

**A button gamer.** Length: no tell any more (see N3 above). Position: there is a tell. SE L95 fixes the rebuttal in slots 2, 1, 3. On a cold start the two record slots are empty, the fill decoy sits in slot 4, and the buttons close up into three. With R3-02 and R3-03 on top, the gamer beats the listener on a cold start, which is the wrong way round. The fixes are small.

Well signposted: a labelled button in each menu, a narrator warning that names it an execution and says HaX is on the line, then "[Do it.]" / "[Lower the weapon.]". It can't be reached by accident. The aftermath (Quintero or the van team takes the weapon, Netherton refuses to sign, HaX withholds the rest) carries the weight without any gloating. Two problems: arc L402 says "sitting" for both versions, and the "cost made concrete", the drafted story going out at 06:00, has no rule across the media room's branches (R3-07). The justified version's button text isn't given anywhere. It should be a plain "[Fire.]" so it never shares words with the cold labels (stage 7 note, not a finding).
