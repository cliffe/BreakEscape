## Cross-cutting notes for the build (both missions)

- **Two submission points raise the same question in both missions.** m09 flag 4 is submitted at the tape room beside Tomb Beta's machine, while flags 1-3 are at the reading-room laptop; m10 flags 2-3 are at the server-hall console and flag 4 at the core host terminal, while flag 1 is at the forward post. Each needs either the laptop carried to the second point or a second in-game terminal bound to the same VM. Decide in the game build (plan §5, M1 and M2).
- **Root login over SSH** must stay disabled on every box (plan build notes). In m10, the release host and core host currently enable it with a generated password, which would let a player skip the escape and read both climax flags; remove it and confirm after the build.
- **Seed content is named but not yet placed.** m09 needs Tesseract's Albion memo and his unsent vendor notice on Tomb Beta's machine; m10 needs the channel list, the cells' early-access orders and the first entries by city in the monitoring host's schedule. The build must place these as files, or the story must move them (plan §5, M3 and M4).
