# Stage 0 review, round 1: carry-over and canon accuracy

## Scope and method

Reviewer: read-only, 2026-10-20. Lens: whether the carry-over and canon claims are factually right. Reviewed `FINALE_ARC.md`, `m09_digital_archaeology/stages/stage_0_initialization.md` and `m10_final_cipher/stages/stage_0_initialization.md` against the shipped missions. VM material was not opened.

- **Every import (all 98 entries: 42 in §3.1, 56 in §3.2)** was checked by script against the rendered `globalVariables` and `exportGlobals` of the source `scenario.json.erb`. The checks were: the name exists, the default matches, and the export status matches the ✱ marking. The two counts and the 41-name export list in §3.3 were recounted.
- **Story meaning** was checked in the source ink and debriefs for about 45 values, covering every m07 and m08 import and at least two per mission for m01–m06.
- **Engine**: `app/models/break_escape/carry_over.rb`, `docs/agents/CARRY_OVER_PLAN.md` §2.1–2.12, and `README_scenario_design.md` "Carrying choices between missions" (L1442).
- **Canon**: `the_architect.md` identity section, `agent_0x99_haxolottle.md` L241–245, `voice_bible.md` L179 and L199, `insider_threat_initiative.md`, and the m07/m08 CONTRACT.md files. New names and codenames were grepped across `scenarios/`, `story_design/` and `planning_notes/overall_story_plan/`.
- **Missed threads**: the "Open threads" and "not exported" sections of both carry-over inventories, compared row by row with §2.

Severity: **blocker** means the design can't be built as written. **Major** means a claim is wrong about what a mission does or about canon, and a player would notice. **Minor** means a wording or precision fix.
