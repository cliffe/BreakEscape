# Bible notes for later: the Season 1 finale (m09, m10)

## About this list

Arc-keeper, 2026-10-21, under stage 8 ruling 21 (`STAGE_8_RULINGS.md`). This collects every "bible stale" item from the three stage 8 canon passes in this folder: `STAGE_8_BIBLE_PASS_A.md` (BA), `STAGE_8_BIBLE_PASS.md` (BP, "Bible notes for later") and `STAGE_8_CHARACTER_CANON_A.md` (CC). Each is a place where the universe bible (`story_design/universe_bible/`) is out of date with what m01–m10 now establish, or lacks a fact the finale adds. In every case the finale is deliberate and stays as it is; the bible is the thing to update.

**The bible is not edited now.** These notes wait for a later bible pass. Line numbers were checked against the bible on 2026-10-21 and will drift.

Duplicates are merged: BA 12 and BP 1 (the cracker supply chain) are one item, as are BA 4 and BP 2 (Tomb Gamma). Items the passes filed as "finale wrong" were fixed in the finale (FINALE_ARC §22) and are not here; items they filed as harmless are not here either.

Each row gives the bible location, what the finale established, the finale source (FA = `FINALE_ARC.md`; S6 = each mission's `stages/stage_6/lore_fragments.md`; ink paths under `scenarios/`), and which pass raised it.
