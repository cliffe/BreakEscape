## Time budget

The arc claims 75 minutes. Rebuilt from the beats, at the arc's own 6–8 minutes per VM stage and walking time between the drop terminal and each find:

| Part | Arc | Realistic | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Briefing | 5 | 6–7 | S-22 |

| Act 3 | 21 | 35–40 | Two VM stages at about 2 minutes each (S-01); trips office → open stacks (the Reading) → tape room → reading room (flag 3) → tape room (flag 4) |
| Debrief | 4 | 4 | Fine |
| **Total** | **75** | **90–100** | |

Where it runs long: Act 3's VM work and its traversal. The traversal is the cheap thing to fix: the Reading in the safe, and flag 3 submitted in the tape room, together save about six to eight minutes without touching a beat. The identity scene can still happen with HaX and Netherton on the phone, since the drop terminal's only job there is to be where HaX "can see it". If the scene must stay at the reading room, accept about 90 minutes and update S0 §1 and FA §8 r6. m08's 70 minutes is not a fair comparison: m09 has two more forced choices, a longer briefing and two machines.
